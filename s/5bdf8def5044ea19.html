Simple Mirror Trips Up Robot Policies, Study Finds

Researchers asked a simple question: can robots still handle objects when a mirror shows reflections of the scene? They built a test called RoboDojo-Mirror to find out. It compares a normal setup with one mirror, two mirrors, and a mirrored tabletop. Five different robot control systems all did worse with mirrors. Average success fell by 10.8 to 56.7 percentage points, and by 62.5 points on the mirrored tabletop. Mirrors are also physical obstacles, but many failures happened with no contact between robot and mirror. The robots seemed to chase reflected objects and misjudge where real ones were. Tests on real robots also showed lower success. The authors say robot evaluation should account for reflections.
