GeForce NOW Launches Black Friday Deals and Arrives on Amazon Fire TV Sticks

NVIDIA is offering 30% off the first six months of a new Performance or Ultimate GeForce NOW membership. GeForce NOW is a service that streams PC games from the cloud. The sale runs through Nov. 30, while supplies last, in select regions. The service is also coming to three Amazon Fire TV Stick models, so people can play on a TV without a console. Amazon will offer 90% off the first month of a membership bought through it, through Dec. 31 in participating regions. Five Bethesda games, including Fallout 4 and Skyrim, add support for their GOG versions. Nine games join the service this week.
