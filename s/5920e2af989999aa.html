Challenge pits graph networks against topological networks on shared tests

The 2026 Topological Deep Learning Challenge compared two kinds of AI models. Graph neural networks learn from points linked by connections. Topological neural networks also handle richer shapes, like groups of linked points. Teams built recent models in a tool called TopoBench and tested them on synthetic data made with GraphUniverse. 34 teams sent 68 qualifying entries, and results were shown on a public leaderboard for the first time. The gap between the two types depended on the task. On community detection, it was small. On triangle counting, the graph models made 1.85 times more error on average. Still, some graph models built with structural knowledge beat the topological average on triangle counting.
