Amateur astronomers use OpenAI tools to hunt for exoplanets

OpenAI shared a story about two amateur astronomers who used its tools, Codex and Astra, to look for planets outside our solar system. These planets are called exoplanets. The pair worked with public data from NASA's Kepler and TESS space telescopes. Using the tools, they found potential exoplanets, meaning candidates that are not yet confirmed. The story shows how people without professional training can use AI tools to explore open science data.
