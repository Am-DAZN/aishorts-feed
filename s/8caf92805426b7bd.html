Simon Willison names three turning points in AI coding over one year

Developer Simon Willison says AI coding tools hit three big turning points in twelve months. The first came in November 2025, when Claude Opus 4.5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.1 models made coding agents reliably useful. He says Opus 4.5 usually got the right answer, so he finally used Claude Code daily. The second was what he calls "Fable class" models in June and July. These models can take a goal and the right tools and work on their own. He gave one a set of microphones and it found a mystery beep to within about 40cm. The third turning point is 3D modeling and game development in September and October. He says top models became good at making 3D models with tools like Blender or Three.js code. He based this on his keynote at the AI Engineer Code Summit.
