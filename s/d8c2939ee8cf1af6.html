Vanguard AI Leader Explains How to Build Scalable AI in Finance

MIT Technology Review shared a session from its EmTech Future conference on building AI that can grow safely. The speaker is Jing Wang, who leads Vanguard's Center for AI and Insights. The talk says a strong base needs data, engineering, platforms, operating models, and governance (the rules and oversight) working as one. It looks at how to do this in a highly regulated setting like finance. It also covers how responsible, trustworthy use can turn new tools, including agentic systems (AI that can take actions on its own), into lasting value for investors.
