SLA teaches robot AI models to understand 3D space during pre-training

Robots need to understand meaning and also move precisely in three-dimensional space. But many robot AI models borrow their vision skills from 2D image-and-text training. Spatial skills are usually added later, for each specific task. Researchers present SLA, a model that builds spatial understanding earlier, through continual pre-training, which means further training on top of an existing model. SLA starts from an existing policy called pi05. It learns to predict actions while also matching its internal image features to 3D information from a frozen 3D perception model. The team combined many different robot datasets to cover varied robots, tasks and scenes. Extra training-only parts are dropped afterward, so the model keeps its original design at run time and can reuse existing pretrained weights.
