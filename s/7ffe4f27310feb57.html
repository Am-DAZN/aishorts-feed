NVIDIA adds science libraries so AI agents can speed up research

NVIDIA has expanded its CUDA-X software libraries, adding tools such as cuPhoton, cuPotential and cuSpa. They are meant to help researchers and AI agents work through huge datasets in fields like astrophysics, fusion energy, drug discovery and materials science. In one example, cuPhoton cut the processing of Event Horizon Telescope black hole data from over a month to eight minutes. Pacific Fusion researchers used another module to study plasma simulations while they run. cuPotential speeds up molecular simulation software by up to 3x, and several drug firms are adopting it. Daviator, coming to NVIDIA ALCHEMI, runs quantum-mechanical calculations up to 22x faster at up to 75% lower cost. The speedups let scientists study larger datasets and more complex systems.
