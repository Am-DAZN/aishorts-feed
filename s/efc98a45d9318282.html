Z.ai Releases GLM-5.4, Its First Looped Transformer Model

Chinese AI lab Z.ai has released GLM-5.4. It is the first of its models to use a looped transformer. In this design, a group of layers shares the same weights and runs several times, refining its work each pass. That makes the network deeper without adding more parameters, the numbers a model learns. Thinking Machines Lab described a similar idea in its Inkling 2 Mini model days earlier. GLM-5.4 is not available as open weights yet. Developer Simon Willison tested it through Z.ai's Coding Plan subscription, since the regular API cannot reach it yet. He also noticed that images from GLM 5.3 and 5.4 look much closer to those from OpenAI or Anthropic models than earlier GLM versions did.
