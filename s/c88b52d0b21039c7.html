Cult.fit uses OpenAI's Codex to build tools and speed up work

Cult.fit, a fitness company, is using Codex, OpenAI's AI coding tool, across its teams. The company says Codex helps people in different functions build their own tools and move faster. It also says the tool is changing how work gets done inside the company. The source gives few details, such as which tools were built or how much time was saved. Still, it shows how AI coding helpers are spreading beyond engineering teams into everyday business work.
