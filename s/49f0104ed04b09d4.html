New ROAM-Bench tests whether AI robots truly explore and reason about space

Researchers introduced ROAM-Bench, a test for AI agents that must move around indoor spaces, collect information, and then make decisions. Many older tests only check if the final answer is right. They do not check if the agent did the needed work to get there. ROAM-Bench adds separate checks using simulation records, such as whether the agent covered enough of the area or placed an object correctly. It has 250 hand-checked tasks in 27 indoor settings, including finding objects, counting, judging directions, and moving items. Humans passed 84% of 100 sampled tasks. Seven leading vision-language models showed two problems: right answers without doing the needed steps, and wrong answers even after passing stronger checks. Tests with ideal inputs suggest that seeing targets more fully and getting better feedback help the models judge space better.
