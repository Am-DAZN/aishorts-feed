OpenAI adds free API credits for top ChatGPT subscribers, matching Anthropic

OpenAI has launched a new perk for people on its $100, $200, or $500 monthly ChatGPT plans. Subscribers now get the same amount in credits to use with OpenAI's API, the tool developers use to build apps on its models. The credits can be applied to one of the subscriber's API organizations. Earlier this month, Anthropic announced almost the same deal for its Max and Team plans, and those credits expire. Developer Simon Willison, who pays for both companies' plans, called it great news. He says he may now release some public demos, since the credits are covered by subscriptions he already has.
