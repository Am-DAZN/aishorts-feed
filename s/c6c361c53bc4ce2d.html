OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Sales with a new plugin and 30+ partner plugins

OpenAI has announced ChatGPT for Sales, a set of tools aimed at people who sell for a living. It includes a new Sales plugin, which is an add-on that gives ChatGPT extra abilities. OpenAI also added more than 30 new and expanded plugins built with partners. Together, they let users research prospects, prepare pitches, update their CRM (the software that tracks customers and deals), and move deals forward. The blog post gives few other details. It matters because it shows OpenAI pushing ChatGPT beyond general chat into everyday work tasks for sales teams.
