Researchers Find a Way Around Hard Math in AI Inference Resale

AI model marketplaces list providers with different prices and abilities. That lets a middleman buy answers from many models and resell them to users at a profit. This is called computational arbitrage. This paper shows that finding the best way to split spending across models is, in general, an NP-hard problem, meaning very hard to solve at scale. But if returns from extra spending shrink as spending grows, the best split can be found efficiently. The authors also offer a way to estimate model performance from limited data, which cuts the upfront cost. They find that at the best split, every funded model gives the same benefit per dollar. Money also shifts away from providers that give little value for their price, and toward models that specialize in complementary skills.
