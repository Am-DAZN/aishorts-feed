PixVid Lets a Top Video AI Model Generate Directly in Pixels

Most leading video AI models work in a compressed form of video, called a latent space. This made training easier. But it now makes high-resolution, controllable and interactive video harder. Doubling the resolution quadruples the work, and controls apply to groups of frames, not single ones. Researchers present PixVid, which takes a pretrained model, Wan2.1-14B, and makes it produce every frame directly in pixels. It keeps the main model and swaps the decoder for a convolutional head that draws the pixels. The team says predicting clean video and using a larger noise shift were key to quality. After training on about 1.3 million clips, PixVid is competitive with the original. It can reach twice the resolution at the same per-frame cost, keeps depth-guided edits aligned in fast motion, and reacts to each action at the next frame.
