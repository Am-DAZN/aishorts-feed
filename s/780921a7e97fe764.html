New Benchmark Tests AI That Hears Speech in Five Languages

Researchers built OmniTag-Bench, a test for AI models that listen to one audio clip and report several things at once: the words spoken, the emotion, the speaker's gender, the language, and named people or places. It covers English, German, Spanish, French and Italian. The team tested ten open models and one they fine-tuned, called Qwen3-Omni. They found a common problem: many strong models turn non-English speech into English, which makes their error scores look worse. Fine-tuning helped the base model on several tasks and nearly doubled emotion accuracy on human-labeled data. It also worked on Dutch, a language it was not trained on. The team is releasing its prompts, scorer and labeling pipeline.
