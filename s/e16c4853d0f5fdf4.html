OpenFrontiers Tests Whether AI Can Rediscover Solutions to Open Research Problems

Researchers released OpenFrontiers, a collection of 12,151 open research problems across many fields, with details like context and progress. It also includes a benchmark of 200 math problems: 110 solved in 2026 and 90 still open. Each one is written in Lean 4, a language that lets computers check proofs. Seven AI agents were tested on a high-quality 50-problem subset. Without internet access, they rarely rediscovered solutions, at most 7 of 40 solved problems. With live web access, success mostly came from finding published answers, not new reasoning. The team says this gives a way to measure how close AI is to solving real open problems. The data is public on Hugging Face.
