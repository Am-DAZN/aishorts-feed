Google expands Gemini Intelligence to more devices

Google says Gemini Intelligence is rolling out to more devices. Gemini is Google's AI assistant, and this update brings its features to a wider range of products. The company also says the new capabilities are meant to help people do more on those devices. The short announcement does not list which devices are included or exactly what the new features are. It also gives no dates for the rollout. For now, the main point is that more people should be able to use Gemini Intelligence soon.
