Sage-Taiyi AI Co-Scientist Uses Self-Monitoring to Beat Rivals at Research Ideas

Researchers have built Sage-Taiyi, an AI system that tries to think about its own thinking, a skill called metacognition. It keeps track of what evidence it has, what it assumes and what questions remain open. It then uses that to pick what to study next and which reasoning methods to reuse. On Taiyi-Bench, a test of 265 research tasks from 50 papers across ten fields, it ranked first on all eight metrics. Its overall score was 54.0, against 47.9 for the best rival, AI Scientist-v2. In five expert-guided projects, its ideas were tested in chemistry, materials science and engineering. In chemistry, one reaction's yield rose from trace amounts to 84%, and another from 13% to 69%. The results suggest self-monitoring can help AI come up with better research ideas that hold up in the lab.
