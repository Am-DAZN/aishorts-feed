NVIDIA pitches its gaming, creator and robot-building tech as holiday gifts

NVIDIA published a holiday gift guide for its products. For gamers, it points to GeForce RTX 50 Series laptops and desktops, new RTX Spark PCs, and DLSS 5, which aims to improve speed and image quality. GeForce NOW lets people stream games from the cloud, with more than 4,500 titles. For creators, NVIDIA says its Studio tools and drivers help with video editing, 3D design and livestreaming. For hobbyists, the Jetson Developer Kit is a small computer for building robots that can see and respond to their surroundings, such as spotting a candy cane with a camera. The guide is mainly a marketing piece, but it shows how NVIDIA wants its AI chips used at home.
