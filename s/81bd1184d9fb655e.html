AI Explained: OpenAI's new model, Anthropic's IPO and a race to the top

A YouTube video from AI Explained looks at a busy stretch in AI. OpenAI released GPT 6.2 Astra, which it calls its best model. Google's Gemini is catching up fast. Anthropic is planning to go public through an IPO, which means selling shares to investors. The video says the bigger story is elsewhere. It looks at new data on what drives model improvement and how close AI is to automating AI research. It also asks whether labs are really in a 'race to the top', as Anthropic's Dario Amodei promised. Topics include how hard models are getting to monitor, and whether OpenAI is now more open than Anthropic about safety.
