Researchers Add Topic Clues to Mental Health Post Classifier

Many mental health problems go unnoticed until they become serious. People often share their struggles on Reddit, but the replies usually come from people without clinical training. This paper builds on an existing AI model that sorts posts by mental health condition, emotion and sentiment. The researchers add a fourth task: spotting the topic of a post, using labels drawn from therapist-client talks in the CounselChat dataset. They also clean up noisy labels in two steps. The AI first re-checks cases it is moderately unsure about, then the most uncertain ones, and keeps only answers with at least 0.7 confidence. Tested on 54,412 Reddit posts, the best setup improved topic classification by 1.57% and gave smaller gains on the other three tasks. The authors say this reduces label noise and that topics give useful extra context.
