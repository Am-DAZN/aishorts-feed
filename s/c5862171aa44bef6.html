One AI Model Outlines Heart Structures Across Many Ultrasound Views

Researchers built P2Echo, a single AI model that marks different parts of the heart in ultrasound scans. Doctors take these scans from several standard angles, called views, and the heart looks quite different in each. Usually, each view needs its own specialized model. P2Echo instead takes a short text prompt that names the view and the structure to outline. A new module called DyITA uses that prompt to adjust how the model processes the image, with no change to its design. The team tested it on a large internal dataset and on three outside datasets it had not seen in training. It matched or beat view-specific models and other general-purpose models, while using far less computing power. The code is publicly available.
