Richpanel uses OpenAI models to automate half of customer support

Richpanel, a customer support company, builds AI agents that handle support requests for businesses. According to OpenAI's blog, it uses three OpenAI models to do this. GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol give the agents their tools and step-by-step procedures, so they know how to act on a request. GPT-6 Luna handles the conversations with customers. The post's title says companies can automate 50% of their support within 30 days. The source gives no further detail on how that figure was measured. It shows how companies are splitting AI work across several models, each with a different job.
