Paper Argues Humans and AI Needing Each Other Won't Keep Humans Safe

A new research paper challenges a common hope about advanced AI. The hope is that humans and AI will depend on each other, so both will stay well off. The authors say this fails as AI gets more capable. Using a simple game theory model, they show that bargaining only guarantees each side what it would get if talks broke down. For humans, that can be less than they had before AI. Humans keep their position only while they have leverage. That means the AI needs something they supply, or they can impose big costs on it. Without that leverage, humans do well only if the AI is aligned with them, meaning its goals match human goals. If the AI's goals are insatiable, the needed alignment nears perfect as AI grows stronger. The authors conclude that mutualism cannot replace alignment.
