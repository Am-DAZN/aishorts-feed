Google DeepMind Unveils Genie 4, a New World Model

Google DeepMind has announced Genie 4, a new version of its world model. A world model is an AI system that can simulate a virtual environment. The company says Genie 4 offers a big jump in realism and works in real time, meaning the simulated world responds as you use it. The source text is brief and gives no further details, such as how the model works, how it was tested, or when people can try it.
