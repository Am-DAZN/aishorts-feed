Why AI Equation Finders Can Miss Key Terms Even When Fits Are Accurate

Some AI methods try to discover the math equations behind physical systems, such as how heat spreads or fluids move. This paper shows that a good fit to the data does not guarantee the right equation. A common step that picks which terms to keep can throw away a real one. In tests on advection-diffusion equations, the diffusion term was dropped even though its coefficient was recovered almost perfectly. The authors derive math rules for when this happens. They also test fixes, such as refitting with the true terms, and find these help only in part. Other selection methods still add extra terms in learned models. The authors stress these are limited tests. They do not show a general cure or that the methods work reliably everywhere.
