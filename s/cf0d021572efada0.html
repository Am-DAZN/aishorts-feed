CCFinance Rebuilds Company News Datasets From Public Web Archives

Many finance studies rely on paid news archives that link stories to companies. That makes the work hard for others to repeat. CCFinance offers a free alternative built on Common Crawl News, a public web archive. Starting from 14.8 million English records, the team found 5.22 million distinct, consistently dated articles linked to companies. The tool records which version of a story was saved, which companies it names and how often. Estimated precision for accepted company links is 96.8%. Requiring three mentions of a company raises the share of stories truly about it from 31.9% to 66.1%, but cuts the number of company-years with at least 50 qualifying stories from 13,918 to 8,641. The authors also reran three earlier-style studies. Exact reconstruction still needs a restricted snapshot and derived files.
