DexForesight Helps Robot Hands Plan Ahead Without Predicting the Future

Robot hands need to understand space and anticipate how fingers and objects will move. Many systems do this by explicitly predicting future scenes. DexForesight takes a different path. During training, it builds a world model from depth data, hand shape and demonstration trajectories. This model predicts how points on the hand move and how visual features change, even for fingers hidden from the cameras. The robot's policy, the part that picks actions, then learns from this model's knowledge. At deployment, the world model is dropped. The policy uses only camera images, language instructions and robot state, with no depth input. In 11 simulated tasks, success rose from 49.4% to 60.5%. On three real-robot tasks, it reached 58.3%, versus 50.0% for the strongest baseline.
