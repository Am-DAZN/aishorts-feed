Google DeepMind Unveils Gemini Robotics 2.5 for Better Robot Dexterity

Google DeepMind has announced Gemini Robotics 2.5, which it calls its most capable vision-language-action model so far. That is an AI system that looks at the world, understands instructions in plain language, and turns them into robot actions. DeepMind says the update improves how skillfully robots use their hands and tools, how reliably they work, and how safely they behave. The source gives few other details. The goal is robots that can handle many real-world tasks, not just one narrow job.
