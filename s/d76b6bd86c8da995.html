New Photo Test Challenges AI to Tell Apart Near-Identical Dwarf Statues

Most tests of image search focus on landmarks. This paper offers a different challenge: small bronze dwarf statues in Wrocław, Poland, many from themed series with the same style. The new dataset, Doppeldwarf-306, has 1,691 openly licensed photos of 306 statues. With no extra training, a model called DINOv2 picked the right statue first 93.1% of the time. The best of thirteen models reached 94.7%. Accuracy dropped slowly as more statues were added. Using the user's location as a weight, not a filter, lifted DINOv2 from 93.4% to 98.7% for a simulated user 25 metres away. Filtering to nearby statues helped less than picking random ones, because real statues sit close together and look alike.
