NYT Photographers Told to Skip Photoshop Update Over AI Features

Simon Willison shared a personal story after seeing a report that The New York Times warned its photographers not to update Photoshop because of its AI features. A Times photographer took his picture at home earlier this year for a story about Moltbook. When Willison asked if a stain on his shirt could be fixed, the photographer said absolutely not. The paper shows photos as they were taken. Cropping and tone balancing are allowed, but no other edits. The photographer also spent time finding the best natural light, and even moved Willison's laptop shot into the garden to avoid screen glare. Willison says newspaper photography is photojournalism, and the Times takes that seriously.
