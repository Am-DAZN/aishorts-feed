LeanGuard Uses Formal Proofs to Block Forbidden AI Agent Actions

AI agents that use tools, like booking systems or databases, sometimes take actions their rules forbid. LeanGuard is a safety layer that checks every proposed action against a written policy before it runs. The policies are written in Lean4, a language for formal proofs, and unclear cases are blocked by default. In tests on Tau-Bench with five Qwen models, unguarded agents ran at least 33 forbidden calls, while LeanGuard let none through. Task success did not change significantly when the agent got feedback on why a call was denied. On another test, LeanGuard cut prompt-injection attack success from 57.9% to 5.9%. It can also try to fix a blocked call and prove the fix is allowed, which worked for 88 of 252 denials caused by argument values.
