Hogwarts Legacy Gets NVIDIA DLSS 5 Graphics Upgrade on GeForce NOW

NVIDIA has added DLSS 5 to Hogwarts Legacy on its GeForce NOW cloud gaming service. The feature, called 3D-Guided Neural Rendering, adds more lifelike lighting and material detail. It keeps the game's original shapes and textures. Developer Avalanche Software tuned it to protect the game's look and style. Ultimate members get RTX 5080-class performance on PCs, Macs, phones, TVs and more, with no big downloads or hardware upgrade. The same post says World of Warcraft: Forever is now streaming, with a launch-week access reward for members. Ubisoft also brings new expansions for The Division 2 and Anno 117: Pax Romana. Two dozen games join the service in November.
