Search method steers crystal-design AI without retraining it

Researchers present a way to guide AI models that design new crystal structures, without changing the models themselves. The method, called Diffusion Tree Sampling, uses a tree search while the model builds each crystal step by step. Retraining a model for every new goal is costly, and other guidance tricks do not work for many real tasks. The team shows how to reuse existing models that already respond to property requests. They also let one search find crystals that balance several goals at once, such as high dielectric constant and high bandgap. In tests, the approach made generated crystals more stable, produced rare space groups and high bulk modulus, and improved outputs overall. This could make materials discovery cheaper and more flexible.
