Hugging Face blog looks at building multi-agent AI systems

Hugging Face has published a blog post titled "Swarms, Flocks, Gangs: How to build, train, and evaluate multi-agent systems." The title suggests a guide to systems where several AI agents work together instead of one. It points to three steps: building them, training them, and testing how well they work. No further details were available from the post, so what methods or tools it covers is not known. Multi-agent systems are a growing area in AI, so practical guidance on them may be useful to developers.
