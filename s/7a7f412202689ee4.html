Two Minute Papers Interviews Spencer Huang on Robots and AI's Future

A new Two Minute Papers video features a long conversation with Spencer Huang about where AI is heading. The chapter list shows the topics. They include why robots are hard to build, what kind of data trains them best, and how AI learns from failure. They also cover building virtual worlds for AI, and the reality gap, which is the difference between simulation and the real world. Other chapters look at robots getting injured, practicing 100 years in seconds, and a popcorn-making robot. The talk ends by asking if AI will ever be good enough. The video description gives only chapter titles, so the details of what Huang says are not included here.
