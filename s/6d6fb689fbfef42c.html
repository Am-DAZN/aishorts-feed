New Test Checks If AI Agents Can Track Land Change From Satellite Images

Researchers have built EarthRecord, a benchmark that tests whether AI agents can study how land changes using four decades of raw satellite data. It covers 56 sites and data from Landsat, MODIS, VIIRS and Sentinel-2. The agents must clean the data themselves, including removing clouds and matching different satellites. The test has 238 questions across ten tasks. Twelve AI agents were tested. The best ones finished most analyses from start to finish, but struggled when they had to make maps from images. In one check, the team changed the data from a single satellite while the ground stayed the same. About half of the correct answers changed too. This suggests the agents follow the pixels, not the real change on the ground. The benchmark gives researchers a way to measure and improve this.
