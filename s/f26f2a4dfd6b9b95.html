OpenAI offers 200,000 researchers free access to its top models

OpenAI is expanding its ChatGPT for Academic Researchers program. The company says it will now give 200,000 researchers around the world free access to its frontier models. Frontier models are the most advanced AI systems a company has built. The move is meant to widen access to AI for scientists, who can use these tools in their work. The announcement was posted on OpenAI's blog. The short post does not say how researchers can apply or which fields will be covered.
