Gondola says revenue doubled as its team builds with Codex

Gondola is a company that helps creative people get credit for their work and get hired. According to OpenAI's blog, the team now uses Codex, OpenAI's coding tool, to build more. The post says Codex raised its lead engineer's output by 50%. It also says the team uses ChatGPT to make everyday work simpler. The headline says Gondola's revenue has doubled. The source gives few other details, so it is unclear how much of that growth comes from the AI tools. The story shows how a small team can use AI helpers to get more done.
