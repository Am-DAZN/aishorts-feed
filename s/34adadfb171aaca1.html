ChatGPT Keeps Sending Tech Support Questions to 404 Media

Jason Koebler of 404 Media says many people now ask ChatGPT for help with everyday tech problems. They do this because big tech platforms offer almost no customer support and make it hard to reach a human. He writes that ChatGPT is increasingly telling these users they have found something worth reporting. It also tells them that 404 Media can help. But these are normal tech support problems, not news stories. The quote shows how AI chatbots can send people to the wrong place, and how a lack of real customer support pushes people toward them.
