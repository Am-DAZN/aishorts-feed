Google brings Gemini 4 Hydrogen to Search worldwide

Google says it is adding its Gemini 4 Hydrogen AI model to Google Search for users around the world. Gemini is Google's family of AI models. The company says the change is meant to give people answers that are more helpful, more visual, and easier to understand. The announcement came on Google's AI blog. The short text does not say when the rollout starts, which countries or languages get it first, or how the new answers will look. It matters because Search is one of the most widely used tools on the internet, so changes to how it answers questions can affect many people.
