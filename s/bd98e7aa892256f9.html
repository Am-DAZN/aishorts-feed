New test asks if AI agents can learn unwritten social rules

Researchers released NormGym, a benchmark that checks whether AI agents can pick up hidden social rules by watching others. A newcomer agent must do its own task in a group of agents while working out an unknown rule. It has to tell what is possible from what is allowed, and learn from others' behavior and from punishments. The best language model tested managed both the task and the rule just 55.3% of the time. Models sometimes changed course after being punished for their own actions. But they struggled to learn the rule from what other agents did. Fine-tuning, which means extra training, may not fix this. The authors say today's models lack a reliable way to learn social norms through interaction.
