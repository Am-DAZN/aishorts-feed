OpenAI and ServiceNow deepen partnership on workplace AI tools

OpenAI and ServiceNow have widened their partnership. ServiceNow is a company that makes software for running work tasks inside businesses. Under the deal, OpenAI's AI models and agents, which are AI programs that can carry out tasks, will be built into ServiceNow. In the other direction, ServiceNow workflows will be brought into ChatGPT and Codex, OpenAI's tools for chat and coding. The announcement says little else, so details like timing and pricing are not yet clear. The move shows AI firms working with business software makers to place their tools where employees already do their jobs.
