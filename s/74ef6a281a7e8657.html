New training method teaches AI models how to reason about following rules

Researchers introduced Reflect-Reasoning, a way to train language models to think through whether their answers follow a given set of principles, instead of just memorizing which principles to follow. Today, many companies steer models with system prompts, which are written instructions given at run time. Across five models, the new method beat both system prompting and prompts that ask the model to reflect on its own answer. The gains held on new topics like medical and financial rules. It also cut rare but serious rule violations. The researchers say it does not weaken a model's built-in safety and resists jailbreaks that use adversarial principles. It lets models adapt to new principles and show their reasoning without more fine-tuning.
