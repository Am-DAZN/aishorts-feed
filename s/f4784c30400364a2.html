OpenAI's MedChemBench tests if AI can spot promising drug candidates

OpenAI has described a new test called MedChemBench. It checks how well AI models handle early steps in drug discovery. The test looks at three skills. First, can the model read molecular structures, which are the shapes and makeup of chemical compounds? Second, can it reason from experimental evidence? Third, can it pick which compounds to pursue? Early drug discovery is the stage where scientists narrow down many possible compounds to a few worth studying. The benchmark gives a way to measure whether AI could support that work. The source gives no results or scores.
