Best Backup AI Model Is the One That Fixes the Main Model's Mistakes

Some AI systems tag spans of text, such as names or places, and flag their riskiest answers for a person to check. A second model can help rank which answers are risky. This paper asks how to pick that second model. Its answer: choose the one that is right where the main model is wrong. Across 576 model pairings on three English tests, this rule kept 82% of the best partner's gain. Picking by overall span F1 score, or at random, kept only 69-70%. The rule also held up on a fourth dataset. The authors also found that using the second model's full probability scores works better than using only its final labels, which throw away half its value. The approach also worked across 51 languages and regions in the MASSIVE dataset.
