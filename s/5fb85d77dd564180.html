AVTR lets vision AI imagine images and pick how to reason

Researchers propose AVTR, a method for AI models that handle both images and text. Most of these models solve hard problems by writing out their reasoning in words. That can hurt questions that depend on fine visual details, and it often produces long, needless explanations for simple questions. AVTR adds a second way to think: the model generates visual images to look again at what it saw and pull out key evidence. It also learns to choose between visual reflection and text reasoning for each question. Across 16 benchmarks, it beat the Qwen3.5 baseline by 4.5 points on average and cut response time by 33%. It also worked with a smaller 2B-size model, beating other 2B-scale baselines.
