FinEvolveKG Keeps AI Prompt Edits Contained When Building Financial Knowledge Graphs

Banks build knowledge graphs, which are maps of facts and links, from company 10-K filings. Different teams, like risk and portfolio managers, need different details. Researchers present FinEvolveKG, which splits the instructions given to an AI into a shared block and a team-specific block. An automatic prompt optimizer, GEPA, may only edit the team-specific block, so every change is easy to track and review. In tests on three team types, the best version beat a fixed prompt by 19.4 to 30.9 percent. It also beat a version that could rewrite the whole prompt by 2.2 to 6.4 percent, and it held up better on new kinds of data. Expert reviewers preferred its results in 70.1 percent of judgments. The authors say the gains cannot be tied directly to the split itself.
