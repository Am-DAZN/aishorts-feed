OpenAI's Ted Sanders explains how its AI models get their names

Ted Sanders of OpenAI explained how the company names its models. He said AI performance has many sides, so the model best at math is not always best at jokes. Still, model size explains much of the difference. Bigger models tend to be more reliable, cost more per token (the small chunks of text an AI reads and writes), and remember more obscure facts. He said OpenAI's models are ordered by size: Luna, Terra, Sol, Astra. Version numbers signal release order, ties to other releases, and how big the improvement is. A 6.1, for example, came after 5.6, may share training data or personality with other 6.1 models, and is not a big enough leap to deserve a 7.
