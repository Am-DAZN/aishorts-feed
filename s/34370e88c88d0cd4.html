Hugging Face Blog Asks If AI Can Judge Research Novelty

A Hugging Face blog post asks a simple question: can we trust large language models (LLMs), the AI systems behind chatbots, to decide whether a research idea is new? The full text was not available, so the details of the post are unknown. The title shows the topic. People are starting to use AI tools to review ideas and papers, and judging novelty is hard even for experts. If these tools get it wrong, good ideas could be missed or old ideas could be passed off as new.
