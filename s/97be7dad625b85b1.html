NVIDIA unveils GR00T 2 robot model at CoRL, trained partly in simulation

At the Conference on Robot Learning in Austin, NVIDIA introduced new open robotics tools. The main one is GR00T 2, a "world action model": it helps robots see their surroundings, predict how actions change them, and carry out multistep tasks. It was trained on robot and human data, including 100,000 hours of human first-person video. NVIDIA says it completed 40 of 50 tasks at least once without task-specific demos. With one demonstration per task it averaged 71% success, rising to 95% with five. Foxconn, Siemens and others are testing it. NVIDIA also released a Video to Data workflow that turns human video into simulated robot demonstrations. The work aims to move robots from lab demos to factory use.
