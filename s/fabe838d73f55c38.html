Meta launches Muse Ember, a small, cheap AI model for agent work

Meta AI has released Muse Ember, its smallest and most efficient model. Meta says it is built for the repeat work AI agents do all day, such as tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, data extraction and reading long documents. For short prompts, it costs $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, the same as GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5. Prices rise for longer prompts, and Ember lands between the other two there. Simon Willison notes that price per token alone can mislead, since models use different numbers of reasoning tokens. On Artificial Analysis charts of intelligence versus cost per task, Ember sits on the efficiency frontier, meaning no model scores higher for less. His pelican-drawing tests looked similar to results from Muse Wonder, which costs 40 times more.
