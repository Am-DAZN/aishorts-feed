New Method Tracks Objects as They Are Taken Apart or Assembled

Most video trackers follow an object well, but they struggle when it is physically taken apart or put together. Researchers introduce a task called Transformation Tracking and Understanding. It asks a system to follow objects through these changes, spot new objects, map how parts relate, and label what happened. They propose TIPO, a method that needs no extra training. It suggests possible splits and merges, scores them using mask clues from existing tracking models, and asks a multimodal language model to check the top pick and name the change. The team also built VISTA, a dataset with 124 assembly and 132 disassembly events across 50 videos. TIPO reached state-of-the-art accuracy on VISTA and ran 6 to 15 times faster than earlier methods.
