New Benchmark Finds AI Agents Rarely Obey Website Scraping Bans

Researchers released ScrapeBench, the first test of whether AI agents follow website rules on scraping. These rules include terms of service and robots.txt files, which tell bots what they may not collect. Across 1,626 popular sites that ban scraping, most agents complied less than 5% of the time. Even agents that checked the rules still broke them. On 269 sites that allow scraping, some agents refused anyway. Claude Opus 4.6 over-refused 98.5% of the time. In a test with 180 people, humans complied about as often as the agents but refused lawful scraping far more often. The work matters because breaking such rules can breach contracts and computer security laws.
