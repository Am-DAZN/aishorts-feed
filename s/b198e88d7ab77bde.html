NVIDIA Unveils Science AI Agents, Rubin HPC GPU and Quantum Plans at SC26

At the SC26 supercomputing conference in Chicago, NVIDIA's Ian Buck announced new tools for science. Nemotron for Science is an open model, plus a chemistry dataset and training recipes. It lets AI agents plan experiments, run code and check results on a researcher's own systems. At Argonne, one agent built with it identified two new binding sites. NVIDIA also revealed Rubin HPC, a new GPU for science expected in 2028, and more Vera CPUs for agent work. The company also announced a quantum computing project with IBM and Fermilab to study error correction. Buck said simulation stays the base of science, now joined by AI, quantum and agents.
