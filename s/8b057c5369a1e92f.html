Transformer Model Predicts the Next Step in Criminal Court Cases

Researchers built an AI model that treats a criminal court case as a series of events, such as charges accepted, bond set or a plea deal made. Given the case so far, it predicts what the next proceeding will be. Past studies mostly predicted one decision, like whether someone would reoffend. The team used public court records from a mid-sized U.S. city, covering thousands of cases. The model reads clerk notes plus details about the defendant, prior charges and the courtroom. It scored 0.57 on a standard accuracy measure (micro-F1) across 20 possible outcomes. That beats several baseline methods and is similar to another model type called a GRU. The authors say the tool could help court observers understand decisions and spot emerging problems in the courts they study.
