AI models can often name who wrote a text from one sample

A new paper shows that top AI models can guess an author from a single text, using only a simple prompt. They get no web access, tools or writing samples. The models can tell if a human or an AI wrote it. If an AI did, they can name the lab behind it. If a human did, they can often name the person. Opus 5.5 picked the right lab for 98% of texts from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google models. Fable 5.2 named the author of 55% of 15,000 anonymous ICML 2020 reviews that were never public. It also found the username behind 69% of comments from active Redditors. Accuracy depends on how much of a person's writing was in the training data. The authors warn that anyone with lots of online writing may be easier to identify than thought, and defenses are limited.
