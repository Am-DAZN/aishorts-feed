Study finds AI models group emotions into broad positive and negative camps

Researchers studied how seven large language models (LLMs, the AI behind chatbots) organize emotions inside. They used GoEmotions, a set of Reddit comments labeled with 27 detailed emotions plus neutral. When they grouped the models' internal data without giving any emotion labels, the clearest split was just two groups: mostly positive and mostly negative. The models squeezed many fine emotions together, which the authors call overcompression. But simple tests called linear probes could still pull out the detailed emotion information. So the fine detail is still there, even though broad feelings shape the overall structure more strongly.
