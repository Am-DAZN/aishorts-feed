Amazon engineering leaders: if AI doesn't help your work, say so

Simon Willison shared a quote from Terk Terkowitz, taken from an update by Amazon engineering leadership on the role of developers in the age of AI. The message is that if AI tools do not help your work, that is a finding, not a failing. Engineers should write down why, share it with their peers, tell their manager, and look for solutions. The update says an engineer who can explain where AI does not yet help is showing the judgement the company wants. It suggests Amazon wants honest feedback on AI tools, not blind use of them.
