Fewer than half of AI agent insights actually help businesses, survey finds

A new report from MIT Technology Review Insights looks at how well AI agents analyze company data. On average, 48% of their insights lead to actions that improve the business. That is up from 37% a year ago, but more than half still fail. A small group of "insight leaders" does much better, with over 70%. The top cause of weak insights is missing business context, cited by 55% of executives. Fragmented data is next, at 45%. Also, 52% of firms say agents produce too many insights to check. Companies are now focusing on stronger data governance (60%) and adding a semantic layer (45%), which helps agents understand what data means. The report was produced by the magazine's custom content arm, not its editorial staff.
