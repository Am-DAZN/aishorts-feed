Two Minute Papers Host Releases a Surprise Original Song

Károly Zsolnai-Fehér, the host of the Two Minute Papers channel, has posted a surprise music video instead of his usual AI research explainer. He says it took him three months after finishing the song to find the courage to record himself on camera. He plays guitar alongside five other musicians: a vocalist, a drummer, a bassist, a saxophone player and a second guitarist. He thanks them for their help. He also shares the guitar tab for free, so fans who want to play a cover can download it. The video is a light, personal break from the channel's normal content.
