Survey: Only 16% of firms get transformative value from AI

An MIT Technology Review Insights poll found that every company surveyed uses AI, but fewer than one in five (16%) get transformative value from it. Another 40% see moderate gains in specific tasks or teams, 21% report meaningful gains, and 23% see minimal or no returns. Most firms are still piloting AI (38%) or using it to assist staff (35%). Only 12% have AI agents in production. Prukalpa Sankar, co-founder of Atlan, says the missing piece is context, meaning how well AI understands a business. She describes a context layer as software infrastructure between business systems and AI tools that turns company knowledge into usable, governed context. Without it, she says, AI is like a blindfolded intern. The report was produced by the magazine's custom content arm, not its editorial staff.
