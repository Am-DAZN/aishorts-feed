Google makes Gemini Tasks free in six countries

Google says Tasks in Gemini is now free of charge in six countries. Tasks lets the Gemini assistant take care of jobs for you instead of only answering questions. Google is also adding new features, starting in the U.S. The tool is getting deeper links with Google Workspace, the company's set of work apps. That should let Gemini get more things done on your behalf. The post does not list the six countries or give details on every new feature. Making the tool free could put it in the hands of many more people.
