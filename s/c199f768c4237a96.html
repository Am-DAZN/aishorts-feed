SpatialArena Offers One Fair Test for 3D Vision AI Models

Researchers built SpatialArena, an open benchmark that scores 3D vision models under the same rules. Past results were hard to compare. Each paper picked its own frames, image sizes and test sets. The way scores are lined up with real measurements also skewed results. Fitting depth in the wrong space nearly tripled its error. SpatialArena covers five tasks, from depth from a single image to mapping while moving. It uses 23 public datasets with 418,929 images, plus the team's own captures. The team tested 52 models. They found that single-image depth is close to maxed out on public tests, but not on large, messy scenes. Progress now comes from keeping results consistent across frames and views. There, models that rebuild 3D scenes directly match or beat single-image specialists. The benchmark, toolbox and leaderboard will be released publicly.
