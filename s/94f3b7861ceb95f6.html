AI Search Rounds Up Gemini 4 Carbon, AlphaGenome 2 and Many New AI Tools

A new AI Search video covers a long list of recent AI releases. The biggest item is a review of Gemini 4 Carbon, a model from Google. The video also looks at Gemini 4 Hydrogen and a Gemini Decisions API. Other topics include AlphaGenome 2 from DeepMind, which is about how cells read DNA to help understand disease. The host also covers Meta's Muse Ember, Baidu's ERNIE 5.5 Flash preview, and Seedream 5.5 from sponsor BytePlus. There are also new voice and speech tools like Fish Audio Live 3 and ZONOS3-Mini, plus ACE Music v1 for making music. Several 3D, video and robotics research projects are included too. The description gives only names and links, not details, so the video itself holds the full findings.
