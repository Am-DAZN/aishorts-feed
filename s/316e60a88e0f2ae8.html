Writer says AI's voice is now everywhere, from videos to inboxes

Simon Willison shared a short quote from Jack Despain Zhou, taken from a piece called "Spitting in the Face of Glaude." The writer says AI has made people sound alike. He hears the voice of Claude, an AI chatbot, in YouTube videos and conference talks. He also sees it in emails and article pitches sent by people with serious jobs and credentials. These people want to publish work tied to their own reputations, and his. The quote's point is that AI writing is flattening individual voices, and that this is already happening.
