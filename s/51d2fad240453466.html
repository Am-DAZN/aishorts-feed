DeepMind opens datasets behind its AI weather models

Google DeepMind has shared the datasets behind its AI weather models. The blog post describes two kinds of data: precipitation records, which track rain and snow, and images from geostationary satellites, which stay fixed over one spot on Earth. DeepMind calls them foundational datasets meant to help people observe the planet clearly. The source gives few other details, such as how to access them or how large they are. Open data like this can help outside researchers build and test their own weather tools.
