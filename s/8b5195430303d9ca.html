Paper Warns AI's Step-by-Step Reasoning May Not Show Its Real Thinking

A new position paper argues that chain-of-thought, where an AI writes out its reasoning before answering, is not a reliable way to understand how the model works. The written steps can look convincing but often differ from the hidden computations that actually drive the answer. The authors reviewed 1,000 recent papers on the technique. About 21.7% treat it as a key tool for interpretability, including some in medicine, law and autonomous systems. The authors say this is risky. They urge researchers to avoid relying on it alone, to test whether the steps are faithful to the model's real reasoning, and to report those tests. They still see value in chain-of-thought for communication and monitoring.
