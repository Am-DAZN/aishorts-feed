New NPC Cancer Benchmark Tests AI on Full Patient Care, Not Just Quiz Questions

Researchers released NPCBench, a test for medical AI built around nasopharyngeal carcinoma, a cancer of the upper throat. Most medical tests ask single questions or look at one image. NPCBench instead follows how a specialist trains, from guidelines to planning a case, reviewing a patient over time, and answering open-ended consultation questions. It has 4,777 test items and a group of 100 patients with 417 scored tasks. The team tried 30 models. Many did well on single tasks, but did not get both the diagnosis and the care steps right for the same patient. Among 21 models that handle images and text, the best strict success rate on full care pathways was 23%. The authors say this shows current tests miss important gaps in AI reasoning over a patient's course of care.
