CoworkOS lets AI agents handle long, complex work alongside people

Researchers introduced CoworkOS, a system that helps AI agents do long projects like documents, slide decks, CAD models and Blender scenes. The AI model chooses what to do, who to hand tasks to, and when work is good enough. A separate runtime keeps track of progress and feedback. Humans and agents can edit the same files, so people can step in by chatting or by changing the work directly. Shared workspaces and lasting memory keep files, decisions and job logs available after interruptions. The authors say it is highly competitive with agent apps such as Codex and DeepSeek Harness across 12 benchmarks. In one case study, it used GLM-5.3 to automatically train Qwen3.6-35B-A3B over five rounds. The average score on eight benchmarks rose from 33.19 to 42.95.
