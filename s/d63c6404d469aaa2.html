llm-gemini 0.38.1 adds missing top reasoning level for Gemini 4 models

Simon Willison released a small update to llm-gemini, his plugin that lets the LLM tool use Google's Gemini models. Gemini 4 Carbon supports three reasoning levels: low, medium and high. Hydrogen and Neon also have a fourth level, called xhigh. Willison missed this when he first added those models, and the new release fixes it. The extra setting matters. In his comparison, Hydrogen at high used 5,311 output tokens and cost 3.99 cents. At xhigh it used 31,477 tokens and cost 23.61 cents. The pelican image it produced was competitive with the one from Gemini 4 Neon. So higher reasoning can improve results, but it costs more.
