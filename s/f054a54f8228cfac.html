Revolut uses OpenAI models and Codex to speed up coding

Revolut, the financial app company, is using OpenAI models and Codex, OpenAI's AI coding tool, in its daily work. According to OpenAI's blog, this has cut coding time by 30 to 60 percent. The company is also using the tools to scale up its financial crime investigations, the work of spotting and checking suspicious activity. Revolut says it can do this while keeping its security standards high. The story shows how a finance company is trying to move faster with AI without lowering its guard on safety.
