DeepMind releases Mass Spec Skills to help scientists identify molecules

Google DeepMind has introduced Mass Spec Skills, a tool for people who use Google Antigravity. It helps scientists interpret tandem mass spectra. A mass spectrum is a kind of readout that shows the pieces a molecule breaks into when it is analyzed. Reading these patterns can reveal what the molecule is and how it is built. The source gives few details beyond this, so how it works and how well it performs is not yet clear. The aim is to make it easier to go from raw spectrum data to a molecule's structure.
