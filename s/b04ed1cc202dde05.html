New benchmark tests AI agents playing League of Legends in real time

Researchers released LOLPlay, a test that checks how well AI agents can play the video game League of Legends. The agents see only screenshots and control the game with mouse and keyboard. The test has 36 tasks tied to specific champions, covering combat, teamwork, resource use and objectives. It also uses 82 automatic checks to show partial progress, not just pass or fail. Three models were tried at three levels of reasoning effort. The best, GPT-6 Astra, completed 25.0% of tasks at low and medium effort. Thinking harder did not help. High effort scored no better than low effort for any model. For Astra, longer thinking left fewer turns to act. The game keeps moving, so some actions landed after the scene had already changed. The authors say agents need to pair careful visual reasoning with timely action.
