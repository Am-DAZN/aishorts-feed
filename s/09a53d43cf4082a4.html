AI Explained: Labs hint at releasing AI models they can't fully control

A new AI Explained video looks at a busy week in AI. It says Sam Altman of OpenAI and Anthropic suggested they might release models whose behavior they cannot verify. The video also covers an Anthropic staff survey, five reasons Anthropic chief Dario Amodei gives for pressing ahead, and Altman's view that only alignment, meaning making AI follow human goals, will work. It also looks at Google's new Gemini 4 Carbon model and why AI models keep surprising even Anthropic. The source is a video description with chapter notes and links, so it gives few details. It matters because it raises questions about how safely top labs plan to ship powerful AI.
