Do AI Language Models Hold Psychology Concepts Inside, or Just Mimic Them?

Researchers asked whether large language models truly represent psychological traits internally or only produce answers that look like psychology data. They tested 14 versions of Llama 3 and Qwen 2.5. Each model saw one questionnaire's answers from 272 Taiwanese university students and predicted answers on six other questionnaires. Larger models matched, and even exaggerated, the links between scales seen in people. Inside the models, larger instruction-tuned versions showed patterns that lined up with the human ones, while base versions showed little. The stronger this pattern, the more the model exaggerated. The authors say this shows the information can be read out of the models. It does not prove those internal patterns cause the answers.
