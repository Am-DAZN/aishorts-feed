Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Launches on GeForce NOW Cloud Gaming

NVIDIA says Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is available on its GeForce NOW cloud gaming service at launch. Players can stream the new campaign, multiplayer and the DMZ extraction mode without downloading the game. Ultimate members get GeForce RTX 5080-class power in the cloud, which lets the campaign use ray tracing, a method for more realistic light and shadows. NVIDIA also says existing Fire TV Stick users can now buy one-month Performance or Ultimate memberships directly through Amazon. Square Enix's FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE also joins the service this week, as part of six new games. The updates make it easier to play big games on devices without a powerful PC.
