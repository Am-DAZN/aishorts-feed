Small AI Models Borrow a Big Model's Know-How Without Retraining

Researchers propose Artifact Distillation, a way to pass skills from a top AI model to a smaller one without retraining it. Usual distillation fine-tunes the small model on the big model's answers, which takes computing power and hides what was learned. Here, the big model solves practice problems and writes down how it did it, building a bank of saved notes. The small model looks up these notes while working. In tests on LiveBench, a bank written by Claude Opus 4.5 helped Claude Haiku 4.5 beat Claude Sonnet 4.5 on spatial reasoning, 0.707 to 0.647, at 60% of the cost. The same bank also lifted Qwen3.5-27B from 0.730 to 0.800, so it works across model families.
