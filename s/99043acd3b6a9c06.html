GeForce NOW Honors Its Community as Black Friday Discounts Start

NVIDIA's cloud gaming service, GeForce NOW, is celebrating the players, creators and moderators behind its community. Cloud gaming means the game runs on NVIDIA's powerful computers and streams to your device. Ambassadors say it let them play new games on old laptops, TVs, phones and a Steam Deck, and make friends along the way. NVIDIA is also offering 30% off the first six months of Performance and Ultimate memberships. The Black Friday sale runs through Monday, Nov. 30, in select regions while supplies last. War Thunder gets a new look with NVIDIA DLSS 5 for Ultimate members, and 12 new games join the library.
