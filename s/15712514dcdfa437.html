InclusionMed Tests Medical AI Across Many Healthcare Roles, Not Just Doctors

Researchers launched InclusionMed, an open project to test medical AI more fairly. Today's tests cover some healthcare roles and tasks much better than others. The team built an Atlas that sorts tests by three healthcare roles and three AI skills. For the first release, they picked four public tests from 388 candidates, then added their own private test on diagnosis. Together, the five tests fill three of nine role-and-skill slots. They scored top language models and combined the results into an Index, with overall scores from 17.5 to 49.1 out of 100. Model rankings changed depending on the role or skill. The ten best models all did better at talking with patients than at clinical decisions. Models with similar scores also had very different estimated running costs. The team says tests must cover more needs, and invites the community to contribute.
