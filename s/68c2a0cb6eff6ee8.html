AI Search roundup covers Gemini Omni 1.2, GPT Images 2.6, Kimi K3.1 and more

AI Search has posted a long news video that runs through dozens of new AI projects in one sitting. The list includes Google's Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, OpenAI's GPT Images 2.6 and GPT-6 Cyber, and Kimi K3.1 from Moonshot AI. It also covers Baidu's ERNIE 5.5 Flash, Ant's Ling 3.2 Flash, Tencent's Hy4 and Meta's Muse, a voice and avatar tool. Other items are research projects on video upscaling, 3D generation, human motion and robotics, plus a piece on weight-space programming. The video points to each project's page, so viewers can read more. It is a quick way to catch up on a busy stretch of AI releases, though the description gives few details on each one.
