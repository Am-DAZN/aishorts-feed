π^4 model reads 3D shape and motion from ordinary photos and video

Researchers released π^4, a single AI model that looks at plain photos or video and works out the 3D shape of a scene and how things in it move. It accepts one image, a loose set of images, or a video, with no camera setup data needed. It estimates depth, camera positions and real-world scale. For multiple images, it also tracks points over time and marks moving objects. The model has about 0.5 billion parameters, less than half the size of most rivals. The authors report the best overall average rank on geometry tests and competitive results on tracking and moving-object tasks. It handles scenes with thousands of images in one pass, even though it was trained on at most 64 views. Code and models are public.
