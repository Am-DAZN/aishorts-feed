Queer Community Members Audit AI and Find Harms Standard Checks Miss

Researchers ran a workshop at the EurIPS 2025 conference where 16 queer community members audited four AI case studies. They used the 4Cs harm framework: Content, Conduct, Contact and Contract. They also looked at where in the AI building process each harm began. In all four cases, participants traced problems back to how the problem was defined and how data was collected. They also found contracts that take value from vulnerable groups while giving them little way to seek redress. The authors say AI often treats majority groups as the default, which can erase or misclassify queer people. Standard audits rely on averages and can miss harms that only affected people notice. The study suggests that involving communities in audits can reveal specific harms that broad testing may overlook.
