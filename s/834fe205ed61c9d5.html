New AI Method Trains a Helper Model to Spark Creativity

Researchers say teaching AI to be creative is hard. If a model learns from past creative successes, it tends to repeat them, which kills new ideas. They call this the creative paradox. Their fix is called creative priming. One model, the priming model, writes a short hint, called a primer, for each prompt. A second model then uses that primer to write its answer. This keeps the creative process separate from the answer itself. The priming model is trained with a method called GRPO, a type of reinforcement learning. It earns rewards when answers are new, good, and varied. No single answer is rewarded, only the process that leads to creative ones. The authors argue this matters because many hoped-for AI advances, like new medicines and scientific theories, depend on creativity.
