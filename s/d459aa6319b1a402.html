WaveView Hides Camera View Clues in Image Noise to Speed Up 3D Scene Rendering

Novel view synthesis means creating a picture of a scene from a new camera angle. Many AI methods do this by warping the original image or feeding in camera data, which slows things down and can make the layout drift from reality. WaveView takes a different path. It places the warped source image into the starting noise of an existing image generator, using a wavelet-based math tool to adjust the noise locally. This leaves the generator free to fill in areas that were hidden in the original view. The model's design stays the same, and the added cost is small. On the DL3DV benchmark, it beat all baselines in every overlap group. In the hardest group, it gained 0.84 dB in PSNR (an image quality score) over MetaView and ran 2.1 times faster. For moving scenes, it also lifted AnyView's score from 11.58 to 12.35 dB.
