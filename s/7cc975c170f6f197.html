SailSearch: AI Agents Help Crack Hard Puzzle Benchmarks

Researchers built SailSearch, a workflow where a human guides two language-model agents to do algorithm research. They tested it on two hard math problems, Market Split and maximum independent set (MIS). For Market Split, the team's randomized algorithm solved 18 of 33 benchmark instances that were previously open, the first reported solutions using only a CPU. It solved 130 to 133 of 156 instances per run, with a 24-hour limit on two CPU cores. For MIS, the team made a generator that builds graphs with known best answers. On seven large graphs, the solvers they tested fell 5 to 15 vertices short of the best answer after eight hours. This shows how AI agents can help researchers design algorithms and build tough tests.
