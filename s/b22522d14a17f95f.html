BotPsy Brings a Small AI Assistant Straight to Your Device

A Hugging Face blog post introduces BotPsy, a personal assistant that runs on the device itself instead of in the cloud. According to the title, it uses an agentic language model with fewer than one billion parameters. Agentic means the AI can take steps and use tools to get tasks done, not just chat. Parameters are the numbers a model learns, so fewer usually means a smaller, lighter model. Running on-device can help with privacy and speed. No further details were available, so how well it works is not yet clear.
