ADIndex Speeds Up Long-Context AI by Trimming Attention Indexing Cost

DeepSeek Sparse Attention picks which parts of a long text a model should focus on. But the step that does the picking, called the indexer, still scans all earlier text for every query. At long lengths, it can take over half of the GPU time spent reading the prompt. Researchers propose ADIndex to fix this. It adjusts the approach for each layer of the model, trains lighter indexers by distillation (learning from the original), and lets groups of queries share a list of chosen tokens. Tests on DeepSeek-V3.2 and GLM-5.1 showed near-unchanged scores on LongBench, LongBench v2 and RULER. At 256K tokens, prompt processing was up to 6.22x faster and decoding up to 3.40x faster. End-to-end serving was 1.54x faster at 128K.
