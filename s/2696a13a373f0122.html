New AI Model Learns When to Think Hard and When to Answer Fast

Many AI models write out long reasoning steps before answering, which costs time and compute. This paper takes a different approach called zero-or-T thinking. The model either answers right away from its internal processing, with no extra words written, or does full step-by-step reasoning using T tokens (small pieces of text). A small routing block decides which to use. It estimates how much the slow mode would add, and only triggers it when that gain beats a price set per request. For tasks without per-step labels, the authors add a training method called CDPO. On JevBench, the 4B model scored 0.857 versus 0.851 for Jev, with median time of 0.179 seconds versus 0.652, a 72.5% drop. On MiniWoB, the 27B model cut token cost by 80.1%.
