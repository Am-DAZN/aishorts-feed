StructRL Builds Replay Graphs to Help AI Agents Learn Faster From Experience

Researchers created StructRL, a method that turns an AI agent's past experience into a map, called a replay graph. Once the map shows a path to the goal, a planning technique picks good actions along the routes the agent has already seen. These actions then guide standard learning systems, DQN and SAC. On three hard MiniGrid tasks with rare rewards, DQN's final success rose from 0.10 to 0.90 on DoorKey, 0.10 to 0.80 on KeyCorridor, and 0 to 0.50 on ObstructedMaze. Plain DQN did not catch up even with three times the budget. On PointMaze, SAC success rose from 0.17 to 1.0 on seven matched runs that reached the goal. The authors also name three limits: gaps in the graph, states that look alike, and lost detail in complex inputs.
