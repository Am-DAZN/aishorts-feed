Hugging Face Hub Now Payable Through Google Cloud Accounts

Hugging Face has announced that people can pay for its Hub using their Google Cloud account, based on the title of a blog post. The Hub is the site where developers share and download AI models and datasets. The source gives no further details, so terms, pricing and availability are not known. The change appears aimed at making billing simpler for teams that already use Google Cloud.
