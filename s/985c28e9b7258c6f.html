DR Pixel Lets AI Research Papers by Reading Page Images, Not Text

Most AI research agents turn scientific papers into plain text before reading them. That can lose tables, figures and equations. A new paper introduces DR Pixel, an open-source agent that searches and reasons over images of document pages directly. It works over 1.06 million pages. The team also built DRPixelBench, a test based on scientific papers. Even without special training, the image-based method beat text-based setups on models like GPT-5.4, Gemini-3.5-Flash and Qwen3.6-27B, and used fewer tool calls. Training added more gains: about 15 points in finding the right pages and over 10 points in final accuracy. The authors say converting pages to text first is unnecessary and worse.
