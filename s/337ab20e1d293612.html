Small 1B Model Limite Solves Hard Math Problems, Released Openly

Researchers have released Limite 1B - Violetto, a small AI model built for math reasoning. It has one billion parameters, the adjustable numbers a model learns. It was trained from scratch on 237 billion tokens, or small chunks of text, mostly about math. It can read up to 131k tokens at once. After training, the team used extra steps to sharpen its skills, including learning from larger models and reward-based training. The authors report 94.01% on AIME 2026 and 74.25% on BeyondAIME, two hard math tests. They say this puts it among models many times its size. It is not a general chatbot. It is meant to solve single math questions and to act as a fast helper inside bigger systems. The model, its checkpoints and a serving tool are free under the Apache 2.0 license.
