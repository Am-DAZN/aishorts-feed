Trident helps AI agents learn from experience while they work

Researchers have built Trident, a method that lets AI agents improve themselves while they are running, without retraining. The team says older methods have three problems. They find past notes by wording alone, they try near-identical options, and they rewrite a whole instruction set at once, which can erase useful parts. Trident reads memory with more care, picks varied candidates, and adds small edits in order instead of rewriting everything. It was tested on five benchmarks covering text games, web tasks and coding, using three open models. It beat all the baselines tested. Against the strongest one, APEX, it gained 30.7%, 30.8% and 38.3% on average across the three models. Tests also showed each of the three parts adds its own gains.
