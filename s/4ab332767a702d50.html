New Maze Test Shows Why AI Agents Fail, Not Just If

Researchers released an early preview of MultiNet v2.0-Gridworld, an interactive maze for testing AI models that see and read. Most benchmarks give one success score. That hides why an AI failed: it may misread the scene, not understand how a part works, use parts in the wrong order, or not recover from a mistake. In this maze, features like key-door pairs and switch-gate pairs can be added or removed one at a time. A search method finds the best possible solution for each maze, which sets its difficulty. Three vision-language models tried 50 mazes. They solved only 6 of 150 episodes. No model solved 45 of the mazes, and none solved any episode with a switch-gate.
