TETHER Uses Table of Contents to Map Long, Complex Documents

Researchers have built TETHER, a method that helps AI understand the structure of long documents like sustainability reports, product manuals and annual reports. Many tools guess structure from how text looks, which fails when designs are fancy. Asking an AI model to extract it directly can lead to made-up results in long files. TETHER instead reads the table of contents first and builds an outline from it, using an image-and-text AI model. Then it matches each block of content to that outline. It needs no extra training. In tests on documents in several languages, it beat other parsers and AI models. The authors also report it uses 90% fewer tokens, the chunks of text an AI processes, and runs much faster than end-to-end approaches.
