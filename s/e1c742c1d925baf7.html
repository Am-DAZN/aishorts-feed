Hugging Face Announces Project ProtoStar for AI-Ready Public Science

Hugging Face has announced Project ProtoStar, according to a post on its blog. The title says the goal is to make publicly funded science ready for AI. The post text was not available, so details such as partners, timelines, and how the project will work are unknown. The title suggests a push to prepare research paid for with public money so that AI systems can use it more easily.
