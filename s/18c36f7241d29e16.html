Ben Hylak jokes that every app should vanish behind an AI agent, except his

Simon Willison shared a short, tongue-in-cheek quote from Ben Hylak about the future of software. Hylak says every product he uses should be a "headless MCP" feeding into an AI agent. MCP, or Model Context Protocol, is a standard way for AI tools to connect to apps and data. "Headless" means the app has no screen of its own. He would never have to think about what runs underneath. Then comes the joke: his own product is the exception, where customers love the interface and love his team. The quote pokes fun at how builders want agents to replace other people's interfaces, while hoping their own stay essential.
