New survey maps how large language models turn questions into database queries

Text-to-SQL tools let people ask a database a question in plain English and get back a SQL query, the code databases understand. This new survey reviews how large language models have changed the field. It covers over 100 papers and technical resources from 2022 to 2026. The authors sort the methods into five groups: prompting, fine-tuning, agent-based systems, retrieval-based methods, and reinforcement learning. They also compare systems by whether they are one model or modular, how much training they need, and how they reason. Recent systems such as ReFoRCE, Agentar-Scale-SQL, Arctic-Text2SQL-R1 and XiYan-SQL have set new accuracy records on tests like Spider, Spider 2.0, and BIRD. The survey also looks at open problems, including working across different domains, efficiency, and real-world use. It also covers the role of the semantic layer and responsible AI practices, since these models increasingly touch sensitive data.
