NVIDIA launches Minerva to spot problems in giant AI data centers

NVIDIA has launched Minerva, a software platform that helps teams watch over AI factories, the huge data centers that train and run AI models. At this scale, one weak part, like an overheated GPU, can slow a whole job. Minerva gathers data from chips, networks, storage and running jobs. It builds a map of how everything connects and uses AI to spot odd behavior, predict failures and group many alerts into one issue with a likely cause. It also has a chat assistant that suggests fixes. IREN says it helps its team do more planned maintenance. Aolani, Bitdeer AI, IBM, Jio, Lambda, Quake AI and Tesla plan to use it. Dell, Lenovo and Supermicro will offer it.
