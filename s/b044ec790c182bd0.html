Specialty chemicals maker Lubrizol uses AI to help its scientists work faster

Lubrizol, a specialty chemicals company, is building a digital base of cloud computing and modern data tools for its research teams. Girish Rao, its vice president of IT, says the aim is to pair decades of lab data with the knowledge of chemists and engineers. Data scientists can model ideas before any physical experiment is run. Rao stresses that AI is not meant to replace scientific judgment. Scientists, data experts and IT staff work together in 'fusion teams', starting with small pilots to build trust. Digital models could also help test more formulas, including starch and corn-based materials, for greener products. Scaling a lab material into production remains hard.
