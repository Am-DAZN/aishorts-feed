Small 1B-parameter model beats giant AI systems at reading documents

VenusOCR2.0 is a compact AI model that reads documents, including text, tables, and math formulas. It has about 1 billion parameters, far fewer than many general-purpose models. On the OmniDocBench v1.6 test, it scored 96.45 overall, ahead of Gemini-3-Pro (92.91) and Qwen3-VL-235B (89.78). It also set a top score of 98.57 on formulas. The team trained it with a data engine that uses outside checks, not the model's own output, to supply reliable training labels. This helps avoid the model degrading by learning from its own mistakes. A small add-on module called PTPv2 drafts several words at once to speed up reading. On a single H20 GPU, it was 2.96 times faster for one request and up to 2.36 times faster for batches, with identical output to the normal method.
