CCDWiki Helps AI Therapy Chatbots Separate Hunches From Facts Over Time

Researchers introduced CCDWiki, an add-on tool for AI therapy chatbots that use Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT). Good CBT needs a clear map of how a person's situations, thoughts, feelings and actions connect. Current chatbots remember past talks, but they cannot tell a guess from a proven fact. So a tentative idea can get treated as true and skew later advice. CCDWiki stores each idea as an entry linked to evidence that supports or contradicts it. Over many sessions, well-backed entries grow stronger, unsure ones stay open, and disproved ones are dropped. The tool then turns this map into short guidance for the chatbot. The authors report big gains in both the quality of the case map and the quality of the advice, with no retraining of the chatbot itself.
