UltraTex Teaches AI Physics to Paint Realistic 3D Textures

Researchers built UltraTex, a system that creates detailed surface textures for 3D models from a single reference image. The hard part is that a photo mixes together the material, how shiny it is, and the lighting. Current tools make good color maps but often guess metal and roughness values wrongly, which hurts realistic relighting. UltraTex works in two steps. It first predicts color and surface direction, then predicts metal and roughness using that color, the image, and hints about what each part is made of. Those hints come from a vision-language model, an AI that links images and words. The team also trains it to understand how materials react to light. The authors report it beats open-source and commercial methods in visual quality, material accuracy, and physical realism.
