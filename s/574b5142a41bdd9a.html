Video Walks Through MOSS Image, a New Local AI Image Maker

A new video from AI Search looks at MOSS Image, an AI image generator that can run on your own computer. The host calls it the new top local option. The video covers its specs, demos, and a comparison with Krea 2. It then shows how to set it up in ComfyUI, a popular tool for running image models. Other parts explain layout control, adding LoRAs (small add-on files that change a model's style), and ways to make it run faster. It also shows how to edit images and train your own LoRAs. Links to the model files are listed in the description. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield.
