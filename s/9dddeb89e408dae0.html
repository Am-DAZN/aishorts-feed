DeepMind uses AI agent teams to improve quantum algorithms

Google DeepMind has shared work on finding better quantum algorithms with teams of AI agents. Quantum algorithms are step-by-step instructions for quantum computers, which work very differently from normal ones. The effort uses formal verification, a method that checks with mathematical proof that a result is correct. The goal is to improve algorithms for fusion, a possible clean energy source, and for chemistry. The source gives only a short description, so details on results are not included here. Pairing agents with proof checks could help make sure that new ideas are both useful and reliable.
