BlockLock makes AI model answers repeatable, even when other requests share the batch

Large language models can give slightly different answers to the same prompt, depending on which other requests are processed alongside it. This paper presents BlockLock, a method that removes that problem. It fixes the layout of the data that a protected request sees, so its calculations stay the same no matter what else is in the batch. Unlike other fixes, it needs no custom low-level code and only constrains the protected requests. The authors tested it on five models, including Qwen3, Llama-3.1 and Gemma-3, and on three kinds of NVIDIA GPUs. In their tests, the outputs matched exactly, bit for bit. At equal work, one mode cut latency by 53.5 to 55.0 percent and raised throughput by 115.2 to 122.2 percent compared with vLLM Batch-Invariant. This matters for anyone who needs reliable, repeatable AI results.
