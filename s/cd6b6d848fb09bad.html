NVIDIA Adds Quantum Processor Support to Slurm for Supercomputers

NVIDIA has expanded Slurm, a popular tool that schedules jobs on supercomputers, so it can manage quantum processors too. Nearly 30 supercomputing centers are deploying over 50 quantum processors, and many already use Slurm. Quantum hardware makers can now connect to every center through one simple file, instead of building new software each time. The update supports the QRMI and QDMI interfaces. Alice & Bob, IBM, IQM, Pasqal, Quantinuum and MQSC are adopting it. Scientists can run mixed quantum and classical programs, such as those built with NVIDIA CUDA-Q, while Slurm coordinates the computing they need. Slurm 26.11 is due later this month. NVIDIA also announced updates to CUDA-Q tools for error correction, algorithms and qubit control.
