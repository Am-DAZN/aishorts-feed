King's Trust and OpenAI team up on £1m AI skills drive

The King's Trust, a UK charity that supports young people, and OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, have launched a £1 million partnership. The goal is to help up to 25,000 young people across the UK learn practical AI skills they can use at work. The announcement gives few other details, such as how the training will be delivered. It matters because it aims to give young people, who may be at the start of their careers, a better chance of using AI tools in the jobs they seek.
