Sumitomo Life Rolls Out ChatGPT Enterprise, With Leaders Going First

Sumitomo Life, a Japanese insurer, now uses ChatGPT Enterprise, a business version of OpenAI's chatbot. Staff use it to research other insurers, prepare proposals, and write code for investment analysis. According to OpenAI's blog, executives began using the tool early on. That early use by leaders helped other teams start using it too. The story shows how company leaders can set an example when a firm brings in new AI tools.
