AutoCompress Shrinks AI Models to Fit Specific Hardware Limits

Researchers introduced AutoCompress, a tool that shrinks large language models so they meet real hardware limits on speed, throughput and memory. Older methods often aim for a set parameter count, meaning the model's size. But size alone does not predict how fast a model runs, since that also depends on the chip, the workload and the software serving it. AutoCompress builds those limits into its search for a better model design, adjusting both the overall shape and each layer. It beat the Minitron pruning method by 6.57, 2.35 and 0.57 accuracy points on RTX 5070, RTX 5080 and H100 chips. It also cut Nemotron 3.5 Super from 120B to 74B parameters to run on a single B200 chip, keeping 95% of its benchmark score.
