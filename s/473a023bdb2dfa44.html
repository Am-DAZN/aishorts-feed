New method helps AI agents reserve the right amount of computing power

AI agents built on large language models work in many steps. They call tools and keep going until a task is done. Nobody knows in advance how much computing power or network capacity a run will need. Reserve too little and the job can stall or miss its deadline. Reserve too much and costly capacity is wasted. This paper proposes a method called Conformal Reservation for Orchestration, or CRO. It studies a sliding window of past runs and uses a statistical tool called conformal prediction to set resource reservations before a job starts. It comes with a guarantee that the reservation covers real demand with a chosen probability, without assuming a particular data pattern. In simulations with mixed tasks, CRO was the only tested method to meet the target coverage at every risk level.
