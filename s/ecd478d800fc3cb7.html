AI Framework Screens 10,000 Research Papers in Four Minutes

Researchers built a system that uses large language models (AI trained on huge amounts of text) to sort through scientific papers. It screens records, pulls out key details from full articles, and sums up what the research says. The team tested 11 AI models on three materials science screening tasks. Seven scored above 0.90 on balanced accuracy, and the best reached 0.96 to 0.98. Screening 10,000 records took under four minutes, versus about 83 person-hours by hand. The system also mapped trends in machine learning for concrete research and found papers with silicate glass density data that normal searches miss. The authors say this could turn messy piles of papers into organized knowledge.
