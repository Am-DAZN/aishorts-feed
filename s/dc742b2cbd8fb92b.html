Samsung, Kakao Mobility and LG team up with NVIDIA on physical AI in Korea

NVIDIA is working with big Korean companies to bring physical AI, meaning AI that controls machines and works in the real world, into production. Samsung is using NVIDIA tools to speed up chip design and materials research. In one test, a simulation dropped from about 18 hours to 41 seconds, a roughly 1,600x speedup. Kakao Mobility plans to use NVIDIA's vehicle computing and safety technology to build a level 4 self-driving system, starting with Kia vehicles. Level 4 means the car drives itself in set areas. LG Electronics showed its CLOiD robot doing manufacturing tasks in a real factory, using NVIDIA robot models and a repeatable process to train and test robots.
