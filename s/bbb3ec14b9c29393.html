AI Search Reviews New Deepseek V4.1 Flash Model and DeepInfer Tool

The YouTube channel AI Search has posted a review of a new model from Deepseek, called Deepseek V4.1 Flash 1017. The video also covers DeepInfer, a separate project from Deepseek that is shared on GitHub. The model is listed on Hugging Face, a site where AI models are shared. The reviewer tries it on many tasks, including a math explainer, building an Unreal game, composing music, a Blender VR scene, identifying cancer, deep research and idea generation. The video also goes over the model's specs and benchmarks, which are scores from standard tests. The description gives no results, so how well the model performs is not stated here.
