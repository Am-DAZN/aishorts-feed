Reviso Teaches AI to Pick What Matters When Captioning Videos

AI models that write video captions often fixate on small details and miss the main events. A new system called Reviso tries to fix this. It first watches a quick, low-frame-rate preview of the video. Then it makes a plan: what the video is about, which parts matter most, and what to check closely. Following the plan, it goes back to chosen moments and uses tools to look more carefully, and it can change the plan along the way. Finally it combines the big picture with the close-up details into one caption. The team trained it with reinforcement learning, which rewards good captions and correct targets and penalizes wasteful tool use. On four captioning tests, Reviso beat the open-source baselines it was compared with. Its captions also helped other AI systems answer questions about videos more accurately.
