WeChat's WeFair Rewrites Video Searches to Match What Users Really Want

Short-video search often gets vague queries. Search systems rewrite them to guess what the user means, and they must do it fast. Past AI rewriters learned from what people watched next. That can push results toward catchy videos instead of what the user wanted, a problem called intent drift. WeFair is a new method for WeChat short-video search that trains on human feedback in three steps: learning from examples, learning preferences, then checking for exact matches with real user rewrites. The 2-billion-parameter model beat strong baselines in offline tests for accuracy and keeping the user's intent. In an online test, effective video views rose 2.593% and query reformulations fell 2.350%. WeFair is now live in WeChat search, which handles over 100 million queries a day.
