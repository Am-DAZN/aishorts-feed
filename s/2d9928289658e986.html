Law firm Ropes & Gray builds AI tool for deal document review with OpenAI

Law firm Ropes & Gray is working with OpenAI to develop AI for transactional diligence. This is the careful check of documents that lawyers do before a business deal goes forward. In the process described, the AI work produces grouped documents, summaries, and reports. Lawyers then review that material before they advise their clients. The source gives few other details, such as how well the tool works or when it will be used. The story shows how law firms are testing AI to help with large piles of paperwork while keeping human lawyers in charge of the final advice.
