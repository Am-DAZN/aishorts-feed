New Benchmark Tests Whether AI Can Design Its Own Lab Experiments

Researchers built TwinLab-Bench to test AI agents acting as scientists. Earlier tests mostly checked whether an AI could follow a set workflow or pick from a list of options. This one asks the AI to design its own experiments. It covers 52 tasks from physical science, life science and engineering labs. Each task has a digital twin of a real lab, run by another AI agent. The twin carries out the experiments and gives readings based on real lab records. The tested agent's report is scored against a hidden answer from those records. The twin almost removes readings that stray from real records and inconsistent answers to repeated requests. The tasks remain hard. The best model, Claude Fable 5.2, averaged only 45.0. The team also studied where it fails.
