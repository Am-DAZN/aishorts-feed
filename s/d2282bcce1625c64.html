OpenAI shares outside voices backing fair use for AI training

OpenAI has published excerpts from court briefs written by other groups about fair use. Fair use is a legal idea that lets people use copyrighted material in some cases without permission. The briefs come from creators, academics, accessibility advocates, and legal experts, among others. They argue that fair use supports AI training and helps innovation. The post matters because courts are deciding how copyright law applies to the data used to train AI systems. The source does not give details on specific cases or rulings.
