New AI Method Restores Blurry Faces in Video Using Speech Audio

AI-made videos often show people in detailed scenes, but their faces are tiny and blurry. Standard sharpening tools can add detail, but they may not make the lips and teeth match the words being spoken. A new paper presents TaoMate-FVSR, a system that uses the audio track to guide face restoration in video. It starts from the partly preserved blurry footage and uses reference images to keep the person's look steady while motion flows smoothly. It was trained to fix both degraded videos and badly dubbed ones. This lets it also help with dubbing when the audio is swapped. On talking-head tests, the authors say it scored best among compared methods for visual similarity and lip sync. Examples show sharper lip shapes, teeth, and facial texture in full-scene AI footage.
