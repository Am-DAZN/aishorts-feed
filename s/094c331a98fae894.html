Google DeepMind and UK government team up to speed up planning

Google DeepMind is working with the UK government on a project to cut the time it takes to assess planning decisions. Planning decisions are the approvals needed before things like new buildings can go ahead. The source gives few details about how the AI will be used or how much time it could save. The effort shows AI being tested on everyday government work, where slow paperwork can hold up projects.
