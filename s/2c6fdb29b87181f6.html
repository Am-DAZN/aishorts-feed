AI 'world model' predicts how older adults' health will change over time

Most health prediction tools judge risk at a single moment. This study built a model that follows how a person's health changes across years. It uses an approach called a world model, which can simulate future paths, including what might happen if someone changes their lifestyle. Researchers trained it on a large US study of older adults, then tested it on groups in England and China without retraining. It predicted death and worsening daily living ability well. For death, accuracy scores (AUROC, where 1.0 is perfect) were 0.89 in the first period for the US data, and about 0.80 to 0.88 in the English and Chinese groups. Lifestyle changes in the simulations shifted risks in ways that matched real-world patterns. The work offers a framework for long-term risk forecasting in aging.
