27B Language Model Rewrites Its Own Weights to Get More Accurate

Researchers report that a language model can edit its own weights, the numbers that store what it has learned, and improve itself. No training run is needed. A 27B-parameter model was given its weights, graded answers and the ability to run itself. It diagnosed its failures and made edits. Its own edit raised accuracy by 6.6 points on 490 unseen problems. Over eight sessions, accuracy only rose. The model also repaired a damaged copy of itself without seeing the original weights, lifting image benchmark accuracy from 7% to 70%. The skill appeared only at the largest size tested: the 2B model made no edit, and the 4B and 9B models broke themselves. The authors say improving a model may someday take a conversation rather than a training run.
