Medical AI Teams Reason Better When a 'Challenger' Spots Gaps Mid-Debate

Large language models do well on simple medical questions but struggle with harder ones. Teams of AI agents debating each other often fail to beat one strong model. The authors say the problem is that these systems pick experts and look up facts based only on the question, not on what goes wrong during reasoning. They add a Challenger that reads the discussion as it unfolds and flags missing facts or missing expertise. That triggers a targeted lookup or brings in a specialist mid-discussion. On 862 hard questions across nine medical datasets, looking up facts upfront helped little, while mid-discussion lookup did better. It improved accuracy on 8 of 9 datasets. Challenger-triggered specialists raised GPT-4.1-mini accuracy on MedQA from 46% to 54%. Combining both tools naively made results worse. The work shows that when and why outside help is added matters a lot.
