OpenAI studies how coding agents speed up AI research

OpenAI has published a post looking at how much coding agents help with AI research. These agents are AI tools that write and run code on their own. The company measures the speed-up they bring to research work. It also finds their performance is uneven, strong on some tasks and weaker on others, which it calls a jagged frontier. The post also asks what this means for recursive self-improvement, the idea that AI could help build better AI, which could then help build better AI again. The source gives only a short description, so details of the measurements are not included here.
