Mathematician's Proposed Proof of Generalized Riemann Hypothesis Shared Early, Without AI

Ravi Vakil says Alessandro Masullo has a proposed proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, a major open problem in mathematics. Brian Conrad has studied it closely for months and given detailed feedback, which is being added. So far, Vakil says, it looks good. He stresses that the work is all human, with no AI. The plan was to bring Masullo to Stanford for a year to polish the writing and share the news after checking with enough people. Now it seems best to release the current draft. The proof has not yet been fully confirmed.
