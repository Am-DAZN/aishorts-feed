VA-TDM Method Speeds Up Image and Video AI With Just Four Steps

Diffusion models make images and videos by cleaning up noise over many steps. That is slow. Researchers want smaller models that need only a few steps, but quality often drops. This paper introduces Velocity Adversarial Distillation, or VAD. A learned critic compares a fast student model with the slow teacher model and turns the differences into feedback. The authors say VAD brings several existing training methods under one view. They then build VA-TDM, which gives feedback at partial denoising stages along the student's path. They report top four-step results for text-to-image and text-to-video across several model types, including the 33B MiniMax-H3. In human preference tests, VA-TDM-H3 beat the few-step Fast-H3 and came closer to the original many-step H3.
