Video Points to 'Weight Space Programming' Work on Opening AI's Black Box

Two Minute Papers has posted a video titled "OpenAI's Astra Just Opened The Black Box Of AI." The black box problem means we often cannot see why an AI model gives the answers it does. The video description links to a project called Weight Space Programming, published as a blog post online. Weights are the numbers inside an AI model that shape how it behaves. The description does not explain what the work found or how Astra is involved, so the details are in the video and the linked post. The page also thanks its supporters and promotes Lambda's GPU cloud service.
