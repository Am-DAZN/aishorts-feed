Hebbia reports better financial research results with GPT-6 Astra

Hebbia, a company that builds tools for financial research, tested GPT-6 Astra, a new model from OpenAI, before its public release. In its early evaluations, Hebbia found improvements in three areas. The model covered more sources, picked more relevant citations (the references that back up a claim), and produced better-presented results. The source does not give specific numbers. Still, the results suggest the model could help analysts who need thorough, well-sourced answers when researching financial questions.
