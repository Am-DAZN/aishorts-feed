DaisyRose-27B Targets Efficient Post-Training for AI Models

Hugging Face's blog has published a post titled "DaisyRose-27B: Pushing the Frontier of Post-Training Efficiency." The title suggests a model with 27 billion parameters, which are the settings an AI learns during training. It also points to post-training, the stage after a model's first big training run, when it is tuned to follow instructions and behave better. The post claims a focus on doing this step more efficiently. No further details were available, so the creators, methods and results are unknown.
