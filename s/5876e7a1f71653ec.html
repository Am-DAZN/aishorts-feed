The Trinity: AI Agents That Build Their Own Tests and Improve Themselves

Researchers have proposed The Trinity, a framework that helps AI agents that handle text, images and other inputs improve themselves. Two problems slow this work today: building varied test environments is costly, and people must tune the agent's supporting setup, called a Harness, by hand. The Trinity uses three roles: Creator, Explorer and Evolver. The Evolver groups tasks by the skills they need, not by their surface type. The Creator then builds simple, checkable simulated worlds to practice each skill. The Explorer upgrades reusable skill modules. A router picks the right setup for each new task. The authors report average gains of 34 points across 28 training benchmarks and 26 points across 18 unseen ones, including two real-world robot-style tasks.
