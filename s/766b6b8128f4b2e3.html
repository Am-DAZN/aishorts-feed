AI Models Struggle to Recognise Slowed-Down Melodies, Showing Weak Sense of Time

Researchers tested how well 21 multimodal AI models, which handle sound as well as text, sense how long things last. They played well-known melodies at slower and slower speeds and checked when each model could no longer name the tune. Humans were the baseline. Most models failed to recognise the melodies unless they were shown labelled examples first. With examples, seven models did well, and most peaked within the range of typical human performance. But the results were fragile. The best speed changed with the speed of the examples, and song titles biased the answers. The authors conclude that in humans this skill belongs to the listener, while in current models it depends largely on the prompt.
