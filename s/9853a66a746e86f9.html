EmoHarness helps small AI models read emotion by adding speech and tone clues

Small AI models that handle video, text and sound often get emotions wrong when words, voice and visuals disagree. EmoHarness wraps such a model in a "harness": the prompts, settings and checking code around it. A stronger AI, called the Builder, studies the small model's mistakes and edits the harness. On 5,507 test items, accuracy at recognizing emotions rose by 7.6 to 13.4 points over a video-only approach. Most of the gain came from adding transcripts and descriptions of vocal tone. The automatic edits added at most 0.95 points, and none held up under statistical correction. So the main benefit is supplying and organizing audio clues.
