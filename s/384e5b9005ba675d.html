New Benchmark Tests Whether AI Can Edit Building Models From Text Instructions

Researchers have built BIBIMBAP, a test for AI language models that work with Building Information Modeling (BIM). BIM files are digital models of buildings, often stored in a format called IFC. AI models can already look up information in these files, but there was no way to measure how well they can change them. The benchmark has 100 tasks. They cover creating, reading, updating and deleting data, across areas like space, shape, and numbers. Each task has a plain-language prompt, a building model, expected results, and test scripts that grade answers automatically. In baseline tests, the best score was 50.2%. Models often broke rules the building data had to follow and struggled with spatial reasoning. Some right answers came from faulty reasoning.
