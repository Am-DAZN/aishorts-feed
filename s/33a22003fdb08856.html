Study: AI Feature Explanations Can Be Scored Without Reading Them

Researchers looked at how AI models are tested when they explain what a part of another AI does. These parts are called sparse-autoencoder features. The test checks if a model can guess where a feature fires in text. The study found the test data has shortcuts. On 500 GPT-2 small features, a simple rule that says yes when the middle word is marked scored 0.979 balanced accuracy. The stored AI scorers got 0.838. A similar shortcut beat GPT-4's published simulator on OpenAI's benchmark. The team built new text windows that remove these cues. On them, scorers that read the explanation still did well, and shuffled explanations did not. The authors say scores should be reported next to the best rule that ignores the explanation.
