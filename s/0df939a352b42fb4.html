Researchers Build a Case-Based System to Help Advise College Students

This thesis looks at using case-based reasoning, a method where a computer solves new problems by looking at similar past cases, to help with academic advising. Advising is an important part of university life, but it takes a lot of time, and many schools want to improve it. The author notes that expert systems of this kind have worked in many fields but have not yet been successfully used for advising. The work covers the design, building, and testing of a prototype recommender system. It is meant to support the usual advising process. The author suggests it could one day replace human advisors if its full potential is reached.
