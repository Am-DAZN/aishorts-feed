AI is straining company networks, and a new report urges ongoing upgrades

A report from MIT Technology Review's custom content arm says AI is now the biggest source of demand on company networks. Tasks done by AI agents are estimated to create 450% more network traffic than the same tasks done by people. Only about half of enterprises think their networks can handle AI's speed and bandwidth needs. The report says running AI models for real-time use needs fast, nearby infrastructure, so some firms may move work back on premises. It argues network upgrades should be an ongoing strategy, not a one-off project. It also points to network-as-a-service and AI-based monitoring as ways to cut complexity. Laws that keep data inside national borders may add costs for global firms. The content was produced by Insights, not the editorial staff.
