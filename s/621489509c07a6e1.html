Simulated Users Let Teams Test Product Ideas Before Launch

Testing products with real people is slow. Finding the right participants and repeating studies as designs change holds teams back. This paper presents a framework that uses AI agents playing detailed user personas, meaning made-up profiles of people, to run controlled experiments. The agents can take part in surveys, websites, chatbots and phone or computer apps. The system logs every trial and produces statistical reports. The authors tested it in twelve cases with industry partners across five fields. Different AI models mostly reached the same main conclusions. But changing or removing persona details altered the results, so detailed personas matter. The authors say the results are directional evidence, not final answers. They can help teams shape questions, guide design choices and decide what to study next.
