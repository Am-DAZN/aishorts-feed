New Benchmark Finds Top Fast-Answer AI Models Are Not the Most Reliable

Researchers built SO-Arena, a test for "System One" models. These are AI models that make quick decisions without writing out long step-by-step reasoning. The test covers 11 domains and five groups of skills. It checks answers against verified sources, and some tasks have models take actions in sandboxes, which are safe practice environments. It also measures reliability: does a model keep its answer when the answer choices change, and does it notice when no choice is correct? The team tested 21 models. Gains from bigger models or extra reasoning varied a lot by domain. The best-performing model ranked only seventh in reliability. The authors say accuracy alone is not enough to judge these models.
