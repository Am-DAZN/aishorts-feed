Researchers Peek Inside AI Models as They Reason Through Games

AI agents often "think out loud" by writing step-by-step reasoning before they act. This is called chain-of-thought. But it is not clear how that written trace relates to what the model is actually computing inside. Researchers studied this in repeated games where players lack full information. Using tools that read a model's internal activity, they found the model builds a picture of the game and keeps revising it as it reasons. If the model commits too early to a wrong belief, it rarely recovers. The mistake shows up both in the written reasoning and in the model's internal signals. The team says this could help diagnose strategic errors as more AI agents work together. They also released an interactive visualizer with sample traces.
