LVMH media arm partners with OpenAI to bring its publications to ChatGPT

LVMH's Media Division and OpenAI have announced a partnership. The goal is to support journalism with AI. It also aims to make LVMH's publications easier to reach through ChatGPT, OpenAI's chatbot. The announcement is short and gives no details on which publications are involved, how the tie-up will work, or when it will start. LVMH is a large luxury group, and its media division runs news and lifestyle titles. The deal is another sign of news publishers working with AI companies to reach readers in new ways.
