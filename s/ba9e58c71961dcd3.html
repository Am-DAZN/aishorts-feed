SwarmEvolve uses swarms of small AI agents to find better solutions faster

Researchers built SwarmEvolve, a system that speeds up AI-guided search for better programs on math and science problems. Older methods make the AI write long text explanations every round, which is slow. SwarmEvolve instead runs hundreds of low-cost agents at once. They think in the model's internal numbers, not in words, and share their memory (called KV caches) with each other. A filter keeps the most different ideas, and a summarizer agent blends them. On 21 problems, it got the highest average score. It matched in 3 to 8 minutes, at the median, what other top systems reached in 60. With the small Qwen3 8B model, it also improved on some solutions AlphaEvolve first found using frontier models.
