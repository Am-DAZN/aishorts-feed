LoopRes helps existing language models get smarter by reusing their own layers

Researchers have proposed LoopRes, a method that makes pretrained language models run some of their layers in loops. Looping adds computing power without adding new parameters, the numbers a model learns. Past attempts needed training from scratch or gave only small gains. LoopRes lets each looped layer look back at the outputs of recent loops. It also gives each loop a learned tag so the model can tell them apart. Tests covered 6 models from 3 families and 9 benchmarks. At twice the compute, Qwen3-1.7B-Base improved 29.1% over standard fine-tuning, more than double the gain of the best earlier method. Gains also held on models up to 30B. The approach scaled better than fine-tuning as compute grew, and needed less compute on long outputs.
