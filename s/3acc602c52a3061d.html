New 'Predicate Neuron' Adds Logic Reasoning to Standard Neural Networks

Researchers have built a new neural network part called the Native Predicate Neuron. It lets ordinary AI models use symbolic reasoning, which means following logic rules such as 'if this, then that'. Older logic-based methods are rigid and hard to combine with deep learning. This neuron learns to switch between logic operations using just one trainable setting. During training it applies strict logic, yet it still lets the network learn through standard methods. The team tested it in several network types, including image and text models, on tests like MNIST and Wikitext. They report faster learning, better accuracy and lower error overall. Gains were strongest on tasks with steadily rising or falling patterns, or sudden jumps that normal networks handle poorly. The neuron works with Keras and PyTorch, two popular AI tools.
