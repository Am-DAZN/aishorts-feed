Actionwave Benchmark Tests AI on Bedside Heartbeat and Vital-Sign Signals

Hospitals record continuous waveforms, such as heart and blood pressure signals, that can show fast changes in a patient's condition. Researchers built Actionwave, a test suite for AI models that predict clinical events from these signals. It covers intensive care, operating rooms and emergency departments. It checks how results change with signal length, how much warning time a prediction gives, and which inputs are available. The team compared many model designs and pretrained signal models under set size and compute limits. The tests use 20 tasks, 1.4 million data windows and over 87,000 model training runs. Rules include splitting data by patient and blocking any future information from leaking in. The results point to the best model choices for each task. They also show why fair comparisons need matched patient groups, single-signal controls and clear timing.
