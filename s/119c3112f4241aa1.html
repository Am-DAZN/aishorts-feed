CARE-OPD trains AI agents faster by smartly reusing past task runs

Researchers have proposed CARE-OPD, a way to train AI agents that handle long, multi-step tasks. In on-policy distillation, a smaller student model learns from a stronger teacher, but it needs fresh runs in the environment for every update, which is slow and costly. Replaying stored histories is cheaper, but the team found two problems. Teacher-only runs miss the mistakes a student makes, and fixed schedules can't spot key decisions at any depth. CARE-OPD adds student-seeded teacher completions, a confidence filter to drop unreliable updates, and a replay step that picks important turns based on what happened later. On software engineering and deep research tasks, it beat online training and earlier replay methods. On SWE-bench Verified, it reached 59.5% task success, cut training time by 45%, and cut environment actions by 44% compared with asynchronous online training.
