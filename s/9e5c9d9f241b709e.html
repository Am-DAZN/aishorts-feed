Microsoft Research Focus Recaps Work on AI Agents, Weather and Proteins

Microsoft Research has released Edition #97 of its Research Focus series, now also on YouTube and podcast platforms. Host Sowbhagya Suresh covers recent projects from across the company. One is ForkPilot, a framework that helps AI agents decide when to go back and try a different approach. In tests on six benchmarks, it matched the best existing methods while using up to 59% less AI processing. Another is CABRA, which builds coding challenges from scratch to show why coding agents fail. The edition also covers how people use AI at work, a tool for teachers, better weather forecasts weeks ahead, and a public preview of BioEmu2.
