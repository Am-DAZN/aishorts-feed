Generated Images Help AI Spot Olive Tree Diseases More Accurately

Researchers studied how to improve AI models that spot plant diseases from photos. These models often struggle when there are too few images, or when some diseases have far fewer photos than others. The team created extra images using several methods. These included simple edits like flipping photos, mixing images together (CutMix and MixUp), and making fake but realistic pictures with a type of AI called a DCGAN. They then tested three ways to use the new data: balancing the classes, adding more samples per class, or doing both. They trained a model called EfficientV2B0 on olive tree images. Accuracy improved substantially. The authors say varied data and balanced classes both matter, and the approach could help detect plant diseases and support precision farming.
