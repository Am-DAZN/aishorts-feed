Berkeley AI Research Lists Its Work at Robot Learning Conference in Austin

The Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) runs November 9–12, 2026, in Austin, Texas. Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) has posted its schedule. Faculty, postdocs and students will present 33 main-conference papers and 4 late-breaking posters. Ten of the papers are talks: 3 orals and 7 spotlights. Professor Negar Mehr will give an Early Career Keynote on how robots can learn to interact and coordinate with others. Some papers are co-written with other universities and companies. One paper, MEM, looks at giving robot models a multi-scale memory. The late-breaking list may grow as more posters are confirmed.
