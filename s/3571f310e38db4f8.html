Training AI Agents to Write Better Notes for Their Future Selves

Most AI agents are trained with reinforcement learning, where the model only gets a single score as feedback. That throws away useful details from the task, such as error logs. Some newer methods let agents reflect and pass notes to later attempts, but how those notes are written is not learned. This paper proposes a method that trains one model to act, reflect on what happened, and write a task-specific playbook. The playbook is judged by how much it helps later attempts. Across agentic benchmarks, the method scored higher on held-out tests than baseline methods. The authors say agent training should improve not just the current actions, but also the experience the model writes for future attempts.
