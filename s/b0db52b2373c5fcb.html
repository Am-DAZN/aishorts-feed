Simple 12-Parameter Model Makes Graph AI Predictions More Trustworthy and Explainable

Researchers have proposed a method called Interpretable Structural Temperature Scaling, or ISTS. It helps graph neural networks, which are AI models that work on connected data, judge how sure they should be about each answer. Older tools use extra neural networks that are hard to inspect. ISTS instead uses a simple linear model with just twelve settings. It adjusts each node's confidence using named factors, such as its confidence, its neighbours and how close it is to labelled nodes. So every adjustment can be traced back. On seven graphs, it had the best average rank on two accuracy-of-confidence measures and cut one error score by 1.8% compared with standard temperature scaling. It also ranked first on four new graphs. It works from about 100 calibration nodes and also improved results on point-cloud segmentation.
