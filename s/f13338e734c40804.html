AuditFL Uses Blockchain Contracts to Keep Federated Learning Honest

Federated learning trains an AI model across many devices without moving their raw data. But most setups still trust one central coordinator to run everything, including payments. AuditFL is a new system that adds a blockchain-based layer to make that coordinator's decisions open to checking. Training stays off the blockchain. Only file identifiers and deposit or reward records go on it. Clients put down deposits to join a round and later claim refunds or rewards. The coordinator still exists, but its round decisions can be traced and replayed. In tests on the MNIST handwriting dataset with up to 200 clients, accuracy matched standard federated learning. At 200 clients, the system used 5,026 transactions. Tests with dropped clients, bad updates and missing files still finished rounds, with accuracy changing by only a few tenths of a percentage point.
