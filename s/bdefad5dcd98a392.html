Trump's order to rename AI 'Super Intelligence' tests who will follow

At the end of September, President Trump signed an order telling government agencies to call artificial intelligence "Super Intelligence," or "SI." MIT Technology Review says the change may work as a political loyalty test, like earlier renamings such as the Gulf of America. Some agencies have switched quietly, and ICE now asks for "SI-powered" surveillance tools. But slips are common. The Pentagon, the FTC and Health and Human Services have used other wording, and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said "AI" in an interview. On October 27, Trump criticized the Wall Street Journal for not using the new term. He hinted at a rule that could ban "artificial intelligence" from government documents.
