Heimstaden turns to OpenAI tools to run its housing business

Heimstaden, a company that manages residential property across Europe, is using OpenAI's ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex. ChatGPT Enterprise is a business version of the popular chatbot. Codex is an OpenAI tool that helps write computer code. According to OpenAI's blog, Heimstaden is using them to change how it manages properties, cut costs, and give its employees more power to do their work. The post is a customer story, so it shows OpenAI's view of the results. It offers a look at how a large landlord is bringing AI into everyday work.
