Op-ed: AI Safety Focus Misses Everyday Harms to Young People

An opinion piece in MIT Technology Review argues that tech policy looks only at the worst-case harms of a product. Examples include a chatbot promising not to encourage suicide, or a platform limiting teen access to fentanyl. The author says these steps matter but set a low bar. Products can pass them and still leave young people hooked on AI companions or endless feeds. The piece says companies like these narrow goals because they cut lawsuits and ease pressure for tougher rules. It says Google backed state chatbot bills with broad exemptions, while stronger bills on emotional dependence failed. The author urges policymakers to protect how people think, learn and connect, not only to prevent disasters.
