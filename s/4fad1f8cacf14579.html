New Training Method Teaches Robot AI Models to Stop Safely Mid-Task

Vision-language-action (VLA) models let robots act on what they see and what people tell them. Researchers found a safety gap. A robot trained to refuse a banned task from the start can still finish it if it takes over halfway through. Even when it does not finish, it may drop the object it is holding. The team built a method called SAFI. It trains the robot to freeze its arm and keep a steady grip during banned tasks, while still learning its allowed tasks. Tested with OpenVLA-OFT on LIBERO and BadLIBERO tasks, SAFI cut banned-task success after takeover to 1.3%. There were no recorded physical incidents, and the robot kept high success on its allowed tasks.
