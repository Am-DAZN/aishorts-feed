History Forcing helps AI video models stay consistent over long clips

Researchers have introduced History Forcing, a training method for AI models that generate video one chunk at a time. These models often lose track of things over long clips, especially when a person or scene leaves the frame and later returns. The method teaches a small "student" model using a stronger "teacher" model. It compares the teacher's predictions with and without past footage, then uses the difference to correct what the student learns. The past footage can come from earlier chunks with less error or from a fixed-view teacher clip that keeps the scene in sight. The team says it leads among 1.3B-size models, scoring 85.07 on VBench for 15-second videos and 79.82 on VBench-Long for 60-second ones. Scene evolution and object consistency also improved when the camera returned to a spot.
