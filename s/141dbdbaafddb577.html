IsoAlign Teaches DNA Models to Link Genes With Their Protein Variants

Genomic foundation models are AI systems that learn patterns from DNA. The researchers say current training methods ignore alternative splicing, where one gene can produce several versions of a protein. These models also trail specialized tools. The team built IsoAlign to fix this. It matches DNA transcripts with the protein versions they produce. It uses two training setups. One predicts a protein's representation from DNA. The other learns how different protein versions of the same gene relate, by guessing masked parts. The authors tested the DNA representations on predicting harmful mutations, fitness, gene expression, and transcription factor binding. They report that this approach clearly improves results. They see it as a promising way to train future genomic models.
