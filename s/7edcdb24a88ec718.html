Microsoft Research manager Mike Walker on the hacker mindset and learning the rules

A Microsoft Research podcast talks with manager Mike Walker about his career in hacking and cyber defense. He leads projects that mix AI, cybersecurity and large computer systems. Before Microsoft, he led DARPA's Cyber Grand Challenge, a program that showed AI systems could find and fix software flaws on their own. It marked its 10th anniversary in 2026. Walker says you must understand rules to do what you want, and that rules are a social idea. In security, each request made of users spends their trust, and wasting it makes security fail. He also discusses teamwork in competitive hacking, lessons on leadership, and whether networked systems are safer than a decade ago.
