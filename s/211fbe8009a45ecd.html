Hugging Face Introduces Harness Adapters to Fit Models to Your Setup

Hugging Face has announced something called harness adapters. The title says they let you adapt your favorite AI model to your harness. A harness is the software setup that runs and tests a model. The goal, according to the title, is better performance and lower cost. The full article text was not available, so details on how the adapters work, who can use them, and how much they help are not known yet.
