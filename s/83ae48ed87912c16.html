EmoReward Teaches Video AI to Show Emotion by Learning Which Scores to Trust

Researchers built EmoReward, a training method for AI video generators that aims to make emotions in videos more convincing. Judging emotion is hard because there is no single right answer. Fixed scoring rules for image quality, motion and text match often miss emotional intent, and models can game them. EmoReward plans what to look for from the prompt, such as facial and body cues and color. It then scores each video with several signals and adjusts how much each one counts. After a training run, it measures which signals were reliable and uses that to set weights for the next run. The reward models themselves are not changed. Tests on HunyuanVideo 1.5 showed gains across several reinforcement learning methods. Transfer tests on SD 3.5 and FLUX also supported the approach.
