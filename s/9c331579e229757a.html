ChatGPT Enterprise Now Sold Through AWS Marketplace

OpenAI says ChatGPT Enterprise is now available in AWS Marketplace, the online store where companies buy software through Amazon Web Services. This means organizations can buy it using the purchasing and billing setup they already have with AWS. The goal is to make buying simpler. Companies may not need to set up a separate buying process just for ChatGPT Enterprise. The announcement gives few other details.
