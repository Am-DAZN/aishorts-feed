Microsoft podcast: researcher Chris White on tech built for real-world good

In a Microsoft Research Podcast episode, program manager Weishung Liu talks with Chris White, who leads Microsoft Research's Catalyst Lab. White began as a postdoc at DARPA, the US defense research agency. In 2010 he worked on whether a new way of analyzing data could protect troops in Afghanistan. DARPA then asked him to prove its value on the ground, so he left academic research to help lead advanced tech work there. Later, he helped build dark-web search tools used to fight human trafficking. He won a 2016 Presidential Award for this work. The talk covers how he picks problems to solve and how he stays hopeful after seeing the worst of human behavior.
