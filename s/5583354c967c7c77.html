New AI Method Finds Exact Social Media Evidence of Depression Symptoms

Researchers built a system called SREE to help estimate how someone would answer a depression questionnaire, using their social media history. Older methods pick posts that sound similar to a symptom. These can be misleading, for example when a post talks about another person's struggles. SREE instead pulls out short pieces of text where the user speaks about themselves and about one specific symptom. It then picks a representative set of these pieces and bases its answers only on them. In tests, it improved post-level precision by 70% and F1 (a combined accuracy score) by 59% over top retrieval methods, while using at least 30% fewer posts. It also had the highest exact item-level agreement on three eRisk datasets. The work shows that finding clear, personal evidence matters before inferring mental health answers.
