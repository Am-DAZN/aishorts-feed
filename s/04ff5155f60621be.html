Nilay Patel: AI Products Aren't Good Enough to Justify Their Costs

On The Vergecast, tech journalist Nilay Patel argued that AI products are not good enough to win people over given what they cost. He pointed to noisy data centers near homes, AI overviews and low-quality fake media, which he called slop. He said AI agents, tools meant to act for users, aren't real yet or aren't used as people want. He noted that companies spend more than they earn on these products. He also tied the costs to politics, citing tech CEOs getting close to the White House. He said the AI industry will not escape this backlash until it makes better products that clearly improve people's lives without needing an ad campaign.
