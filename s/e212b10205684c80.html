HERMES AI Reads Tumor Slides and Shows Checkable Reasoning Steps

Doctors study the tumor immune microenvironment, meaning the immune cells around a tumor, using two kinds of lab images. Most pathology AI reads only one kind, and its reasoning is rarely checked. Researchers built HERMES, a 4-billion-parameter AI model that reads both image types together and follows clinical guideline steps. It is trained with a reward that checks every number it states against cell-level ground truth from the second image type. On four colorectal cancer tasks, it reached 84.5% average accuracy, versus 74.7% for the best of twelve other models. It also scored higher on a different imaging dataset and on head and neck cancer, which it never saw in training. This matters because doctors can inspect the steps behind an answer, not just the final result.
