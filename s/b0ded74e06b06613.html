OpenAI Shares Work on Computer-Use Agents for Healthcare Teams

OpenAI has published a blog post titled "Advancing computer-use agents for healthcare teams." The title suggests the company is working on AI agents that can use a computer the way a person does, such as clicking, typing and moving between programs, to help people who work in healthcare. The full text of the post was not available, so details on what was released, how it works, or who is using it are not known. Still, the topic points to growing interest in using AI to handle routine computer tasks in medical settings.
