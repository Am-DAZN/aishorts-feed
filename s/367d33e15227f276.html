Researchers Say Tiny-Change Attack Tests Give a False Sense of AI Security

A new paper argues that AI security research focuses too much on one narrow kind of attack. This attack makes tiny changes to an input, too small for people to notice, that trick a model. Researchers measure these changes with math rules called lp norms. The authors say this focus is a trap. It gives defenders a distorted picture of real risk and pushes them to build math-based robustness instead of overall system security. It also lets attackers use other paths that these tests never cover. The authors warn that this focus may even help cause harm, because it creates a false sense of safety against the wrong threats.
