Survey Calls for One Unified Way to Represent Text, Images and More

Embedding models turn data like text or images into numbers that computers can compare. They power search engines and recommendation systems. A new position paper says research on them is split by data type, with each group working mostly alone. The authors argue these fields are actually moving toward the same ideas, driven by contrastive learning (training a model to pull similar items together), Transformer designs, and a shift in the level of detail. They propose three goals of rising difficulty: coverage, comparability, and compositionality. They describe three routes to unify the field: transformation, alignment, and joint embedding, which they advocate. The paper claims to be the first unified look across all data types.
