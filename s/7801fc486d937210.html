Spatial Harness lets AI check spatial answers inside a physics engine

Vision-language models, which understand both images and text, often struggle with spatial questions, such as how a scene looks from another angle. They usually work it out in their heads, so one misread scene or bad mental step leads to a wrong answer. Spatial Harness takes a different route. A Perceiver places objects and cameras in a shared map of the scene. That scene is then built in a physics engine, where a Reasoner moves the viewpoint and uses measurements and rendered views to decide. On the MindCube test, Qwen3.5-27B rose from 52.70% to 71.14%, beating pySpatial by 7.52 points. With the same given object and camera positions, it scored 98.25% against 71.81% for model-internal reasoning. The code is public.
