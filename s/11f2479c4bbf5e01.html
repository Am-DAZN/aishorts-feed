Same AI Negotiator, Different Language, Different Deal Outcomes

Researchers tested whether telling an AI to act a certain way, such as "headstrong," works the same in every language. They had AI buyers and sellers haggle over 12 Craigslist-style scenarios in English and Korean, running 432 attempted negotiations. Korean talks ended in a deal far more often than English ones (77.3% vs. 52.5%). Sellers gave bigger concessions in Korean, and agreed prices were lower. A second AI model showed a similar but smaller gap. The authors point to politeness: Korean dialogue softened rejections and counter-offers more, especially from sellers. This is a small pilot study. Still, it shows that changing only the language can change how an AI behaves, not just how it sounds.
