WorldBridge builds consistent 3D worlds from scattered photos that barely overlap

Researchers have introduced WorldBridge, a method that creates 3D worlds you can explore from a few scattered images. The photos may share little or no overlap, which has been hard for earlier tools. Methods that rely on geometry struggle here, while image and video generators can fill in unseen areas but often get the shapes wrong from one view to the next. WorldBridge combines all the photos into one shared picture of the scene, which guides both shape and look. A special attention step helps it follow reference images along the camera path and use others outside it. A test-time step also checks results by reprojecting them to stay true to the references. The team also built a new benchmark for scattered, low-overlap inputs. They report better results than current top methods.
