OpenAI teams up with NATO and allies on AI cyber defense

OpenAI says it is working with NATO and cyber agencies from allied countries. The goal is to help trusted defenders use AI to protect essential systems. According to the company, the work covers finding weak spots in software, known as vulnerabilities, and testing fixes before attackers can exploit them. The focus is on giving this help to trusted defenders, not to the general public. The source gives few other details, such as which tools or agencies are involved. It matters because cyber attacks can hit services people rely on, and defenders often have limited time and staff to find and fix problems.
