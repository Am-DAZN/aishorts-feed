Trimming Training Captions Helps AI Rewrite Image Prompts Better

Prompt enhancement tools turn short user requests into long, detailed descriptions so image generators make better pictures. These tools are usually trained on pairs of short prompts and dense image captions. Researchers found that captions which boost image quality a lot do not train better enhancers than captions with small gains. They argue dense captions force models to copy small details that have little to do with what the user asked. Their method, called Prune to Expand (P2E), removes redundant sentences from captions, within a limit per example, while keeping the user's stated needs and key visual structure. In tests with identical training settings, P2E gave results that matched human preferences better and looked better than standard training. It worked across many image types and image generators.
