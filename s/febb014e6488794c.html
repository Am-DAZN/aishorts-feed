Drex: Open Models That Pick Answers Fast Without Writing Out Reasoning

Researchers released Drex, four open-source "decision models" with 8B to 35B parameters. Instead of writing long reasoning step by step, they take a question and a list of options and return the probability of each option. This avoids the inconsistent answers, output parsing, cost and delay that come with long chain-of-thought text. The top model, Max, scored 67.52 on the public Decision Index 0.4.1 suite, versus 60.70 for the next decision model, Jev 1.14.0. On Decision Bench v4, it is statistically tied with d3-turbo and GPT-6 Sol, and trails Claude Opus 5.5 by 1.6 points. Max answers a typical request in 183 milliseconds, against about 2.0 seconds for GPT-6 Sol and 3.5 seconds for Claude Opus 5.5. Its answers also stay stable when the option order changes. All four models are open source.
