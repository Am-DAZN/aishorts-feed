Innata-0 Pairs a Robot Model and Agent Built Together

Researchers introduced Innata-0, a system meant to help robots act reliably in the messy, changing real world. The real world is open-ended and never fully visible, and single AI models often struggle with long tasks. Simply bolting a model onto an agent, a program that plans and manages steps, can make planning and physical control disagree. So the team designed both parts together. The model, Innata-0-WAM, combines understanding, predicting what happens next, and generating actions. The agent, Innata-0-Agent, handles long tasks using memory, checks, and recovery from mistakes. A shared training method links the two and reuses validated experiences to improve the model later. The authors report strong results in understanding, world prediction, and robot manipulation. They call it a first step toward physical intelligence.
