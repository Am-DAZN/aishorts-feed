Free trial abuse floods AI product ChatPRD, forcing founder to end trials

Claire Vo runs ChatPRD, an AI tool that helps product managers turn an idea into a spec. In a new YouTube video, she describes a wave of "token fraud" from people abusing her free trial. Tokens are the units AI companies use to measure and charge for usage. Some users just wanted free AI. Others resold tokens or ran businesses on top of her agent. She says one group looped through free trials and promo codes to build Roblox and Fortnite maps. Many prompts asked for SVG drawings of pelicans riding bicycles, which may be a way to guess which AI model sits underneath. Vo is now ending free trials entirely.
