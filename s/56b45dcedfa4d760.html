GigaSpeech 3 Gives Arabic Dialects a 39,000-Hour Speech Dataset

Arabic speech tools struggle with regional dialects because there are few accurately transcribed recordings, even though plenty of audio exists online. Researchers built GigaSpeech 3, a dataset of 39,000 hours covering Algerian, Egyptian, Iraqi, Saudi and Syrian Arabic. They started with just 60 hours of hand-written transcripts per dialect. Then they trained models to label web audio automatically, so the transcripts are imperfect but numerous. Models trained on it reached a 23.86% word error rate on average, 16.6% lower than the best outside system covering all five dialects. A single multi-dialect model scored 25.51% and also handles Modern Standard Arabic. The team has released the dataset and training recipes for others to use.
