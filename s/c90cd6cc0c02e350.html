NVIDIA Says AI Is Speeding Up Chip Factory Engineering Work

NVIDIA describes an "AI-defined fab," a chip factory where AI helps engineers test and improve processes faster. Chip making is getting harder, and physical tests on a fab line are slow and costly. NVIDIA says faster computing cuts simulation time, so engineers can try more options first. COMSOL reports up to 10x faster simulations, and Applied Materials reports up to 10x and 35x gains on its platforms. Siemens reports up to 17x faster lithography work. Lam Research showed AI trained on physics can predict plasma behavior in milliseconds, instead of days. Digital twins, which are virtual copies of a factory, let teams test changes before trying them for real. Engineers still make the decisions.
