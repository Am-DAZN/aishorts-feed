SpecSlot Cuts Cost of Image Generation by Splitting Fine Detail from Global Attention

Researchers introduced SpecSlot, an image-generating Transformer that works directly on pixels. Usual models use one token per image patch, so adding finer detail means more tokens and much costlier global attention. The team noticed that broad structure needs global sharing, while fine detail can be handled locally. SpecSlot splits each image anchor into several slots, each covering a band of frequencies. Only the lowest-frequency slot joins global attention, while all slots talk locally. On ImageNet-512, it improved FID, a image quality score where lower is better, by 2.84 over a matched control with 3.9% fewer FLOPs (a measure of compute). It came within 0.69 FID of JiT-B/16 at 38.2% lower cost. In text-to-image tests, it beat JiT-B/8 on DPG-Bench by 1.06 at 40.6% lower forward cost.
