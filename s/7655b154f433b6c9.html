New Method Helps AI Copy a User's Writing Style More Closely

Researchers built a way for AI to write long texts the way a specific person would. Usual methods just show the model a few of the user's past texts and hope it picks up the style. The new approach, called SP-Refine, adds a clear step first. The model fills in a style profile called StylePrism, which covers 28 style traits, with the user's habits, quoted examples, and things to avoid. It then writes a draft and revises it twice against that profile. Tested on three long-writing tasks with three language models, it beat the standard method in 25 of 27 score comparisons, by 10.2% on average. The profile works best as a guide for revising, not as a replacement for the user's samples.
