OpenAI outlines layered defense against adversarial distillation

OpenAI has described how it protects its models from adversarial distillation. This is when someone copies the abilities of an AI model by studying its outputs and using them to train another model. OpenAI calls this capability extraction. The company says it uses a layered approach. It combines model training, safeguards, detection, and enforcement. The source gives only a short overview and no further detail, so specifics on how each layer works are not provided. The post matters because it shows how AI developers are trying to guard their work from being copied.
