OpenAI launches a blog focused on AI in science

OpenAI has started a new blog about science. The blog will share new findings, practical methods, and firsthand accounts from researchers. These researchers are using AI to help advance scientific discovery. The post gives few other details, such as how often the blog will be updated or which fields it will cover. For now, it offers a place to read how scientists are putting AI tools to work in their research.
