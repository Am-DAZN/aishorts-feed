New test shows AI models can't predict chain reactions from still images

Researchers built DominoBench to test whether AI models that understand images can predict what happens in a physical chain reaction, such as a ball setting off ramps, seesaws and dominoes. It has 854 pictures in 427 pairs. In each pair, a tiny change decides whether one person survives. They tested 30 models from 10 families. None was reliable on its own. The best models from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI got the key person right in at most 72% of cases. They got both pictures in a pair right in at most 34%. When the exact calculation and needed numbers, like speeds, were written out, top models reached 82-92%. Small open models stayed near chance. The finding is that models fail at the math step, not at describing the scene. This matters for AI that must act in the real world.
