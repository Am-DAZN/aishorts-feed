OpenAI Explores Perplexity as a Shared Yardstick for AI Progress

OpenAI has published a blog post about perplexity, a measure used to judge how well AI language models predict text. A lower score generally means the model is less surprised by what comes next. The post looks at how this measure tracks progress in AI models. It also argues that perplexity could help developers agree on a common way to evaluate new abilities as they appear. A shared method matters because it makes it easier to compare models and see real gains. The source gives only a short description, so further details are not included here.
