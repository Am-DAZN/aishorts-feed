PolyRoute Benchmark Tests Message Routing Across 12 Languages and Mixed-Language Text

Researchers released PolyRoute, a test for AI models that read a customer support message and pick the right department out of three. It has 322 messages in 12 languages and 9 writing systems. It also has 156 mixed-language messages, such as Hinglish and Spanglish. A leaderboard of 13 systems shows big gaps. An English-based model scored 61.2%, a shipped multilingual one 77.3%, and the best fine-tuned model 96.6%. A simple word-counting method still reached 91.9%. On mixed-language text, the English model scored 85% against 79% for the multilingual one, but that gap is not statistically clear. The authors note all texts and labels were generated by AI, and the test can only reliably show differences above about 4 points.
