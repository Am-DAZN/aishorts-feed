Moving Traffic Can Fool Drone Video Stabilization, New Benchmark Shows

Drones that hover over a road often stabilize their video by lining every frame up with one reference frame. But the methods used are tested only on scenes where the camera moves and the world stays still. Researchers found a problem at busy intersections. Cars moving together can pull the alignment toward their motion instead of the background. They call this consensus capture, and standard checks on matching points do not catch it. The team built a two-part benchmark with 4K aerial footage to measure it. In tests, the ORB method was captured on 28 of 304 real-time pairs, with a median error of 18.8 pixels. Masking out moving objects removed the problem for every method and did not hurt the best ones. The code, data and results are public.
