New method picks the best AI answer by comparing pairs, one trait at a time

Researchers propose Aspect-wise Pairwise Tournaments, or APT, a way to choose the best answer when an AI model writes many. Instead of scoring each answer alone, APT pits two answers against each other in a knockout tournament. Each matchup is decided by a vote from several checkers, and each checker compares the pair on one trait. Across five areas and three language models, APT beat other selection methods, with the biggest gains on open-ended tasks. Users can also steer choices by rewriting the traits in plain language, with no need to regenerate answers. Nothing is trained, so it can move to new areas just by changing prompts.
