OpenAI releases BioBasicsBench to test AI on everyday lab science

OpenAI has introduced BioBasicsBench, a new benchmark for AI agents. A benchmark is a standard test used to compare how well different systems perform. This one has 100 tasks. It measures how well AI agents can read raw experimental data and handle routine wet-lab science. Wet-lab work means hands-on experiments with biological or chemical materials, as opposed to computer-based research. The test focuses on basic skills that scientists use every day. The source gives few other details, but the benchmark offers a way to check whether AI agents can help with real lab work.
