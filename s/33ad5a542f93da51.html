LynnReal-Hand lets people steer AI-generated video worlds with their hands

Researchers have built LynnReal-Hand, an AI model that generates video in real time based on hand movements. Earlier video world models mostly respond to text and camera controls, which makes it hard to interact with objects. The team trained on 100,000 clips of hands touching objects, both real and synthetic. They also sped up generation by cutting the steps needed per frame and by building a smaller, faster video decoder. On a single H200 chip, it streams 540p video at 18.03 frames per second with 741 milliseconds of delay. On the HandBench test, it followed hand motion more accurately than existing approaches. The authors say they will release the data, model weights and code.
