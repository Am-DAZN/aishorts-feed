Small AI Agent Learns to Take Notes and Read Whole Books

Researchers built MEMENTO, an AI agent based on the small Qwen3-8B model. It learns to manage its limited working memory by writing notes that replace its past history. Larger models often write poor notes, and training with rewards alone rarely teaches this skill. So the team first trained it on example notes written with hindsight, then refined it with reinforcement learning. On book-length question tests, with books up to 2.5 million tokens, accuracy rose from 36.9% to 67.8%. The agent never held more than 20,000 tokens at once. It beat a GPT-5.5 agent (13.1%) and full book context (56.7%) at under 1/50 the cost. It nearly matched a much larger GLM-5.3 agent (69.3%) at 1/9 the cost.
