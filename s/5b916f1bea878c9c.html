DeepMind unveils AlphaGenome 2 to study how DNA affects disease

Google DeepMind has announced AlphaGenome 2, an AI model aimed at understanding how cells read DNA. According to the announcement, it predicts how DNA sequences, and changes within them, affect specific types of human cells and disease. The source gives few further details, such as how well it performs or who can use it. Even so, the focus is clear. Knowing how small DNA changes alter cell behavior could help researchers learn why some diseases develop.
