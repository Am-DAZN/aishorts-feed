Two-Ledger Model Tracks What Each Business Order Costs in AI

Companies now run orders and claims through several AI agents across different clouds and models. But bills only show totals by product and period, so finance cannot tell what one order or claim cost in AI. This paper proposes the Two-Ledger Model. One ledger holds billing data in the FOCUS standard format. The other logs each AI call using OpenTelemetry, a common tracking tool, and links it to a business transaction. Costs are then checked against the invoice, and any unexplained gap can point to shadow AI, meaning unapproved or untracked use. In a simulation, the method kept cost coverage above 99% even with no trace data passed along. It found hidden spend within 0.24 percentage points and cut the highest transaction costs by up to 61%. The authors suggest making the second ledger a standard.
