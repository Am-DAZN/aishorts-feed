NVIDIA Expands AI Agents and Digital Twins to Cities Worldwide

NVIDIA and its partners are bringing AI tools to cities around the world. Cities collect huge amounts of data from cameras and sensors, but often struggle to use it. The new tools aim to fix that while letting local governments control sensitive data. In Spain, 1MillionBot runs resident-service agents that handle city procedures and appointments. In Malaysia, ITMAX is scaling camera-based AI agents to 17,000+ cameras by 2027, and its traffic signal testing cut peak-hour corridor travel times by up to 26%. In Poland, K2K turns existing camera streams into safety and traffic insights. Digital twins, which are virtual copies of real places, are also being used. They help plan infrastructure and run a refinery in Nigeria. NVIDIA says a workstation or small GPU system may be enough to start.
