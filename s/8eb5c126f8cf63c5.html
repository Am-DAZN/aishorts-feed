European banks use open AI models on their own infrastructure, NVIDIA says

At GTC Berlin, NVIDIA showed how European banks and finance firms are building their own AI with open weight models, which are models whose settings anyone can download and adapt. Revolut is moving to one foundation model, PRAGMA, serving 80 million customers. Lloyds built ATUM to learn from billions of transactions. Bunq says its assistant Finn matches leading closed models at 60% of the cost per answer. Stripe reports 42x faster data pipelines in offline tests for its fraud detection. The pitch is that banks can keep control of their data and costs.
