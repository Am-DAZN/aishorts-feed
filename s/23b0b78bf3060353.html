Microsoft podcast asks how AI could reshape medicine

Microsoft Research has released a podcast episode on how AI could change healthcare. Host Doug Burger talks with Ava Amini and Eric Horvitz of Microsoft, and Eric Topol of Scripps Research. They discuss AI-assisted diagnosis, medical imaging, and virtual cells, which are computer models of how cells work. They also look at how AI could guide experiments and drug discovery. The group explains why good research often reaches patients slowly. They say better biological data and real-world testing are still essential before AI's promise can be met.
