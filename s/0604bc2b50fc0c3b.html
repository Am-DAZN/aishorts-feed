OpenAI's Astra for Law tops legal test across 24 areas

OpenAI says Astra for Law came first in a launch evaluation that covered 24 areas of law. An evaluation is a set of tests used to compare how well AI tools perform a task. The source does not say what the tests involved or how rivals scored. OpenAI is also opening the tool up to its business users. Every ChatGPT Enterprise customer can now request access. ChatGPT Enterprise is the company's paid version of ChatGPT for large organizations. The news matters because law firms and legal teams are looking for AI tools they can trust with complex work. A strong result across many legal fields suggests the tool is built for broad use, though the details behind the ranking were not shared in this announcement.
