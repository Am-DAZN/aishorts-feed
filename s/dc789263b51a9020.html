Hidden Date in AI Prompts Can Quietly Change Test Results

Many chat models get a system prompt that includes today's date, added automatically. A new paper shows this hidden detail can change evaluation scores. Researchers tested six models on four benchmarks with everything else fixed. Switching between two plausible dates flipped a median 6.6% of test items between right and wrong. Rerunning a test one day later changed about as many answers as changing the batch size. A search method found dates that cut Llama-3.x accuracy by up to 36.5 points. Date changes also shifted AI-judge verdicts and undid 15 to 32% of reproduced jailbreak successes on Llama-3.2-3B. None of the eleven evaluation tools checked fixed the date. With the date pinned, results repeated exactly. The authors advise pinning, logging and reporting the date.
