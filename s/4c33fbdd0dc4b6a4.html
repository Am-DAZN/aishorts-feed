Study finds agent RL gains fade when AI gets fewer turns

Researchers looked at what multi-turn reinforcement learning (RL) really improves in AI agents. RL trains a model by rewarding good results over many steps. The team tested agents with different numbers of tries and different limits on how many turns they could take. They found the benefits of RL depend on having enough turns. When the turn budget shrinks, the advantage drops a lot. RL also does not make each single step better across the board. Its strength is in recovering from mistakes and staying on track in long tasks. To reduce the risk of this budget dependence, the authors discuss the pros and cons of rewarding intermediate steps. They also propose BudgetMix RL, which changes the turn limits during training. In their experiments, it performed steadily across different settings.
