FlexFloor Frees Idle Workers to Speed Up AI Model Training

Researchers present FlexFloor, a system that makes a step of AI training cheaper. The step is reinforcement learning post-training, where a large language model writes many responses and learns from the results. Writing those responses is slow. In mixture-of-experts models, which split work among specialist parts, some workers finish early but stay busy because their experts are still needed by others. About one-third of occupied worker time in the baseline was spent this way. FlexFloor releases those workers sooner while keeping the experts that ongoing responses need. Against the verl baseline, it produced about 65.40% more response tokens per unit of occupied computing time and raised generation throughput by 52.74%. Rewards showed little change. The authors say this can speed up building better language models and AI agents.
