EmbeddingGemma 2 Maps Text, Images, Audio and Video Into One Space

EmbeddingGemma 2 is a new open model that turns text, images, audio, video, and mixes of them into numbers called embeddings. These numbers let computers compare and search different kinds of content in one shared space. The team used simpler per-layer embeddings and trained the model in stages, adding new content types and goals step by step. The authors say it sets a new best balance of quality and size across benchmarks for all content types. They report it beats specialist models and models two to twelve times larger. It also suits limited hardware: users can load only the parts they need, limit image detail, shorten the embeddings, or use compressed weights. The model is released to support further research.
