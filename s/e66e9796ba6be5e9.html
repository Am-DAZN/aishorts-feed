DeepMind Maps Forest Types Across the Whole World

Google DeepMind has published a global map that sorts forests into different types. It covers every 10-meter patch of land on Earth, roughly the size of a small room. This goes beyond simply showing where trees are. Knowing what kind of forest grows in a place gives a fuller picture of the land. The source gives few other details, such as how the map was built or how accurate it is.
