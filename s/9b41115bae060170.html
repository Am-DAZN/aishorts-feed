Can AI Write an Endless Poem? Researchers Put It to the Test

A research paper asks whether AI can write an infinite poem. The team used couplets, which are two-line pairs, where the end words are linked and the rest of the structure repeats. In the first experiment, people and AI wrote together to extend a poem, which became an animated web artwork. In the second, an AI language model was paired with a tool that measures how close words are in meaning. This method reached 1,000 couplets without manual help and did better than the other. The authors conclude that AI makes some interesting word pairings but still falls short of human creativity and grasp of poetic links. They also suggest poetry may be revived by using AI in its workflow.
