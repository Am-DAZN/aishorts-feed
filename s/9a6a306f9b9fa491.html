Study Tests If AI Can Rate Veterans' Crisis Posts Like Experts

Researchers tested whether large language models (LLMs), the AI behind chatbots, can rate how severe a crisis is in veterans' social media posts. They compared 25 models against eight veteran experts on 149 tweets, using five severity levels. The experts often disagreed, mostly between neighboring levels. The AI models agreed with each other much more. Paid models generally matched the experts better and were more stable than free, open-source ones. But agreeing with each other did not mean the models agreed with humans, and they were not reliable at spotting rare, high-severity cases. Misleading examples in prompts also threw some models off. The authors say LLMs could help experts at scale, but need careful testing before use in high-stakes mental health work.
