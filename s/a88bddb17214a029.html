LLMON markup language now supported in Granite 4.1 3B and 8B

A Hugging Face Blog post announces LLMON, described as an LLM-native markup language. The title says it is now available in Granite 4.1 models, in the 3B and 8B sizes. The post text was not available, so details on how LLMON works and what it is used for are not known. Based on the title alone, the idea is a markup format built for large language models rather than for people or older software. Granite is a model family, and the 3B and 8B refer to the model sizes.
