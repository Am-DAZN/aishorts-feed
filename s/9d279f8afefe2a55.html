PhysLoop Uses LLMs to Teach Video AI Better Physics

Video generation models make realistic-looking clips, but objects often move in ways that break the laws of physics. Researchers have built PhysLoop, a system that uses large language models (LLMs), which know a lot about how the physical world works, to guide the video model. The LLM helps create videos, pick good ones, and fix mistakes, with rules that keep it on track. The result is training data that follows physics more closely, and a way to repair flawed videos when they are generated. The team says this is a step toward models that improve themselves with little human help. They release 100,000 videos of indoor and outdoor scenes, plus tools and two test datasets. They also test physical behavior on an outside benchmark called Physics-IQ Verified.
