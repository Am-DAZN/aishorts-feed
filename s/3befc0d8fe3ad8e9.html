New Metric Helps IoT Devices Decide When to Update Their AI Models

Small IoT devices, like sensors, keep a copy of an AI model to make quick decisions. Updating that copy often uses up bandwidth and energy. This paper proposes a measure called Age of Intelligence, which checks how much an old model would actually hurt a device's decisions, not just how old it is. The authors balance the cost of bad decisions against the cost of updating. They test two methods: one compares changes in the model's outputs, and the other tracks how much the model's settings changed. In their numerical tests, the simpler settings-based method cut total synchronization cost by 6.15% compared with a policy that always updates right away. It performed about as well as the output-based method.
