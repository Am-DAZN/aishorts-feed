Egyptian and Nigerian English join Open ASR Leaderboard

Hugging Face has added Egyptian and Nigerian English to its Open ASR Leaderboard, based on the title of a recent blog post. ASR means automatic speech recognition, which is software that turns spoken words into text. The leaderboard is a public ranking that compares how well different speech models perform. The full post was not available, so details such as the datasets, the models tested, and the results are unknown. Still, the addition suggests wider testing of speech tools on English as it is spoken in more parts of the world.
