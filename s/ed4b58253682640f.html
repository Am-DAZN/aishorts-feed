OpenAI Introduces LifeVal, a Test for AI Help With Daily Tasks

OpenAI has announced LifeVal, a new benchmark. A benchmark is a standard test used to score how well AI systems perform. This one measures how well AI helps people finish everyday tasks. Examples include finding childcare, managing money, and handling government paperwork. The announcement was posted on OpenAI's blog. It matters because most AI tests focus on exams or puzzles, while LifeVal looks at practical chores people face in real life. The post gives no further detail in the text provided.
