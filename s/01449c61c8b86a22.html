METR updates its AI task-length graph, adding harder, judgment-based tests

METR, a nonprofit that tests AI, has updated its well-known "time horizon" graph. The graph rates models by how long a task takes a human, and the original showed the task length AI could finish doubling every four months. The new version adds much longer tasks and fuzzier ones that need judgment. Examples include designing new AI tests and scoring philosophical arguments. Here, even top models fell short of humans. None of the AI-designed tests was good enough for METR staff to find useful. This matters because such skills are key if AI is to automate AI research. Experts welcomed the more creative tests, though scoring them is hard.
