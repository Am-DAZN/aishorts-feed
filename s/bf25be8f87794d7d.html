DevRev uses OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol for self-service support tools

DevRev, a software company, is using OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol model to run two of its products, called Computer and Operator. According to OpenAI's blog, the goal is to serve business customers with automated, self-service help. The model was chosen to balance three things: how well it reasons, how fast it responds, and how much it costs to run. The short post gives no further detail, such as results or numbers. It shows how companies are picking AI models by weighing quality against speed and price.
