OpenAI says GPT-6 models can design proteins and show binding in lab tests

OpenAI says its GPT-6 models can design proteins, which are the tiny building blocks of living things. The models work out both the structure and the sequence of a protein. They do this by reasoning through the problem and writing code. The company reports that lab tests found evidence of binding across five targets. Binding means a designed protein sticks to the molecule it was built to attach to. The source gives few other details, so it is not clear how strong the results are or how they compare with other methods. Still, the work suggests general-purpose AI models may help with protein design.
