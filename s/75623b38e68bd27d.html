Why fine-tuning jobs often run slower than expected

A Hugging Face Blog post asks why a fine-tuning job can take longer than the FLOPs say. FLOPs count how many math operations a job needs, and they are often used to guess training time. The title suggests the real time can be longer than that estimate. The full text was not available, so the causes and any fixes are not covered here. Fine-tuning means training an existing AI model further on new data for a specific task.
