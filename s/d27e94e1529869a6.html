New Benchmark Shows AI Agents Struggle to Design Good Experiments

Researchers released Causal-Discovery-Bench, a test for how well AI agents can run experiments to find cause-and-effect links. The sandbox uses made-up systems with no real-world clues, and limits the agent's resources. They ran 3,802 trials with 13 models from 5 companies. The main finding: 94.5% of mistakes came from poor experiment design, not from faulty reasoning about the results. Giving agents more budget did not reliably fix failures. Basic statistics tools helped many models a lot, but advanced tools added little when models lacked simpler skills. The work matters because AI agents are increasingly used for autonomous research.
