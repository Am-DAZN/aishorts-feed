YouTuber Finds Video Location Using Faint Airplane Sounds in the Background

A YouTuber named Colsto showed how quiet background noise from planes can reveal where a video was recorded. First, he pulls the faint plane sounds out of a video's audio. Then he studies the Doppler curve, which is how a sound's pitch shifts as a plane flies past. Next, he compares those clues with ADS-B data, the public flight tracking signals planes broadcast. The plane speeds, their distance, and the gaps between flights help pin down the exact spot. Simon Willison called it a genius piece of OSINT, meaning research using public information. It matters for privacy, because a video may give away its location even when nothing in the picture does.
