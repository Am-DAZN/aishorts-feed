OpenAI names Dan Driscoll senior counselor for national security work

OpenAI has appointed Dan Driscoll as Senior Counselor for National Security Modernization. In this role, he will advise the company on using AI responsibly in national security. His work will also cover cyber resilience, which means helping systems stay safe from and recover after digital attacks. He will also advise on working with U.S. allies. The announcement was short and gave no other details, such as when he starts or what specific projects he will lead. The move shows OpenAI is paying closer attention to how its technology fits into government and defense settings.
