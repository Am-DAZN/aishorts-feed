DeltaHarness Lets AI Agents Tune How They Run Tools to Cut Delay

AI agents often use tools, and a "harness" is the software that organizes and schedules those tool steps. The authors say the same task can run faster or slower depending on hidden conditions, so the best plan can't be known in advance. Their method, DeltaHarness, learns by actually running the tools in the target setting and measuring what happens. It then updates how work is dispatched, revises its plans, and picks new experiments to try. A separate confirmation panel decides whether a new policy replaces the old one. On one video-editing task, a revised policy cut full delivery time by 30.0%. On unseen tasks, evolved policies cut latency by 8.2% against the best fixed plan in one test run, and by 4.6% against the first proposal in another.
