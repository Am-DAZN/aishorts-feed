LingBot-Touch Lets Robots Use Many Types of Touch Sensors

Robots that handle small objects with skill need a sense of touch. But touch sensors on the market differ a lot, and each has strengths and weaknesses. Researchers built LingBot-Touch, a model that turns data from different sensors into one shared format. It works with pressure-based, magnetic and camera-based touch sensors, and can be extended to new types. Its touch signals can be added to different robot control systems, including diffusion policy and vision-language-action models like π0.5 and LingBot-VLA. Tests in simulation and on real robots across several hardware platforms showed better results on complex tasks, with low computing cost. The authors say mixing data from many sensors could help scale up touch learning and guide future robot design.
