AI Model Identifies Creator of Anonymous 1808 Sketchbook in 22 Minutes

History professor Mark Humphries shared a case study on GPT-6.2 Astra, an AI model. With very little prompting, it worked out who made an anonymous 1808 sketchbook held at Yale's Beinecke Library. It took just 22 minutes and used public sources. The AI found a diary note about a visit by the vicomte de Chabot. It guessed that Chabot's travel journal would confirm he made the sketchbook, and it did. The journal is also a major find for Canadian and fur trade historians. Humphries expects historians may face a mild crisis of confidence, but less than mathematicians, since history is about reinterpreting the past, not solving one problem.
