Adding a Second AI to a Brainstorm Team Cuts Human Ideas

Researchers tested how many AI partners help people brainstorm. In an experiment with 168 people, they compared teams with one AI or two AI partners. Some AIs acted like the average person, while others were customized by the participant. Rooms with two AIs posted more ideas overall, but the ideas written by humans dropped a lot. People kept talking but posted fewer ideas to the shared board. With one AI, a average-style AI led to the most human posts. That edge vanished with two AIs. AI chatty, social talk led to more human posts, while heavy AI posting led to fewer. AI also made human ideas more varied overall. The study warns that results from one AI do not carry over to teams with several AIs.
