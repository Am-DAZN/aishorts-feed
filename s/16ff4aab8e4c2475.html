Large Training Runs Show Which AI Designs Really Work, and Which Benchmarks Miss

Researchers ran 64 training runs from scratch, using Transformers and three types of linear models, from 1.3 to 8 billion parameters. Some used up to 2 trillion tokens of text. At small scale, design differences nearly vanish. At large scale, they show up. Canon layers, which mix nearby tokens, helped Transformers and saved about 30% of training tokens for the same MMLU score at 8B. With Canon, the simplest linear model matched a more complex one and beat Mamba2. But standard benchmarks hid flaws: linear models could not reliably find information in a 500-token text, and no model beat random guessing on a simple two-step question. The authors suggest adding reasoning examples to training data early.
