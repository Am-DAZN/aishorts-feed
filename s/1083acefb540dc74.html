JoVox Generates 3D Voxel Shapes Faster and With Less Computing Power

Voxels are tiny 3D blocks, like pixels in space, used to build 3D shapes. Making detailed voxels without heavy computing is hard. Many methods build a shape from coarse to fine. But the coarse stage is done first, with little information, and never fixed later. JoVox changes this. It learns all levels of detail together, so coarse cells can be guessed with finer cells already nearby. It also predicts two levels of detail at once and revisits early choices to fix mistakes. The authors report better results than the current top method, TRELLIS.2: 8x higher training throughput, 1/15 of the computing per forward pass, training on 4 GPUs instead of 32, and an 11% lower Chamfer distance, a measure of shape error.
