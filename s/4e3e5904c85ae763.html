Singapore Airlines Uses OpenAI Tools for Customer Help and Staff Work

Singapore Airlines is using OpenAI's models and API (a way for software to connect to the AI) in several parts of its business. The airline says it uses the technology to improve customer support, help people discover travel options, and make employees more productive. One result stands out: customer satisfaction scores, known as CSAT, doubled for its chatbot, called Kris. The source gives few other details, so the full scope of the rollout is not clear. Still, the example shows how large airlines are turning to AI chatbots to answer travelers' questions and support their own staff.
