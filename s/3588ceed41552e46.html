OpenAI and Upwork study finds AI is reshaping freelance work

OpenAI and Upwork, a freelance job platform, have published new research on how AI is changing freelance work. The study finds that demand for different kinds of freelance jobs is shifting. It also points to new combinations of skills that clients are looking for. Projects tied to AI come with larger budgets and more hours of work. The source gives only a short overview, so details on the data and methods are not included here. The findings matter because they offer an early look at how AI is changing what freelancers are hired to do and how much those jobs pay.
