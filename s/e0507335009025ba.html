OpenFlyV2 lets a small drone understand language and fly itself onboard

Researchers have built OpenFlyV2, a single AI model that helps drones see, understand instructions and fly. Most drone systems handle just one skill, or rely on a distant computer to do the thinking. This model runs fully onboard an NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX, a small, low-power computer. It plans 12 flight waypoints at a time, 10 times per second. The team trained it in simulation, using about 70,000 navigation trajectories and 60,000 question-and-answer pairs about images. Without any real-world flight training data, it flew a real quadrotor and reached a 48% navigation success rate across four real-world environments. The authors say this shows broad language and vision skills can sit alongside continuous flight control in one model on limited hardware. They plan to release the code publicly.
