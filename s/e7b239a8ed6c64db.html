New Benchmark Tests AI Agents on Turning 3D Building Scans into Models

Researchers introduced S2Bench, a test for AI coding agents that turn 3D laser scans of buildings into engineering models. It covers 128 real-world scenes across five domains, with expert-made reference models. Agents measure the scan in Python, build labeled parts in a CAD tool, and refine their code. The study tested five agents and one team setup. The best agent matched scan shapes well, with 96% of points within 5 cm. But it scored only 63% on labeling parts correctly, and no agent passed all engineering checks on more than 38% of scenes. Results got worse on larger scenes. A smaller model working as a team improved results, especially on big scenes. The work shows agents can copy shapes but still struggle to understand what building parts are.
