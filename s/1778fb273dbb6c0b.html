AI Adoption Pushes Southeast Asia's Digital Economy Toward $368 Billion

A Google AI blog post says Southeast Asia's digital economy is growing fast, and AI is a main reason. The region's digital economy is projected to reach $368 billion in 2026. It is expected to climb to $1 trillion by 2035. The post credits widespread use of AI for this growth. The digital economy means money made from online activity, such as e-commerce, digital services and online platforms. The source gives few other details, but the figures suggest AI could play a large role in the region's economic future.
