OpenAI Paper Claims the Exponential Time Hypothesis Is False

A new Two Minute Papers video looks at a paper from OpenAI. The paper's title says the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false. That is a well-known guess in computer science about how long some hard problems must take to solve. The video's description gives no details about the method or the proof, and no one has confirmed the claim in the text provided. The paper is posted on OpenAI's GitHub page for anyone to read. If the result holds up after expert checks, it would be a big deal for the study of hard problems. For now, it is a claim that needs careful review.
