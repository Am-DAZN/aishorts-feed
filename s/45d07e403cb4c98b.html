FasterRW Speeds Up Chip Temperature Simulation by Up to 28 Times

Heat simulation matters more and more in computer chip design and manufacturing. One method, called random walk, estimates the temperature at a single spot without solving the whole chip. But when the chip has no fixed-temperature edges, the walks must run very long to keep errors small. A new paper presents FasterRW, which makes these walks shorter. It uses a cheap, rough guess of the temperature to fill in what a cut-short walk leaves out. The authors prove a math rule that lets them do this and pick when to stop a walk while controlling error. For many points at once, it reuses leftover walk data as extra samples and refines them with a statistical method called kriging. The team reports up to 28 times faster results than earlier top methods, with similar accuracy. The code is open source.
