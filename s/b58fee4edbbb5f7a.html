Australian universities bring ChatGPT into classrooms to prepare students for AI jobs

Universities in Australia are widening access to ChatGPT, an AI chatbot made by OpenAI. They are also weaving AI into how they teach and how students learn. The goal is to get graduates ready for a workplace where AI tools are becoming common. The source gives few details, so it does not say which universities are involved or how many students will get access. Still, it shows how schools are moving from asking whether students should use AI to teaching them how to use it well.
