AutoPILOT Lets AI Agents Keep Playing Games Between Thinking Steps

Researchers built AutoPILOT, a new setup for AI models that play video games in real time. Many models do well when a game pauses for them, but struggle when it keeps running. The team says the problem is partly the harness, the layer that decides what the model sees, remembers and does. Older harnesses turn each model call into one action. AutoPILOT lets the model send a single action, start a fixed sequence of moves, or write a small controller that plays from the screen on its own. The team also built AFK-Bench, with 120 browser games. With GPT-6.1 Sol, AutoPILOT reached 68.6% real-time success versus 38.3% for VISTA, and matched VISTA's final score at a fifth of the API cost.
