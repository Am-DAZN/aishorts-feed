OpenAI Studies How 1,800 Scientists Use ChatGPT in Research

OpenAI has shared findings from a look at how more than 1,800 scientists use ChatGPT in their work. The blog post covers how the tool fits into their daily routines, how much more productive they say they have become, and what they expect it to help them discover. The productivity numbers are reported by the scientists themselves, not measured independently. The source text gives few other details, but the study offers an early picture of how AI chatbots are being used in real science.
