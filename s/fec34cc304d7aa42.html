New Measure Tracks How Much AI Can 'Think' Without Showing Its Work

Researchers propose a way to measure how much hidden, step-by-step thinking an AI model can do without putting it into readable words. Today, chain-of-thought, where a model writes out its reasoning, helps people oversee AI. But some new designs could make that harder. The paper defines a score called NLS depth, which can be worked out before training from the model's design and setup. They find it tracks closely with a model's layer count and has grown slowly in recent years. Designs that reason in hidden internal signals could raise it a lot. The authors say AI developers should report this score alongside direct tests of how well their models' reasoning can be monitored.
