OpenAI teams up with Hacktron to hunt for security flaws

OpenAI has announced a partnership with Hacktron, a security company. The goal is to find vulnerabilities, which are weak spots that attackers could use to break into a system. The two will also test how OpenAI's systems connect with third-party services, meaning tools made by other companies. They plan to use frontier AI, the most advanced AI models available, to help strengthen defenses. The announcement is short and gives few details. It shows OpenAI is bringing in outside experts to check its own security.
