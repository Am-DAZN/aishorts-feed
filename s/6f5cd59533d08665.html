TimeOmni-o1 links two AI model types to read and forecast time series

Researchers have built TimeOmni-o1, a model that works with both text and time series, which are numbers tracked over time, like sales or sensor readings. It joins a language model with a time series foundation model, a system trained on huge amounts of such data to forecast. The foundation model compresses the numbers for the language model, which reasons over them alongside the text. Then that reasoning goes back to the foundation model, which makes the forecast. The team also built MMTR, a dataset of over 500,000 samples across 12 domains, with written reasoning steps. They report that TimeOmni-o1 beats existing models at understanding and forecasting in most domains, and beats each part used alone. It also did better than dedicated time series models on three open benchmarks.
