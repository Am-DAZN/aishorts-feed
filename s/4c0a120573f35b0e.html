New method brings back variety in fast AI image and video generators

Fast image and video generators are often made by shrinking a big model into a small one that needs fewer steps. This is called distillation. The catch is that the small model gives nearly the same result whatever random seed you use. Researchers looked inside these models to find out why. They found the small models keep the same total amount of variation in their internal signals, but squeeze it into far fewer directions. The problem shows up in one middle block at the first denoising step. So the model has not lost its ability to create varied output. Its variation is just poorly spread. The team built a fix called ReDiverse. One version fine-tunes a small add-on so the student's signals match the teacher's. Another needs no training and nudges signals along the teacher's main directions.
