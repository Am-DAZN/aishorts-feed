DocSEAM Lets AI See Neighboring Pages When Reading Long Documents

Many AI models that read multi-page documents look at each page alone. That means a page is processed without knowing what is on the pages next to it. Researchers introduce DocSEAM, a small add-on module placed between the model's image reader and its language model. For each page, it looks at the pages before and after, and blends in useful details from them. The number of visual tokens, the chunks of image data the model uses, stays the same. Their 9B-parameter model scored best among open-weight models up to 10B parameters on five document question-answering tests. The method also made results less sensitive to where pages are split, and helped the model find answers that span two pages.
