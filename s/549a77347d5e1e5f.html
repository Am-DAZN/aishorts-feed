OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol Models

OpenAI has introduced two new AI models, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol. The company says they give better results for professional work, writing code, and using a computer on a person's behalf. They are also described as more efficient and cheaper to run. The models are available in ChatGPT Work, in Codex (OpenAI's coding tool), and through the API, which lets developers build the models into their own apps. The announcement gives few other details, so it is not yet clear how big the improvements are or how the two models differ from each other.
