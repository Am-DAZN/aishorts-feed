NVIDIA RTX Spark Mini Desktops Go on Sale From ASUS, Dell and MSI

NVIDIA says new RTX Spark compact desktops from ASUS, Dell and MSI are available today. They are small Windows PCs built to run AI models and AI agents (programs that do tasks for you) on the machine itself. The systems have up to 128GB of shared memory and a 20-core Grace CPU. NVIDIA says they reach up to 1 petaflop of AI performance and can run 120-billion-parameter language models locally. Running models locally keeps files on the PC and avoids per-token cloud charges. Mini PCs from Acer, GIGABYTE and HP are coming soon, along with a Microsoft Surface dev box. NVIDIA also points to support from Adobe apps, Blender and ComfyUI for creators and developers.
