New test checks if AI video reasoning holds up in bad visuals

Researchers released GRAPE-Bench, a test for AI models that reason about video when the picture is poor. It mixes synthetic videos with twelve types of added damage, plus real driving footage at night or in rain, snow or fog. Each video comes with three questions, from basic safety-related judgments to hard reasoning puzzles. The team found that model rankings can flip between synthetic and real footage. Overall accuracy also hides weak spots at certain question levels. Fine-tuning Qwen3.5-9B on synthetic data raised its real-world accuracy from 76.7% to 81.1%. The authors also show a way to steer a model between safety-focused judgments and harder reasoning. This matters because video AI used in areas like driving must stay reliable when conditions are bad.
