Google's Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash makes longer video clips for less

Google has released Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, a model built to be fast and cost-effective. It can generate and edit video natively, with clips now up to 15 seconds long. Google says the model is also more consistent, which means the results should hold together better from one moment to the next. The price is 30% lower than before. Longer clips and cheaper pricing could make it easier for people and businesses to try AI video tools. The announcement came on Google's AI blog and gave few other details.
