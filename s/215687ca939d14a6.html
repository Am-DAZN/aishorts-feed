NVIDIA's open source 6G tools see 10x growth among researchers

A year after NVIDIA made its Aerial wireless software open source, adoption of Aerial and Sionna has grown 10x. The open source 5G and 6G tools have passed 1 million downloads, and more than 8,000 research and academic institutions use them. Early projects show where 6G could head. At King's College London, a robot sent short descriptions of objects instead of full images, cutting task completion time by up to 52.6%. SoftBank's AI signal receiver beat a conventional one in all 15 tested settings, with a gain of roughly 60%. ETH Zurich found that tuning AI receivers with local measurements cut block error rates by over one-third. Together, the work points toward AI-native 6G networks.
