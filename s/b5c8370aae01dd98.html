Xing 4 AI models trained at scale on Ascend chips

Researchers behind TeleChat have released Xing 4, a new series of large language models built and trained on Ascend chips and the MindSpore software framework. The report covers model design, large-scale pretraining, and later training for reasoning and agent tasks. A 285-billion-parameter model was pretrained on 8,192 Ascend 910C cards. The team used tricks to split work across chips, save memory, and cut scheduling delays. For later training, they built TeleGym, a platform where AI agents run tasks, get scored, and learn from the results. It handles tasks of different lengths without breaking their progress. The work shows a full AI training setup can run on domestic chips and software.
