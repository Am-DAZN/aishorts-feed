Why AI Models Treat Images and Text Unequally, New Study Finds

Vision-language models are AI systems that read both images and text. They often handle the two unevenly, a problem called the modality gap. This paper looks at one possible cause. The authors show that text tokens, the small pieces the model reads, carry much less meaning at the start than image tokens. As a result, the model's early layers spend their effort processing the text first, and only later combine it with the image. In a test, the researchers swapped image positions for the closest matching text tokens. The model then paid more attention to those positions in its early layers. The findings give researchers a base for narrowing the gap and improving how these models reason across images and text.
