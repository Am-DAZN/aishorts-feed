Datasette Cluster Map 0.19 Updates Plugin for Datasette 1.0a42

Simon Willison has released version 0.19 of datasette-cluster-map, one of the oldest plugins for Datasette, his tool for exploring data. He calls it one of his favorites. The update now works with Datasette 1.0a42. It also depends on a new package, datasette-leaflet, which makes sure OpenStreetMap map tiles are requested with a referrer header. That header tells the map service which site is asking. As a demo, Willison shared a map of power plants owned by California's PG&E. The demo also shows Datasette's recently redesigned facets, which are filters for browsing data.
