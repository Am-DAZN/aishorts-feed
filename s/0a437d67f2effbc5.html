OpenAI Launches Images 2.6 With Faster Generation and GIF Support

OpenAI has released Images 2.6, an update to its image tools. The company says it makes image generation faster. It also adds the ability to create GIFs, which are short looping animations. Editing is now more precise, so users can change parts of a picture with more control. Developers get new controls in the API, the tool that lets other apps use OpenAI's models. The update is available in ChatGPT, Codex and the API. OpenAI's short announcement gives no further details, such as speed figures or pricing.
