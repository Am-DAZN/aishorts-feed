Google Opens Gemini 4 Carbon to Everyone, With a Price Rise Planned

Simon Willison released llm-gemini 0.36, an update to his tool for using Google's Gemini models. It supports Gemini 4 Carbon, which Google first announced two weeks ago for only a small group of cyber defenders. Now anyone can use it. Willison calls it Google's best Gemini yet. At launch it costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. On December 1st, the price is set to double to $4 and $20. Willison tested it by drawing pelican pictures at low, medium and high thinking effort, with medium as the default. He also used it in Google's Antigravity coding tool to build a 3D animation of a giant mech walking through New York City, using map data.
