OpenAI Releases GPT-6.1 Terra, a Faster Midrange Model

OpenAI has launched GPT-6.1 Terra, a fast and efficient model. It focuses on visual reasoning, computer use and clearer writing. OpenAI says it suits workday tasks like drafting proposals, reading technical drawings, filling out forms and editing videos. Blogger Simon Willison notes Terra sits in the middle of the lineup, much like Sonnet in Anthropic's models. OpenAI's Ted Sanders says it beats Astra and Sol at technical writing and matches them on vision and computer use, but runs much faster. It is a bit worse on most other things. Terra costs $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens. That is almost half the price of Sol, and around 10 times the price of the smaller Luna model.
