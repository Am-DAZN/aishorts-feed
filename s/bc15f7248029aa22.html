V-JEPA-LM Teaches Video AI to Read Motion, Not Just Captions

Video encoders like V-JEPA learn how scenes change, such as direction, speed and order, from video alone. But when paired with a language model, that skill often goes unused. Captions name only a few details, so the model learns to look up those details. In one test, a simple probe read speed from V-JEPA at 87%, while the full video-language model answered at chance. Researchers built new training data by altering public videos with pixel changes, so each answer depends only on the change. Trained in one stage on 4.53M public samples, the V-JEPA-LM models (1B to 8B) lead every size class across eleven video benchmarks. The 4B model scores 72.1, against 61.1 for the best open 4B. The team says the encoder sets what can be read, and the training data sets what is read. Code and models are public.
