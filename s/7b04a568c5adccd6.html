Google DeepMind shows AI agents helping with drug discovery tasks

Google DeepMind has shared how its Google Antigravity tool and a set of Cheminformatics Skills can speed up drug discovery work. Cheminformatics means using computers to study chemical compounds. According to the post, the tools can help search for compounds and suggest new rigidified macrocycles. Macrocycles are large ring-shaped molecules, and rigidified ones are made stiffer in structure. The post presents this as an example of AI agents, which are programs that carry out multi-step tasks, being used in science. The source gives few details, so results and performance are not described here.
