EgoX Lets Robots Learn From Humans, Other Robots, and Simulations

Robots can learn from many sources, including humans, real machines and simulated agents. But their bodies, sensors and controls differ, so mixing their demonstrations is hard. EgoX is a new framework that lines up these different demonstrations by matching similar kinds of observations and actions. It trains a model called MXT+ on the mix, then fine-tunes it on the target robot. A training-only step called embodiment dreaming asks the model to predict future images and body positions, which helps it grasp how each body moves. Tested on 13 task and robot pairs, including five real robots and one simulated humanoid, average task progress rose from 0.63 to 0.80 versus training from scratch. In unfamiliar conditions, it rose from 0.53 to 0.77.
