AnyS1: One Small Model Handles Routine Choices for Any AI Agent

AI agents often call a large, slow, costly model at every step, even when the choice is just picking from a few known options. Earlier fixes used a small, fast model for these picks, but each setup needed custom code. Researchers built AnyS1, which uses human-written step-by-step skills to find where such choices come up. They mined over 1,000 public skills, collected examples by running tasks in isolated containers, and trained one 4B-size small model to pick the right option. If the small model is not confident enough, the large model takes over. On three new test sets and several large-model families, AnyS1 kept tasks reliable while cutting episode cost by 75-91% and model-call time by 63-92%. It also worked when the confidence threshold was reused without retuning.
