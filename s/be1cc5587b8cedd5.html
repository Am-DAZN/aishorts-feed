New AutoformBench Tests AI on Building Full Lean Math Formalizations

Researchers have released AutoformBench, a test for AI systems that turn advanced math into computer-checked proofs. The proofs are written in Lean, a language that verifies each step. The benchmark has 341 tasks from 24 advanced math sources. Unlike older tests, it does not just hand the AI a single theorem. The AI gets a normal written proof and must translate it into Lean. It also has to build the missing definitions and smaller supporting results that connect the theorem to Lean's math library, called Mathlib. Mathematicians who know Lean wrote and checked each target statement. The team compared leading AI models and found they struggle in similar places. Success depended less on the area of math and more on whether a model could build the missing formal groundwork. The data and code are public.
