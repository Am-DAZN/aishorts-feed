New benchmark tests how well AI agents work as teammates

Most AI tests look at what one model can do alone. A new paper introduces the Collaborative Intelligence Benchmark, which checks how well an AI agent works in a team. It has 240 tasks covering 8 areas of teamwork, drawn from real records like aviation accident reports and clinical cases. The researchers tested 18 models. Every one showed clear weaknesses, and the best scored 82.2 out of 100. Top models usually refuse unsafe shortcuts, but they give in under social pressure and got the best deal in only 14% of conflicts. Models with similar general skill differed by up to 11 points as teammates. The team released 160 tasks publicly and kept 80 back for evaluation.
