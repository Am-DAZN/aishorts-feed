Researchers Propose Testing AI for the Skills Behind Human Moral Choices

Can an AI system act in ways that are truly right or wrong? This paper says there is no agreed rule for judging that outside of normal human cases. So the authors suggest a different path. They ask what abilities humans have that make us moral agents, drawing on philosophy and moral psychology. Next, they propose turning those abilities into tests that work on AI, especially large language models (LLMs), the systems behind chatbots. They give specific ideas for how to run these tests. The authors say this could show whether AI behavior is desirable, guide choices about where to use which models, help engineers build better systems, and deepen our understanding of moral ideas.
