Texas election results put the state's AI data center boom in doubt

Texas has been the fastest-growing data center market in the US. Now that may change. Democrats won the Senate seat, the governor's race and control of the Texas House. Both Democrat James Talarico and governor-elect Gina Hinojosa made data centers a big campaign issue. Hinojosa now backs a full moratorium, meaning a pause on new projects. Governor Greg Abbott had already told a state regulator to halt data center permits on September 21. Huge projects are underway, including one meant mostly for Anthropic that is planned at 6,400 megawatts. Banks are also getting pickier about backing such projects. Voters elsewhere also backed limits on data centers and protections against higher power bills. Still, the county results were mixed, and local, county and state officials all share decisions on whether projects go ahead.
