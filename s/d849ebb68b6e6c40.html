RFBench tests whether AI can design and understand radio-frequency circuit layouts

Researchers released RFBench, a new test for AI models that work with both text and images. It focuses on passive radio-frequency (RF) circuits, the parts that handle radio signals inside devices. The dataset has 10,000 simple circuit layouts, each checked with a simulation tool called ADS Momentum. Each layout comes with shapes, an image, a grid, and signal measurements. There are seven tasks, such as generating a layout from a spec and predicting how it behaves. Seven AI models showed clear weaknesses in making valid layouts and predicting signal behavior. After fine-tuning on RF data, valid layouts rose from 0.0% to 83.2%. One prediction score rose from 17.7% to 62.8%. The authors say much room for progress remains.
