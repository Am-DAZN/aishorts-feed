Startup Osmo says its AI can smell as well as a person

Osmo, an AI startup spun out of Google Brain in 2023, says its model has reached human-level smell skills. It defines that as being about as good as an average person at telling scents apart, and as a junior perfume designer at creating them. To train it, about 30 expert sniffers and 70 data labelers describe odors, while chemical sensors break down the molecules. The company says it has built a dataset of over 10 million human labels. Osmo says perfume brands already use it to design scents, and that it beat in-house perfumers at a large consumer goods firm. Its founder, Alex Wiltschko, thinks it could one day help detect threats at airports or diagnose diseases. These are the company's own claims.
