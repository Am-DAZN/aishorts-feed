Apriel Voice 27B Preview: Open Model That Listens, Reasons and Uses Tools

Researchers have released Apriel Voice 27B Preview, an open model that works with speech. A good voice assistant must do more than hear words. It must understand requests, think through context, keep a conversation going and use tools. This model does all of that in one system, so no separate dialogue model is needed. It is built on Gemma 4 26B-A4B and can still take text and images. The team trained it in two stages. The first sets up the speech input. The second tunes the model further, using a method where a larger teacher model guides a student. In thinking mode, it scored 98.90 on Big Bench Audio and 81.13 on Speech-BFCL. It reached a 65.51% task success rate on τ-Voice, with average latency of 1.91 seconds. The team also runs it on a custom platform for live voice chats.
