NVIDIA Engineer Teaches Developers to Build With Open AI Models

Chris Alexiuk, a senior product research engineer at NVIDIA in Toronto, teaches developers how to use open large language models (LLMs), the AI systems behind chatbots. He does it mostly through YouTube videos on building, fine-tuning and shipping AI apps and agents. Alexiuk argues that truly open AI means sharing the weights, the training data and the training recipes. He champions NVIDIA's Nemotron family of open models, from smaller ones for repetitive tasks to larger ones for long-running agents. He says openness helps more people understand AI and use it in creative ways. His interest began with a bird-spotting image classifier he built in college.
