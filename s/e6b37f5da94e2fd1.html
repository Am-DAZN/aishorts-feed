SAP and Infosys Leaders Say AI Must Be a Company-Wide Platform

Many companies have treated AI as a set of small tests. As AI agents, which are programs that carry out tasks on their own, move into core business work, that approach falls short. In an MIT Technology Review podcast made with Infosys, two executives explain why. SAP's Thorsten Leiduck says AI depends on a strong, connected data base. He says 80% accuracy may be fine for some uses, but not for supply chains or financial close. Infosys's Satinder Pal Singh suggests judging use cases by impact, feasibility and scalability. He warns that skipping governance creates a risk backlog that can force a disruptive stop and audit. Both expect agents to coordinate work across applications.
