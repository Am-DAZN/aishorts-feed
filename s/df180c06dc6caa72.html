SPLICE Helps AI Agents Learn From Tasks They Keep Failing

When an AI agent is trained with reinforcement learning (learning by trial and reward), long tasks often end in total failure. If every attempt scores zero, the training method has nothing to learn from. A common fix is to show full answers from a stronger model, but that is costly and strays far from how the agent acts. SPLICE takes a different path. In a failed attempt, a helper model picks a point and suggests a few short options from there. The agent tests each option by continuing on its own, then trains on the ones that work. Tested on software engineering (SWE-bench Verified) and deep research (BrowseComp-Plus), SPLICE beat the standard GRPO method by 2.8 to 9.8 percentage points in all four settings. It also used less helper-model computing than full-solution guidance.
