Google AI introduces Tabletop, a shared space for groups

Google AI has announced Tabletop, a tool that lets groups plan, play and do more together. According to the title, the spaces are customizable and built for collaboration, so people can shape them to fit what they want to do. The announcement was posted on the Google AI blog. No other details were provided, so it is not yet clear how Tabletop works, what features it has, or who can try it.
