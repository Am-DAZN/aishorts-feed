InfraRSI: AI Agent Speeds Up LLM Training by Up to 2.75x

Researchers built InfraRSI, an AI agent that tunes the setup used to train large language models. Training across many GPUs is fragile. A single change can run out of memory, clash with the compiler, or quietly shift the numbers. InfraRSI works in five stages. It locks down what can be edited, measures a baseline, tests changes, re-checks the best ones, and records results. It also keeps a bank of past experience that carries across different GPUs. On four training workloads, including Mixture-of-Experts models, it sped training up by as much as 2.75 times. Average hardware use rose from 17.4% to 30.0%. It beat existing tuners and coding agents. The team says this helps models improve the systems that train them, aiding larger model scaling.
