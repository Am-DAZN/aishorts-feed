AI Model Learns From Verified Results to Predict Materials Better

Finding new materials is a loop: researchers predict, test the prediction, and learn from the result. But most AI predictors are frozen after training, and they don't learn from their own outcomes. Researchers built MatMemBench, a test of 22 materials tasks that shows the true answer only after each prediction. They also built MatRecall, which lets a large language model save checked predictions, including the evidence, result and error, and look them up later without retraining. MatRecall improved median performance by 62% over a model with no memory. It beat specialized models on 11 tasks, stayed useful after feedback stopped, and carried lessons between related tasks. The authors say verified experience could become a reusable resource for self-improving discovery.
