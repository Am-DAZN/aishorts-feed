AI Search video reviews new model GPT 6.2 Astra

YouTube channel AI Search has posted a review of GPT 6.2 Astra, described as newly out. The video tests the model on many tasks. These include making an educational animation, a piano duel, 3D characters and a game, and music composition. It also covers building in Blender VR, understanding physics, spotting cancer, deep research and coming up with new ideas. The last section looks at specs, cost and benchmarks, which are standard tests used to compare AI models. The description gives no results or scores, so how well the model performs is not stated. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, and a segment shows it used with GPT 6.2 Astra.
