New Benchmark Tests If AI Can Spot How People Hide Information

Researchers have released InfoCon, a test set for AI models that handle text, audio and video. Most older tests only ask whether someone is telling the truth or lying. InfoCon looks at finer tricks people use in conversation, such as withholding facts, letting secrets slip, making things up, dodging questions, and answering an accusation with a counterclaim. It defines six strategies in all. To judge them, a model must know the facts, what each person knows, and the context. The team built labels using several AI agents that check evidence and verify each other's work. They then tested leading multimodal models to see how well they grasp these behaviors. The benchmark gives researchers a way to measure social understanding beyond a simple truth-or-lie call.
