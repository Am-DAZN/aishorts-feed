SteerDistill helps compressed AI agents match full-size accuracy

Shrinking an AI model to low-bit numbers (quantization) makes it cheaper to run, but AI agents that do multi-step tasks often fail more. The authors found these compressed agents miss task requirements or details about their environment. Their new method, SteerDistill, uses a full-precision teacher model to guide the compressed student and fix its failed answers. The teacher adjusts its guidance based on how the student's actions play out. The student is then trained to give those corrected answers without any help. Across five agent benchmarks and three models, it beat the best compressed baseline by 1.67 to 2.18 points and stayed within one point of the full-precision model. On τ^2-Bench, it beat QeRL by 1.84 percentage points in success rate while using 67% fewer training GPU hours.
