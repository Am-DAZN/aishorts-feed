OpenAI adds violence prevention safeguards to ChatGPT and promises public safety reports

OpenAI says it is strengthening how ChatGPT handles the risk of violence. The company is expanding its safeguards, which are built-in rules and checks meant to stop harm. It also plans to step in earlier when a conversation raises concern. OpenAI has made a new commitment to publish public safety reports as well, so people can see how these protections work. The post's title points to new protections for teens, though the short text provided gives no details on what those changes are. The move matters because millions of people use chatbots like ChatGPT, and safety groups and parents want clearer proof that companies are tackling serious risks.
