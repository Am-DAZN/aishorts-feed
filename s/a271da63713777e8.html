DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 to Study How Cells Read DNA

Two Minute Papers highlights a new DeepMind project called AlphaGenome 2. According to the linked blog post, it aims to decipher how cells read DNA, so researchers can better understand disease. DNA is the instruction code inside every cell, and reading it correctly is key to knowing why illness happens. The video describes this kind of work as what AI is really for. The description does not give technical details or test results, so those are best found in DeepMind's blog post. The video also links to an interview with Dr. Pushmeet Kohli, who is named as the creator.
