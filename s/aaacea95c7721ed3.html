Study finds peer reviews of the same paper increasingly repeat each other's criticisms

Peer review works best when different readers spot different problems in a paper. A new study checked how much reviewers' critiques overlap at conferences and journals. At ICLR, a top AI conference, overlap rose from 12.0% in 2026 to 21.3% in 2027. Similar year-on-year rises showed up at NeurIPS, ICML and the F1000 and PLOS journals. A third ICLR review added 5.46 new problems in 2027, down from 6.12 in 2026. The authors also used MELD, a tool that detects text written by AI language models. Reviews it flagged overlapped more with each other than unflagged reviews did. The authors say this suggests the variety of critiques on a single paper is falling.
