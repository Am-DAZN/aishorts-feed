Franklin Templeton builds AI tools with OpenAI for investment research

Franklin Templeton, an investment firm, is using OpenAI technology to support its research work. It is starting with equity research, which means studying company stocks. The firm is building AI workflows, or step-by-step automated processes, for its portfolio managers. These tools are meant to help managers compare different research views and look closely at investment risks. The source gives few other details, such as when the tools will roll out or how well they work. The effort shows how big finance firms are starting to use AI in everyday analysis.
