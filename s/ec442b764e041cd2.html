Intentful founder uses AI 'dot' to handle research and proposals

OpenAI shared a customer story about Intentful and its founder, Marina Petrova. She uses an AI assistant called a dot, named Aria, to take on everyday work. Aria researches websites and helps build proposals. It also flags messages that need attention and resolves issues without waiting to be asked. The post suggests the goal is to free up Petrova's time so she can focus on growing her business. The short source gives no numbers or other details about how well it works.
