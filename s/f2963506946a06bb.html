New MMDM Benchmark Shows AI Decision Models Struggle With Complex Choices

Researchers released MMDM, a test for AI models that make decisions in one quick step instead of writing out their reasoning. It has 5,448 questions across 16 task types, such as tracking an agent's state, picking tools, judging preferences and scoring against a rubric. Questions offer from 2 to 1,024 options, and some inputs run past 64,000 tokens (word-sized chunks of text). Most questions are new or rewritten, so models are less likely to have seen them before. The team tested 28 models. Many did well on common knowledge but struggled when options depended on each other, when rules limited choices, or when several answers were correct. Perplexity Decider-V1.2 scored 78.75% on a hard world-knowledge test, but 53.95% on option logic and 47.95% on constraint planning. The best score on hard multi-select questions was only 36.81%. The authors say these are the key areas to improve.
