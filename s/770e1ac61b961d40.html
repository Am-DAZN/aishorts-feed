MovieRoll Framework Helps AI Generate Minute-Long Videos Without Quality Loss

Making long AI videos is hard. Quality tends to fall apart past the length the model was trained on, and the memory needed to remember earlier frames keeps growing. MovieRoll is a new framework that tackles both problems. It trains a base model to generate video in chunks, and a compression method shrinks the stored memory of past frames by up to 78% while improving quality. A faster distilled version, which needs fewer steps to make each frame, is then trained on long self-generated clips. Other tricks keep generation stable beyond the training length. On the HeliosBench test, the base model stays competitive at 30 seconds after training on clips up to 15 seconds. The distilled model scored highest overall among compared methods on tests up to 60 seconds.
