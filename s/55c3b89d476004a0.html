When Words and Tone Clash, Audio AI Models Misread Emotion

Voice AI models listen to what people say and how they say it. Sometimes the two do not match, like a cheerful voice saying sad words. Researchers built a score to measure that mismatch and checked it against human judgement. They tested three audio-capable models on acted, conversational and spontaneous speech. Whenever audio was included, a bigger mismatch made errors more likely, in every dataset. Standard trained classifiers did the opposite. This suggests the models struggle to combine the evidence, not that the signal is missing. The score still worked when rebuilt without an emotion word list. The team has released its code, data tables and prompts.
