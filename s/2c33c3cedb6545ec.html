Scott Aaronson: AI Opens a New Continent of Mathematical Truth

Simon Willison shared a short quote from computer scientist Scott Aaronson about AI and math. Aaronson says we will get more than one-off answers to single questions in complexity theory, the study of how hard problems are to solve. He expects a whole new continent of mathematical truth that we had only glimpsed through fog. He compares this to van Leeuwenhoek aiming a microscope at pondwater, or Galileo aiming a telescope at the stars. In his view, our resolution into the world of complexity theory, and the wider world of math beyond it, has now improved sharply. The quote is filed under math and AI tools like large language models.
