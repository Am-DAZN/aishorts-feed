PsyWorld Predicts Human Behavior by Learning How People's States Change

Researchers introduced PsyWorld, an AI model that predicts what a person will do next. Most personalized AI agents use a fixed personality profile to pick actions. PsyWorld also learns from how a person's situation and state actually changed over time. A "world model" is an AI that learns how conditions shift from one moment to the next. The team tested it in a behavior simulator called VEET, using 48 unseen test participants. It reached 0.904 Top-1 accuracy, meaning its first guess was right that share of the time. Tests removing parts of the model showed that predicting the next state drove most of the gain. The authors say the results do not prove real psychological causes, and do not show reliable long-term predictions.
