NVIDIA Announces Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe and Granary v2 for Multilingual Transcription

NVIDIA has published a post on the Hugging Face Blog about two new releases: Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe and Granary v2. The title says they are meant for multilingual transcription, which means turning speech in many languages into written text. It also says users can prompt and adapt the system, so they may be able to guide how it works and tune it for their own needs. The source gave no further text, so details such as how well it performs, which languages it supports, or how to get it are not known. Readers should check the original post for specifics.
