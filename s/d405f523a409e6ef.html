CoFiBreak Trains AI Attackers to Find More Varied Ways to Break Chatbots

Researchers built CoFiBreak, a method for automated red teaming, where one AI is trained to trick another AI into giving harmful answers. A common problem is that the attacker keeps repeating the same few prompts, so safety tests miss other weak spots. CoFiBreak adds a reward for variety. One part pushes the attacker to use different strategies. Another part discourages reusing the same personas, framings and formats. Rewards only count for attacks that already work. On the HarmBench test, it found 1.6 to 2.0 times as many distinct successful strategies as the best baseline. On AdvBench it reached 98.8% success with 10 tries per intent. Attacks also worked on an unseen model, Claude-Opus-4.6, at 71.0%. The authors say code will be released.
