Holiday Extras uses AI 'dots' to work before staff ask

OpenAI shared a story about how travel company Holiday Extras uses AI helpers called dots. According to the post, the dots do useful work without being asked. They send launch reports on their own and flag problems that people might miss. They can also talk staff through upcoming meetings during the commute to work. The source gives few other details, such as how the dots are built or how well they perform. It points to a shift from AI that waits for a question to AI that acts on its own to support daily work.
