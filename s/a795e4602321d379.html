New Metric Shows LLM Token Use Doesn't Match Classic Problem Difficulty

Researchers asked whether the number of words, or tokens, an AI model writes while thinking tracks how hard a problem is in classic computer science terms. They show it does not. To measure difficulty from the model's view, they propose a metric called LLM Complexity. It looks at the area under a curve that plots accuracy against tokens used. Different models ranked tasks in very similar ways under this metric, which suggests it captures a stable sense of difficulty. The team used these rankings to order training data from easy to hard for fine-tuning. This raised accuracy and cut token use. On real-world tasks, the metric also matched across models better than difficulty labels written by humans.
