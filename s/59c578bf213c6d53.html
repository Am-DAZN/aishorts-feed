JVT: A Simple Transformer That Generates Video Straight From Pixels

Researchers have introduced JVT, short for Just Video Transformer. It is a model that makes video directly from raw pixels using a diffusion method, which builds a video by gradually cleaning up noise. Many earlier pixel-based systems needed complex add-ons. JVT skips them. It uses no pretrained autoencoder (a tool that compresses video first), no cascaded stages, and no U-Net-style skip connections. The team tested video patch embeddings, register tokens, and adaptive layer normalization to find a balance of quality and computing cost. JVT matched the quality of latent diffusion baselines, which work in compressed space, in both class-conditioned and unconditional tests. Because it works in pixels, it can also generate aligned data types together. In one demo, it made color video and depth-like disparity maps at once, which followed camera motion better than RGB-only baselines and gave scene geometry without a separate reconstruction model.
