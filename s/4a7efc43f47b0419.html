Review: Combining Many Scan Types With AI to Find Breast Cancer

A new review paper looks at how doctors and AI can combine different medical images to detect breast cancer. It covers more than 15 imaging methods, from common ones like mammograms, ultrasound and MRI to newer ones like thermal and microwave imaging. Each method shows something different, so no single scan gives the full picture. The review says AI can now merge this information at several stages, helping with detection, sorting tumors, outlining them, and predicting outcomes. Some systems also add patient records, lab results and genetic data. Big problems remain. There are few large, varied datasets, imaging methods differ between hospitals, some scans are often missing, and few tools are tested outside their home hospital. The authors call for clearer AI, shared standards, testing across many centers, and privacy protection.
