Google Arts & Culture launches online archive of artist Esther Mahlangu

Google has added a new online archive about the life and art of Dr. Esther Mahlangu to its Google Arts & Culture platform. The archive is free to browse on the web. It gives people a way to explore her work and her story from anywhere. The source gives few other details, such as what the archive contains or whether AI tools were used to build it. The launch shows how technology companies are using digital platforms to share and preserve the work of artists.
