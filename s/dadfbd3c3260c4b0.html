Pratchett's 'horizontal wealth': spend more on what you already love

Simon Willison shared a comment on Hacker News about Terry Pratchett's study. It recalls Pratchett's idea of "horizontal wealth." Vertical wealth means buying rich-people things, like yachts. Horizontal wealth means spending more on what you enjoyed before you got rich, so higher income does not change your tastes. If you love books, buy more books, and swap flimsy shelves for good hardwood ones. Pratchett also pointed to "blind research," which is research with no goal, done for the joy of it. The idea comes from his book A Slip of the Keyboard.
