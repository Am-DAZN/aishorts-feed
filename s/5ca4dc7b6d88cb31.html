OpenAI Introduces Business Tools Inside ChatGPT

OpenAI has announced a new feature called Businesses in ChatGPT. It lets business owners manage their business information in one place. They can also see insights about how their business shows up in ChatGPT. The goal is to help them turn interest from users into action. The announcement is short, so details such as pricing, availability and how it works have not been shared in the text provided. It matters because more people use chatbots to look for products and services, and this gives businesses a way to take part.
