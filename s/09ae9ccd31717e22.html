New AI Model Predicts When a Turkish Speaker Is Done Talking

Researchers built TurnTurk-GPT, a tool that guesses from text alone when a person has finished their turn in a Turkish conversation. They fine-tuned a Turkish GPT-2 language model on both made-up and real dialogue. It beat a widely used multilingual end-of-turn detector. It also beat a Turkish model used without extra training, but only when the test speakers had appeared in training data. On new speakers, the two were statistically tied. Cutting the endings from the last word cut accuracy by up to 44%, so verb endings seem to be the key cue in Turkish. Made-up dialogue alone gave the weakest model. The model and the test method are public.
