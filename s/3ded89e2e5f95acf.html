Microsoft Research Focus: math puzzle solved, AI translation, faster coding agents

Microsoft Research's latest Focus newsletter rounds up recent work from across the company. Researchers report they have resolved the KLS conjecture, a math problem in high-dimensional geometry that had been open for 31 years. The result removes a theoretical obstacle to understanding how well certain random-based computer methods work. It has been independently checked. The edition also covers a talk on how AI is moving closer to a universal translator, and a new approach to make AI coding agents faster and more efficient. It also features a research intern in Tokyo working on robotics and AI that acts in the physical world.
