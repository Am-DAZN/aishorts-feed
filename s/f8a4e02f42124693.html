How AI can help in-house legal teams work smarter

OpenAI has shared a video discussion on how AI can support in-house legal teams, the lawyers who work inside a company. The speakers are OpenAI's Stacey Wang and Craig Bruney, Mary O'Carroll of LECG, and Richard Segal of the law firm Cooley. They look at three ideas. First, using AI to connect business context, so legal teams better understand what the company is doing. Second, building reusable workflows, meaning set steps that can be used again for similar tasks. Third, getting more value from outside counsel, the law firms companies hire for extra help. The source gives no further detail, but the talk shows how AI is being pitched to legal departments.
