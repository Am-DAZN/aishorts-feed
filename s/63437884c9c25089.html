SEEK lets robots keep what they learn between attempts and improve

Robots powered by general AI models can adapt on the job, but they forget everything once a task ends. They may repeat the same mistakes next time. SEEK is a new framework that helps robots remember while the underlying model stays unchanged. It saves how-to steps as small programs that adjust to the current scene. It also keeps a record of what each action actually did, including failed ones. That record helps fix the programs when something new comes up. On the RoboDojo test, ten attempts raised the average task score from 27.0 to 54.4, and success from 19.7% to 48.0% across 42 tasks. On 34 tasks with GPT-6.2 Astra, SEEK scored 80.1 after five attempts, versus 49.6 for the same model acting directly, with 77.6% fewer model requests.
