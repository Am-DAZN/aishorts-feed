Hugging Face and OpenRouter team up on open-weight models

Hugging Face and OpenRouter have announced a partnership. The goal, according to the title of the Hugging Face blog post, is to make open-weight models easier to run. Open-weight models are AI models whose trained settings are shared publicly, so anyone can download and use them. The source gives no further details, such as how the tie-up works or when it starts. It suggests that people may soon have a simpler way to use these openly shared models.
