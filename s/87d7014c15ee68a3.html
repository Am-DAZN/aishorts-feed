OpenAI Says GPT-6 Astra Helped Prove Lattice Idea for Standard Model

OpenAI has published a blog post about a possible way to define the Standard Model of particle physics on a lattice. A lattice is a grid that physicists use to turn smooth space into points a computer can handle. The approach is called nonperturbative, meaning it does not rely on small-step approximations. The post says GPT-6 Astra helped turn the idea into a detailed proof. The source gives no further details on how much the AI contributed or what the proof shows. If the idea holds up, it could be a step toward a firmer mathematical footing for the theory that describes known particles and forces.
