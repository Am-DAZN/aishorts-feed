Clay adds ChatGPT and Codex to help sales teams research deals

Clay, a tool for sales and marketing teams, now works with ChatGPT and Codex, according to a post on OpenAI's blog. The integrations help Clay's users research possible deals. They also help turn raw data into go-to-market plays, which are plans for how to reach and win customers. The post says GPT-6 models handle the reasoning behind these tasks. The source gives few other details, such as pricing or how many people use it. For sales teams, the aim is to spend less time digging through data and more time acting on it.
