OpenAI updates Model Spec with five core values for its AI

OpenAI has released an updated version of its Model Spec. This is a document that describes how the company wants its AI models to behave. The new version lays out five core values. They are meant to guide more capable AI toward sound judgment, respect for human agency, and faithfulness to what people actually intend. The update matters because AI systems are getting more powerful, and clear rules about their behavior help people understand what to expect from them. The source gives only a short description, so it does not list the five values or other details of the changes.
