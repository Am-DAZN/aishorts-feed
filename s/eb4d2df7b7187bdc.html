Researchers Peek Inside AI Vision Models to Test Brain-Like 'Global Workspace' Ideas

Vision-language models read images and text together, but their answers don't show how they handle ideas inside. Researchers built a tool called MC-Lens to look at the model's middle steps and translate them into words. They tested ideas from global workspace theory, which says the brain picks information, shares it in a common space, and passes it to specialised parts. They found concept information can be read inside the models and is picked out when a task needs it. Changing those concepts changed the model's later reasoning. In training tests, the model's ability to match images and text improved before it learned to pick the right concept reliably. Similar patterns showed up across different model families and sizes. The authors say this gives a base for studying these theories in AI and improving how models link images and language.
