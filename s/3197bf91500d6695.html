NVIDIA's Vera Rubin Debuts and Tops Every MLPerf Training Test

NVIDIA's new Vera Rubin platform made its first appearance in MLPerf, an industry benchmark that compares AI systems. NVIDIA says it swept every MLPerf Training v6.1 test and was the only one with results on every benchmark. Vera Rubin NVL72 delivered up to 2x more performance per GPU (graphics chip) than NVIDIA's GB300 NVL72 system. In the new MLPerf Endpoints test for AI agents, NVIDIA reports more than 10x the work per watt of power versus GB300 NVL72 on a provisional Kimi K3 result. Power matters because data centers are limited by it, so more output per watt means more agents served. NVIDIA also says it will keep improving performance through software updates.
