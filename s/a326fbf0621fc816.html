Dyania Health's AI Reads Years of Patient Records to Help Doctors

Dyania Health, a New Jersey clinical AI company, built Synapsis AI to read through a patient's full medical record, including doctors' notes, reports and lab results. It links findings that may be years apart and flags which patients need attention and why. Each signal can be traced back to its source, and clinicians review the evidence and make every decision. The tool runs on NVIDIA computing inside a hospital's own firewall, so patient data never leaves the building. Cleveland Clinic used it in a study of more than 7,800 aortic valve replacement patients. It built a model predicting valve wear and the need for repeat procedures. The chart review would have taken clinicians years by hand.
