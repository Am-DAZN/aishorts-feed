MemOnce Gives Language Models a Leaner, Scalable Memory

Researchers introduced MemOnce, a new design for large language models that need to remember long conversations. Standard models store a lot of data for every word they read, so memory use and compute costs balloon as the text grows. Other designs squeeze history into a small fixed space but lose fine details. MemOnce saves only a small set of compact memory pieces, then rebuilds what the model needs when it needs it. It also picks the relevant memories just once instead of at every layer. In tests, adapting Qwen3.5-4B with MemOnce cut memory cache by 1313 times and sped up prefill, the step of reading the input, by 6.9 times at 1 million tokens of context. Throughput rose 3.5 times and overall memory performance improved by 5.77%.
