OpenAI plans new security sprint with Cathedral Systems to harden defenses

OpenAI says it is getting ready for its next internal security sprint. A sprint is a short, focused push to get a specific job done. This one is meant to test and strengthen the company's defenses. OpenAI is working with a partner called Cathedral Systems on the effort, and it plans to use AI as part of the testing. The source gives few details, such as when the sprint will start or what exactly will be tested. Still, it shows that OpenAI is putting work into protecting its own systems.
