OpenAI and ThinkYoung share research on AI guidance in schools

OpenAI has published new research from ThinkYoung, along with lessons from its education partnerships across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. The work looks at how to close the gap between AI tools arriving in classrooms and the guidance people have on using them. It focuses on three areas: supporting teachers, building AI literacy (knowing how AI works and how to use it well), and helping students learn independently. The source gives few other details, so the specific findings are not covered here.
