OpenAI Models Help Optimize Circuits in Jalapeño Chip and RISC-V Designs

OpenAI says its AI models helped improve chip design work. The models optimized circuits used in Jalapeño silicon, a chip. They also improved designs for RISC-V cores. RISC-V is an open standard for the basic instructions a processor follows. OpenAI links the progress to GPT-6 Astra, its model used in this work. The announcement points to AI taking on more of the hard, detailed steps of designing computer chips. The source gives few details, so it does not say how large the gains were.
