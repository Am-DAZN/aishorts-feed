OpenAI and Chile's government team up to support AI adoption

OpenAI has announced a partnership with the Government of Chile, called OpenAI for Chile. The goal is to help the country use AI in a responsible way. The plan also aims to build AI skills among public-sector workers, such as people who work in government. In addition, the partnership will help local startups create their own AI applications. The announcement gives few details beyond these three goals. It shows how AI companies are working directly with national governments to spread the use of their tools.
