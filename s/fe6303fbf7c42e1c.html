NVIDIA and Microsoft Bring Newest AI Chips and Open Models to Azure

At Microsoft Ignite, NVIDIA and Microsoft said they are widening their AI partnership. Azure data centers are now running NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems, built for large AI training and for agents that reason through tasks. The companies are also working on confidential computing, which keeps sensitive data and models protected while they are being processed. On the software side, NVIDIA's Nemotron open models are available in Microsoft Foundry, where businesses can customize them. EY adapted Nemotron to sort corporate purchasing records. It reported 55% better accuracy on its test data and 15x lower cost than proprietary frontier models. Two more Nemotron models are coming soon.
