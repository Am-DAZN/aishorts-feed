AURA Turns Speech Into Talking Avatar Video in Real Time

Many AI models can make realistic talking-head videos from audio, but they are slow. They need many processing steps and several seconds of audio ahead of time, so they do not suit voice assistants or live avatars. AURA fixes this by building video one short chunk at a time, using only the audio of the current chunk. It comes in two sizes. The 1.3B model streams 576p video in real time on one GPU, at 20.7 frames per second. The 5B model streams 720p video at 9.7 frames per second. The team trained them by distilling a slower teacher model, which means a faster student learns to copy it. AURA 1.3B runs 23x faster than its teacher, and AURA 5B runs 17x faster while matching its teacher at 720p. The researchers released both models and a dataset of 30K synthetic videos.
