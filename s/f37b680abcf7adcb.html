OpenAI details Control Room, a tool to track and stop AI workloads

OpenAI has published a post about Control Room, a central system it uses across its research infrastructure. The system keeps track of model workloads, which are the computing jobs that train or run AI models. It can also stop those jobs quickly if a safety or security concern comes up. The post does not give further details in the text provided. The idea matters because AI labs run many large jobs at once, and a single place to see and halt them gives teams a faster way to respond when something looks wrong.
