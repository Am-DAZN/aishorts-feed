Hugging Face Shares Microduck Obstacle Avoidance Workflow Using Isaac Lab and Newton

Hugging Face has published a blog post about Microduck, a robot that learns to avoid obstacles. The title says the post covers a sim2real workflow. That means the robot is trained in a computer simulation first, then moved to the real world. The post names two tools, Isaac Lab and Newton, as part of this process. No further details were available, so the exact methods and results are not known. Sim2real work matters because training robots in simulation is faster and safer than training them only in the real world.
