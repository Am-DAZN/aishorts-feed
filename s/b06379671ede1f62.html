OpenAI pledges $2 million for research on AI and children's safety

OpenAI has committed $2 million to research on how AI affects children's safety. The money will support shared tools that measure both the risks and the opportunities AI brings to young people. Shared tools mean different groups can use the same methods to check results, which makes findings easier to compare. The source gives few other details, such as who will receive the funds. The step matters because more children now use AI products, and clear ways to measure harm and benefit can help guide safer design.
