UUBench tests whether AI picks up on what users don't say

Good real-time chat with an AI needs more than understanding words. People also show what they need through tone of voice, facial expressions, gestures, and context. Researchers have released UUBench, a test that checks whether interactive AI systems notice these unspoken needs and respond well. Scoring this is hard because reading hidden intent is subjective. So the test grounds its scores in cues that can be observed, such as tone and gestures, and rates each type of need separately. It also scores how natural, fitting, and human-like a reply is, in audio, video, or both. A scoring module uses timestamped notes on what the user and the digital human did, so each judgment can be traced to evidence. The goal is to help build AI that responds to meaning beyond the literal words.
