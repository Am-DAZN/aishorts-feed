HALO Test Finds AI Helpers Often Step In Too Early or Too Much

Proactive AI agents offer help without being asked. They must decide whether to help, when, and how far to go. Researchers built HALO to test this using human judgment instead of only automated scores. It has 400 scenarios across 10 everyday areas, covering needs like dependencies, blockages, risks and opportunities. Agents must first look up evidence in databases or documents. The team collected 8,000 responses from 10 models, and 20 experts rated them. The best proprietary model was judged acceptable 72.88% of the time, and the best open-source model 56.63%. The main problem was over-eagerness. Acting too early or with too much force explained 61.20% of rejected responses. This suggests proactive agents need better judgment about when to hold back.
