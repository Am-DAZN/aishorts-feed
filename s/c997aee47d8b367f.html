TurnWise Teaches Video AI When to Speak and When to Stay Quiet

Some AI systems watch live video and must keep deciding: answer now, or keep watching? Training these models with reinforcement learning, where good behavior earns rewards, has a flaw. One score is shared across all the turns in a session. So an early success can reward repeated, pointless replies, and an early mistake can punish useful follow-ups later. Researchers propose TurnWise, a method that scores each decision on its own. It judges each turn by what happens after it, so earlier rewards do not blur the signal. It also gives short "stay silent" turns the same weight as long answers. The method needs no extra critic model or additional practice runs. The authors report it beats earlier reinforcement learning methods for streaming video understanding.
