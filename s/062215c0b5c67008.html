TabBioMed benchmark tests 23 AI models on 94 biomedical datasets

Researchers have released TabBioMed, a large test suite for AI models that work on table-style biomedical data. It brings together 94 public datasets, including health records, drug discovery, genomics and protein data. All of them go through the same preparation and testing steps, so results can be compared fairly. The team tested 23 models, from classic methods and decision-tree models to neural networks and tabular foundation models, which are large models pretrained to handle tables. Foundation models did best as a group. But their lead depended on the situation. Tree-based models and well-tuned simple neural networks stayed competitive, and often won when speed or training cost mattered. The takeaway is that the best model depends on the data, the task and the computing budget. The datasets, code, baselines and results are all public.
