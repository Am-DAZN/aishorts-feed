Researchers merge 128 aviation safety sources into one accident dataset

A new dataset called the FlightFinder Occurrence Corpus combines 279,485 aviation accident and incident records, plus 131,882 written narratives, from 128 sources. These include national investigation bodies and four global lists. Records are put in one format, and matched using a fixed order of checks, such as registration and date. Missing fields never create a match. Uncertain matches are scored and merged only if the score is high enough or a person confirms them. Each record keeps links back to its original sources. In a check of 60 automatic merges, none were wrong, but the upper error estimate is still 6%. Deterministic matches were not checked. The data has no flight-volume figures and carries the under-reporting of its sources. It is meant for cross-country safety studies and text mining.
