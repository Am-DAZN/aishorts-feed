New AI agent learns to use computers by watching and listening to tutorial videos

Researchers have built VEX, short for Video as EXperience, a way to teach AI agents to use computers. Most past methods turn tutorial videos into a series of screenshots and actions, which throws away the sound and motion. VEX keeps both. The agent watches a tutorial, listens to the spoken explanation, tries the task, then rewatches a video of its own attempt to check its progress. The team built a 9B-scale agent called video-SALMONN-VEX on top of Qwen3.5-9B. They also created a training method, AGGO, that improves the guidance drawn from videos, and added over 24K real tutorial videos to existing datasets. On the OSWorld test, the agent solved 22% more tasks than its base model, in relative terms. Its guidance also helped Gemini-3.5-flash do 10% better. The work suggests sound and video together can help computer-use agents learn and check their own work.
