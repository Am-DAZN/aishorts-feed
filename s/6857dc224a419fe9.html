Simon Willison Finds Laptop-Sized Qwen Model Can Also Read Neural Network Weights

A new paper called Sparks of Weight-Space Programming claims that large language models can read and edit the raw numbers inside another AI model. The authors say GPT-6 Astra could tell which game a network was trained on just from its weights. Developer Simon Willison tested one example from the paper. He found that Qwen 3.8 27B, running on his own laptop, can do it too. He focused on the question of what models can do without tools or code. The test fed weights from four small game-playing networks to a model and asked which game it learned. Kimi K3.1 Pro guessed Breakout correctly, after nearly ten minutes of reasoning. Critics say the paper mixes two separate ideas, so Willison treats them apart.
