Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV Heads to GeForce NOW Cloud Gaming

NVIDIA says Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV will stream on GeForce NOW when it launches on Steam on Dec. 3. GeForce NOW is a service that runs games on remote computers, so players need no big downloads. The strategy game has base building, more than 70 campaign missions, and solo or co-op play. NVIDIA also says its DLSS 5 graphics tech is coming to War Thunder and Where Winds Meet. DLSS uses AI to improve how games look. In addition, Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen is now streaming, and 10 more games join the service this week.
