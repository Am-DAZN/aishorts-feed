Google Launches Gemini Decision 1, a Model for Yes/No and Scoring Questions

Google has released "Gemini Decision 1," a new decision model API. It joins similar services from Jev and OpenAI. It supports the same three question types: yes/no, choice, and rubric scoring. It costs 10 cents per million input tokens, and output is free. That matches OpenAI's price. Jev is cheaper at 4.2 cents. Jev handles only text, and OpenAI handles text and images. Google's model also takes audio, video, and PDFs. Simon Willison tested it on a robot video. It gave 92% odds for a robot and about 5% for a dragon. Unlike its rivals, it does not answer with a flat 1.0 or 0.0.
