Paul Ford: Web Browsers Are Powerful, and Getting Cheaper to Use

Simon Willison shared a short quote from writer Paul Ford's piece, "The Web Is More Than Enough." Ford says the web browser is very powerful software for building applications. He adds that the cost of tapping that power seems to fall almost every day. In plain terms, people can build real apps that run in a browser without much expense or special tools. The quote is brief and gives no numbers or examples, but it backs the idea that the web alone is enough for many software projects.
