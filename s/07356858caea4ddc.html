Nvidia privately said chip tracking is possible, lawmaker says

Illinois Representative Bill Foster says Nvidia staff told him it could technically track its AI chips to stop smuggling to China. He said their reaction was "yeah, of course we can do this," but nothing was put in writing. An industry group that includes Nvidia had called the Chip Security Act's tracking rules "potentially infeasible." The bill would verify chip locations through inspections, company statements, or a signal the chip sends when it comes online. Nvidia did not respond to requests for comment. The issue is getting fresh attention as three chip-security bills are considered for the Senate's defense bill. Tracking would show where chips are, but it could not switch them off.
