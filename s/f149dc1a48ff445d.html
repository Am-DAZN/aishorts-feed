Lambda Uses NVIDIA's Light-Based Network Switches to Link 10,000+ GPUs

Lambda, a cloud provider, is using NVIDIA Photonics switches to connect more than 10,000 GPUs in its AI data centers. These switches put the parts that turn electrical signals into light right next to the switch chip, instead of in plug-in modules. In Lambda's tests, network delay fell about 4% at rack scale and up to about 10% at pod scale, with the same data speed as older plug-in optics. NVIDIA says the design uses up to 5x less power per bit and is up to 15x more reliable. It also needs 4x fewer lasers, and technicians can swap a failed light source without replacing the whole module. This matters because one broken network link can stall thousands of GPUs during training.
