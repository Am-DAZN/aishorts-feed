Google and Adobe bring creative tools closer to everyday Google apps

Google says it is bringing Adobe's creative and productivity tools closer to the Google apps people use every day. That is all the source says. It gives no details on which Adobe tools are included, which Google apps are involved, or when the change will arrive. The idea is to make creative and productivity work easier by putting these tools nearer to where people already work. More information is needed to know how it will work in practice.
