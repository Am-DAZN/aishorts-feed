HINT-WAM Lets Robot Models Learn From Hindsight Video of Demonstrations

Researchers introduced HINT-WAM, a new way to train world action models, which are robot AI systems that predict future video while choosing actions. Current training methods struggle. Standard fine-tuning only uses noisy copies of expert actions, and reinforcement learning needs rewards that are rare and hard to use. HINT-WAM instead lets the model learn from its own attempts. For each demonstration, the model acts as a student and generates an action. The same model then acts as a teacher that also sees the future video of the demonstration, which is hindsight. The teacher's action choices guide the student, who only sees what it would see in real use. The method needs no rewards and no simulator. It uses an energy score to match the teacher's range of actions. The authors call it the first on-policy self-distillation framework of its kind.
