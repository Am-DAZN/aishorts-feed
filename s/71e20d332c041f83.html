Together AI's TKCC Uses AI Agents to Keep Speeding Up Model Serving

Together AI built Together Compiler, or TKCC, a system that keeps tuning how AI models run as new models and traffic patterns appear. It uses AI agents that try changes and keep what works. Past tools had to trade speed of testing against accurate timings. TKCC adds an engine that avoids slow restarts and a cost model that picks good starting settings. The authors say it beats hand-written Megakernels on Llama 1B by up to 1.7x. On newer models like Kimi K3 and DeepSeek v4.1 Flash, it reached up to 2.6x higher tokens per second than the best open source engines. It also serves trillions of tokens in Together AI's production systems.
