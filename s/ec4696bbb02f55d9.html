Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability

Linear attention offers an efficient solution for long-sequence modeling via linear complexity and fixed-size recurrent states. Gated DeltaNet-2 (GDN-2) introduces independent channel-wise control over erasure and writing, enhancing state-update expressiveness and in-context retrieval, yet it suffers from training failures during long-context training. We trace these failures to the asymmetry between reading and modification directions during erasure, which yields non-normal erase operators whose directional gains accumulate across tokens, triggering exponential state growth even without new writes. To resolve this, we propose , employing symmetric erasure to ensure non-expansive state propagation and decay-regulated residual writing to preserve writing flexibility. Theoretically, we establish an O(T) upper bound on recurrent state growth while retaining chunkwise parallel computation. Experiments on ~1.6B parameter models show that achieves stable training alongside improved long-context retrieval.
