MIT Technology Review's AI Hype Index: AI Now Seems Inescapable

The latest AI Hype Index from MIT Technology Review takes a wry look at how AI has spread into daily life. It points to Google's AI Overviews, which the piece says cost the company billions in lost ad revenue. It also mentions AI-powered humanoid robots cleaning homes and an AI-assisted cyberattack at a supermarket. The piece adds pop culture touches, including a film with Andrew Garfield playing Sam Altman and a Grok-made version of The Odyssey released by Elon Musk. The writer compares modern life to the journey of Odysseus, wandering through a world full of AI. The index is a regular check on how much of the AI buzz matches reality.
