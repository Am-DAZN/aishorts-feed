NVIDIA's Operations Chief Explains How Its AI Supply Chain Gets Built

Deb Shoquist runs operations at NVIDIA, including the supply chain and manufacturing behind its AI products. She works with partners such as TSMC, Foxconn, Wistron, Amkor and Amphenol. TSMC has worked with NVIDIA since 1998. Shoquist says her job is turning designs into physical products. She looks for suppliers who are technical, financially strong and willing to try things never done before. One example is Amphenol's cable cartridge, which has 1,500 small cable legs and aims for fewer than one defect per million parts. Amphenol built over a hundred automated lines to make it. She meets key suppliers every quarter and spends six weeks a year visiting factories. She recently won the 2026 Dr. Lisa Su Woman of Innovation Award.
