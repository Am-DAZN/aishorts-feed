New method teaches AI to predict what happens next in videos, no labels needed

Researchers built Future-OPSD, a way to train vision-language models (AI that understands video and text) to anticipate future events or next actions. It learns from unlabeled videos by pairing an earlier clip with the one that follows, so no human labels or help from large outside models are required. The method also scores which parts of the future clip matter most for a decision, and it pushes the model to focus on those and ignore background detail. On open-ended prediction and closed-ended planning tasks, it improved base models by up to 14.2 and 15.9 points. General video understanding did not get worse. The authors say code will be released.
