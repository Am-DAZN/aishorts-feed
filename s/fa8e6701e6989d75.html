New System Lets AI Agents Remember Corrections Between Chats

AI agents based on large language models often forget what users teach them. People end up fixing the same mistake at the start of every new conversation. Most agent memory tools store facts, like preferences and past chats, not how to act. Researchers built a system called Forge Decision Context. It learns from human corrections and from the agent's own failed tool calls. It turns these into short, clear rules and shows them to the agent at the moment they matter. On a new test of 71 scenarios, the agent followed the rules 95% of the time, up from 55% with simple retrieval. It also used about 10 times fewer tokens, the small text units models read. The authors say how rules are written, triggered and placed matters most.
