PixelOne Teaches AI to Click Precisely on Screens in One Pass

GUI grounding means an AI finds the right button or control on a screenshot and clicks it. It needs to be accurate and fast. Shrinking screenshots hides small controls, which makes them hard to find. PixelOne works on screenshots at their original size. First, a teacher model searches step by step, zooming into likely regions. Then a student model learns from the teacher's choices and its search patterns. At use time, the student picks a visual piece of the screen and a small extra part pinpoints the exact pixel, all in a single pass. The largest version, PixelOne-Pro-35B-A3B, scored 83.8% average accuracy across four benchmarks. That is the best among the single-pass models tested. It took 0.43 seconds per target on average.
