TOUMAII Releases 4.5 Million Examples to Teach AI Better Tool Use

AI agents often need to call tools, such as software interfaces or databases, to finish tasks. Even a small formatting slip can break a call. Researchers have released TOUMAII, a dataset of about 4.5 million pairs that each show a good tool call next to a bad one. Training on such pairs is called direct preference optimisation, and good data for it has been scarce. The pairs come from an existing dataset called Toucan-1.5M and cover single and multi-turn chats. The team also shared TOUMAII-Gen, a tool that builds these pairs from other tool-calling datasets, and a smaller set made from ToolACE. A 3-billion-parameter model trained on the data matched results from other strong preference datasets. Code and data are public.
