Developers use AI agents and NVIDIA Omniverse to build simulations from plain-language instructions

NVIDIA showed how developers pair advanced AI models with its Omniverse tools to build simulations. Developers give an AI agent instructions in everyday language, then review its results and guide changes. Omniverse supplies the physics, rendering and sensor simulation. Falkor, a KONGSBERG company, turns engineering data into digital twins, which are virtual copies of facilities, so engineers can test plans before going on site. NVIDIA developers built a robot pick-and-place simulation in Isaac Sim. Community developer Michael Wagner made a voice-controlled VR scene over a weekend. These examples show how AI agents can cut the manual work of building and testing simulations.
