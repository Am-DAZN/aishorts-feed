NVIDIA Shows Developers Building Simulations by Directing AI Agents

NVIDIA described how developers are pairing advanced AI models, such as GPT-6 Astra, with its Omniverse software to build simulations. Developers give instructions in plain language, then review results and guide changes. Omniverse supplies the physics, rendering and sensor simulation. One project turned site scans into an editable warehouse model, so layouts can be tested before physical changes. Another built a what-if tool for a gas turbine, letting engineers change load, temperature or fuel and see the effects, including faults like a stuck fuel valve. A third assembled a kitchen scene from NVIDIA's SimReady library of more than 2,000 ready-made 3D assets. The examples show AI agents taking on much of the setup work in simulation.
