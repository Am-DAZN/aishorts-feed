New Method Helps AI Models That See and Read Forget Private Data

Multimodal AI models, which handle both text and images, can memorize private information from their training data and repeat it. Removing that information, known as unlearning, is hard. Many methods hurt the model's overall usefulness or fail to forget enough. Researchers found two causes. Training targets that still hold more private information lead to weaker forgetting. Targets that differ more from the model's original outputs cause more damage to its abilities. Their new method, called FPD, starts from the model's own original outputs. It then tweaks the parts of the network linked to private information, in both text and images. This creates training targets with less private data and less change. On the S-MLLMUn Bench test, FPD balanced forgetting and usefulness better than existing methods.
