OpenAI outlines how it manages risk in its most advanced AI models

OpenAI has published a blog post on how it handles the risks of its most advanced AI systems, often called frontier AI. The company says it works on safety from the time a model is trained until it is released to the public. The post names four main tools. Model alignment aims to make the AI behave the way people intend. Sandboxing keeps the AI in a restricted space so it cannot affect other systems. Blocking safeguards stop harmful requests or actions. Monitoring watches how the AI is used after launch. The post matters because it shows the steps one major AI lab says it takes to keep powerful systems under control.
