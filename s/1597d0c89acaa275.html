AI's hidden cost: the storage left behind by every prompt

When people think about AI costs, they picture GPUs, the chips that power models. But data storage is a growing expense that often gets missed. Every time a model answers a request, it leaves data behind, such as logs, cached context, backups and safety records. Western Digital (WD) measured one 8-second AI video. The user got a 3.97MB file, but more than 500MB of data stayed on the back end. That is about 130 times larger. McKinsey expects inference demand to grow 35% a year through 2030, faster than training at 22%. A WD-sponsored IDC survey of 763 decision makers found nearly 95% saw data volumes rise because of generative AI. The article argues that budgets focused only on compute may be too narrow, since slow data access can leave costly GPUs idle.
