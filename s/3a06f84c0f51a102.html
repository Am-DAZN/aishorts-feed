OpenAI to buy Pierre Computer Company to boost Codex

OpenAI has announced plans to acquire Pierre Computer Company, Inc. The deal is meant to expand the tools in Codex, OpenAI's coding product. Those tools help people build code, understand it, and review it. The announcement is short and gives no price, deal terms, or timeline. It shows OpenAI wants Codex to do more than write code, and to help developers read and check it too.
