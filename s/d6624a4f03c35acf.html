StrideWorld tests whether AI-generated worlds keep their shape and steps steady

Interactive video world models turn a user's commands into a generated scene you can explore. For this to work, the scene must keep its 3D shape as the camera moves, and each command must move the camera by a steady amount. A new benchmark called StrideWorld checks both. Older tests compared a video against references built from that same video, which can hide slow warping or changes in pace. StrideWorld fixes its references first. In tests on open models from 140 start views, all but one warped their scenes as the camera moved. Many stalled on multi-step routes. Fine-tuning two models on a few rendered clips removed most stalls. Code, data and a leaderboard are public.
