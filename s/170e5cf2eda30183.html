Looks Matter in Word Learning, But Teaching Examples Matter Too, Study Finds

Researchers asked why people give two objects the same name. They reanalyzed data from an earlier study of 520 adults, measuring how visually close each test image was to the examples shown. They used DINOv2, an AI model that learns to see images without labels. The farther an image was from the examples, the less likely adults were to use the same word for it. The examples also mattered. After three examples, adults applied a word to 1.1% of broad-category items if the examples were narrow, but 77.5% if the examples were broad. An AI learner also struggled more when two joined categories were far apart. Its naming accuracy fell from 84.7% to 53.0%. The AI's distances matched human similarity ratings fairly well. But this did not clearly show that human-based distances predict how hard the AI finds a word.
