Datasette-charts adds annotations to mark up your charts

Simon Willison has released datasette-charts 0.3a0, an early alpha version of his charting plugin for Datasette, a tool for exploring data. The new version lets you add annotations to a chart after it is drawn. You can use lines, shaded areas, labels, or a mix of all three. You can try it in Datasette Lite and change the annotations with the chart builder screen. If you also install Datasette Agent, the agent can build annotated charts for you. Willison showed this on the agent.datasette.io demo. He asked it to compare his tagged content about LLMs with his tagged content about Datasette, with annotations. Annotations make charts easier to read by pointing out what matters.
