New Benchmark Shows AI-Controlled Robots Often Cause Harm; LEASH Safety Layer Cuts Risk

Researchers say using big AI models like GPT and Claude to control robots carries serious safety risks, and good tests were missing. They built RoboRisk, a benchmark with 92 simulated tasks and 13 real-robot tasks. It covers five kinds of risk, including refusing bad tasks, fixing unsafe plans, hazards during action, injected instructions, and memory over several steps. Six leading models generally showed high rates of harm. The team also proposed LEASH, a safety add-on that checks the task, each action, and each irreversible step against a shared safety policy. In tests, LEASH reduced harm across all six models, kept most of their task success, and added little time and cost.
