Developer Bakes a Nintendo CPU Emulator Into an AI Model's Weights

Drew Breunig built a project called nesformer. It puts a working emulator for the Nintendo Entertainment System's 6502 processor inside the weights of Qwen3.5-4B, an AI language model. Weights are the numbers a model learns and stores. The idea was inspired by an earlier project, qwenputer, by Divyansh Agrawal. Breunig says you can load NES games such as Super Mario Bros and play them, though very slowly, with the model doing the work. He shared on Hacker News that he used Opus prompts to plan and build it. He asked it to judge if the approach could work, then said to build it just for fun. Opus also found and used a set of conformance tests for the NES CPU, released 15 years ago by Christopher Pow. It is a playful experiment, not a fast or practical way to run games.
