Flipsnack uses ChatGPT Ads to boost signups and cut costs

Flipsnack, a company that makes tools for creating digital publications, ran ads inside ChatGPT. The company set up its campaigns to focus on one goal: getting people to try its product. This approach is often called conversion optimization. According to OpenAI's blog, the result was more registrations. The cost of each signup also went down. The source gives no exact figures, so the size of the gains is not clear. The story shows how businesses are starting to test ChatGPT Ads as a way to find new customers.
