RynnWorld-Latent Teaches One Video Model to Predict Actions Across Many Robots

Robots use different control systems, and human videos come with no control labels. That makes it hard to train one model on all of them. RynnWorld-Latent tackles this with "latent actions," a shared way to describe movement that works for any body. The model was pretrained on 23 datasets, about 553.5 million video frames, drawn from human videos, robot demos and simulations. It can also create synthetic training demos in two ways. Tests showed better video prediction, and the model followed different action sequences from the same starting scene. Training with its synthetic data raised policy success rates on LIBERO and LIBERO-Plus, and on real robots. The authors say this shared action format could help models learn how the physical world responds across many kinds of robots.
