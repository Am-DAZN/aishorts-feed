Le Figaro partners with OpenAI to bring its news to ChatGPT

Le Groupe Figaro, the French media company behind Le Figaro, has teamed up with OpenAI, the maker of ChatGPT. Under the deal, ChatGPT will show summaries and short excerpts of Le Figaro's journalism. Each one will come with a link to the original story, so readers can click through and read the full article on the publisher's site. The source gives no other details, such as financial terms or a start date. The partnership shows how AI chatbots and news publishers are working together to bring reporting to people who ask questions in chat tools.
