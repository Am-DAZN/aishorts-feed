Korean firms team with NVIDIA to build safer AI agents

AI agents are programs that carry out tasks on their own. NVIDIA says they need strong open models and security. Hyundai Motor Group is exploring NVIDIA's NemoClaw blueprints and its OpenShell runtime. OpenShell is a secure space where agents run with limits on data and tools. Korean security firm AIM Intelligence is linking its Starfort platform to OpenShell. The aim is to catch malicious instructions and data leaks. It is also weighing a early test for the public energy sector. Separately, LG AI Research is opening API access to K-EXAONE-2.5. It was built using NVIDIA Nemotron datasets, including Nemotron-Personas-Korea, an open set of 7 million synthetic Korean personas based on official Korean statistics.
