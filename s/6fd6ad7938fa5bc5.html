OpenAI raises security bug bounty rewards and adds new bonuses

OpenAI is paying more to outside researchers who find security flaws in its systems. The company is raising rewards in its Security Bug Bounty program. It is also adding new bonuses. These include extra pay for high-quality reports, for researchers who keep contributing over time, and for work on priority campaigns. A bug bounty is a program that rewards people for reporting software weaknesses so they can be fixed. Bigger rewards may encourage more independent researchers to look for problems and report them responsibly.
