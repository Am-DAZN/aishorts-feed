Scott Wiener wins Pelosi's seat and plans to push for AI rules

Scott Wiener, a California state senator who wrote major AI safety bills, has won the San Francisco congressional seat held by Nancy Pelosi. He could now help shape federal AI policy. Wiener wrote SB 1047, which Governor Gavin Newsom vetoed, and SB 53, a lighter bill Newsom signed. He says a new report from the nonprofit METR worries him. Many read it as a sign Anthropic reached recursive self-improvement, where AI helps speed up its own progress. Wiener wants a strong federal AI regulator, whistleblower protections, testing and audits, and a kill switch requirement. He opposes broad federal limits on state AI laws. A group backed partly by Anthropic gave his campaign over $3 million, which drew criticism. He rejects the claim that he is too close to Big Tech.
