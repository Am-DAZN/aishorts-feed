Open recipe trains one vision-language model from several expert teachers

Researchers released M3OPD-Reasoner, a fully open recipe for teaching AI models that handle images and video to reason better. It uses multi-teacher on-policy distillation. In plain terms, several expert teacher models each master one area, and a single student model learns from them by studying its own answers. The three areas are image science and math, image perception, and video. Starting from Qwen3.5-9B, the team found that this approach beat training one model on all the mixed data at once, across 21 benchmarks. They then ran more reinforcement learning on the student with the same prompts, and it kept improving without breaking down. That produced their main model, M3OPD-Reasoner-9B. The team is releasing all data, training code, and checkpoints from every stage.
