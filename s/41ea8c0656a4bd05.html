SynthCorp Builds Fake Company Data to Test AI Data Agents

Researchers have built SynthCorp, a platform that creates test environments for AI data agents, which are programs that use large language models to answer questions from business data. Existing tests get maxed out quickly and can't target specific skills. SynthCorp simulates business events over time, like routine work and disruptions, to produce databases, documents and a data catalog that stay consistent. It then writes tasks and checked answer programs from a log of everything that happened. The team made seven data estates, with up to 1,000 tables, and 256 tasks across five domains. Tests on three models showed the benchmarks could tell model tiers apart. Some multi-event tasks exposed reasoning failures in top agents. The authors hope others use it to build harder tests.
