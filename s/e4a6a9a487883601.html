LambdaMix lets one AI model shift between goals like helpfulness and safety

Language models often must balance competing goals, such as being helpful and being harmless. Usual training locks in one fixed balance. Researchers introduce LambdaMix, a method that trains a single model to handle many balances. It adds one small add-on module, called a low-rank adapter, for each goal on top of a frozen base model. A preference setting picks how the adapters are mixed and how rewards are weighted during training. After one training run, users can change the balance at use time without retraining. Tests on four models covered helpfulness versus harmlessness and math with instruction following. LambdaMix beat all compared baselines on a combined score called hypervolume. It also used fewer training samples and stored fewer adapter parameters than separate specialist models.
