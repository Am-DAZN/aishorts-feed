Hugging Face Blog Covers Token Pooling Training for Multi-Vector Embedding Models

Hugging Face has published a blog post titled "Token Pooling-Aware Training for Multi-Vector Embedding Models." Only the title is available, so details are limited. Multi-vector embedding models turn a piece of text into many small number lists, one per token (a word or word piece), instead of a single list. Token pooling is a way to merge some of those lists to save space. The title suggests the post is about training models with that merging in mind. The full article is needed to confirm the method or any results.
