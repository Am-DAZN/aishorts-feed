Dementia Research Group Uses NVIDIA AI to Tap Hidden Data

About 57 million people live with dementia, and no treatment yet stops or reverses Alzheimer's. Researchers face too much data, too few experts to review it, and slow progress. A group called C-BRAIN, led by Dr. Randall Bateman and made up of 18 institutions, launched in November 2025 to speed things up with AI. It is working with NVIDIA. The group wants to use "dark data," such as unpublished results and private data that AI tools have never seen. To protect privacy, AI models learn from data at each institution without the data leaving it. C-BRAIN runs 64 NVIDIA GPUs, which are powerful computer chips, plus cloud systems on Microsoft Azure. It has built two tools so far, OpenScientist and InsightEngine.
