Credit Saison reports 954% ROI from ChatGPT Enterprise pilot

Credit Saison, a financial services company, tested ChatGPT Enterprise in a pilot program. According to OpenAI's blog, the pilot delivered a 954% return on investment, or ROI. That is a measure of how much value a company gets back compared with what it spent. The post says company leaders and employees both played a part in turning those results into companywide adoption. The source gives few other details, such as how the savings were counted or which tasks improved. Still, the story shows how a small trial can grow into wider use of AI tools at work.
