OpenAI explains how it watches for misbehaving advanced AI models

OpenAI has published a blog post about how it detects misalignment in its most advanced AI models. Misalignment means a model acts in ways its creators did not intend. The company says it uses layered monitoring, which means several checks working together rather than a single safeguard. These checks also look for unauthorized behavior by AI agents, which are systems that take actions on their own. The goal is to alert responders early so they can step in before problems grow into serious incidents. The post matters because AI systems are getting more capable and independent, and catching bad behavior early is a key part of keeping them safe.
