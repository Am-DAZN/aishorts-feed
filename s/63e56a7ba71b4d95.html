SplatAnything Fixes Scale Confusion in Fast 3D Scene Reconstruction

Researchers have proposed SplatAnything, a method for building 3D scenes from photos in a single pass. These systems use 3D Gaussian splatting, which models a scene as many small fuzzy blobs called Gaussians. The team found a flaw in current approaches. They size the blobs using a measure based on distance from the center, but the gaps between neighboring blobs still change a lot with scene size and camera setup. This confuses the training signal. Their fix, called Gaussian Spacing Normalization, uses a steady measure of blob spacing instead. The team also built a new benchmark with varied datasets, different numbers of input photos, and hard camera angles. They report top results, especially when scene scale and viewpoint change a lot.
