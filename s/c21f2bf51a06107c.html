New open-source platform lets researchers safely study kids chatting with AI

Children and teens are using chatbots more and more, but we know little about how this affects them. Most studies so far rely on surveys, because watching real chats raises worries about safety, privacy and consent. A new paper introduces AYO (AI Youth Open Science), a free, open-source platform built to solve this. It has a child-friendly interface, safety limits that can be set by age, tools for researchers to monitor chats, and privacy-protecting data practices. Researchers can use it to study how kids trust AI, judge what is true, and stay curious over time. The goal is to produce evidence that helps shape safer AI, better teaching practices, and sound policies for children growing up with AI.
