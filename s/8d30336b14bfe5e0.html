Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Its Cheapest Model, but Pricing Confuses

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the lowest-cost model in its Gemini 4 family. Developer Simon Willison released llm-gemini 0.37, his tool's update to support it. Hydrogen costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. That is only 25% below the larger Carbon and Argon models, which cost $2 and $10 for now. Their prices double on December 1, when Hydrogen's discount grows to 75%. Willison notes it still costs about 15 times more than Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna. Google says Hydrogen is built for fast everyday coding work and is the new free tier model. It will also power AI features in Google Search.
