Check Point uses OpenAI Daybreak to speed up vulnerability research

Security company Check Point is using OpenAI Daybreak to move faster on the research behind its new protections. According to OpenAI's blog, the tool helps Check Point's analysts turn vulnerability intelligence, which is information about weaknesses in software, into defenses for customers more quickly. The post gives few other details here. It matters because the faster a security firm can act on a known weakness, the sooner its customers can be protected.
