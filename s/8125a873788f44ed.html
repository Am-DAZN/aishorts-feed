Mamba's Market Regime Skill Vanishes Once Data Leaks Are Fixed

Researchers studied how Mamba, an AI model for time series, understands stock market moods like bull, bear and sideways. An earlier setup seemed to find these regimes almost perfectly, with an F1 score (a accuracy measure) of 1.00. The new paper shows that was a leak. Some inputs overlapped with how the labels were defined, and an extra training head gave hints. With those removed, the score fell to 0.398. A simple logistic baseline scored 0.435, and an untrained Mamba scored 0.364. The remaining effect in the model's selective gate was detectable but too small to matter for money. The authors offer a repeatable leakage-control method for testing such models and a small set of features that survive the checks.
