Decoupage lets AI direct long, multi-shot videos with separate sound and picture timing

Researchers built Decoupage, a system that makes videos with sound from a screenplay. In films, audio and picture often change at different times. A character's voice can carry over a cut, or a scene can fall silent. Current AI tools struggle with this over longer stories. They may invent speech during silences or move the mouths of people who are only listening. Decoupage handles picture and sound as two separate tracks, tied to consistent characters. It also has a lip-sync feature that stops speech from driving faces when a speaker is off-screen. On tests with multi-shot conversations, it controlled shot changes, audio timing and lip sync well. It produced coherent clips of 15 to 60 seconds.
