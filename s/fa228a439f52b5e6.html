ProLand Offers a New Way to Fine-Tune AI Models With LoRA

Researchers have introduced ProLand, a new method for fine-tuning large AI models with LoRA. LoRA is a popular shortcut that adjusts only a small part of a model, which saves computing power. One known problem is that LoRA can waste effort on repeated, overlapping directions. A fix is to keep those directions orthogonal, meaning independent of each other. ProLand does this without forcing a strict correction at every step. It lets the model drift slightly off the rule, then steadily pulls it back. It also borrows Muon-style update directions to improve learning. The team tested it on 11 tasks, using DeBERTa-v3-base, Qwen2.5-3B, Llama-3.2-3B and Moonlight-16B-A3B at several adapter sizes. In every tested model and size setup, ProLand had the highest average score among the compared methods.
