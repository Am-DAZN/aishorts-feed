NVIDIA's AI Day Seoul draws 2,000 to focus on robots and AI agents

NVIDIA held AI Day Seoul at COEX over two days, with more than 2,000 people attending. The event focused on physical AI, meaning AI that powers robots in the real world, and AI agents, which are programs that act on tasks. NVIDIA's Deepu Talla said Korea is "punching above its weight," citing firms like Doosan Robotics, Hyundai Motor Group, LG Electronics and SK Group. Developer groups ran a bootcamp, and a hackathon asked teams to build agents trained on Korean language and culture. The winner, FlyGate, got an NVIDIA DGX Spark system. Over 500 developers joined training workshops and certification exams in Korean. Ten startups also pitched their robotics and AI work. Sessions will soon be available online.
