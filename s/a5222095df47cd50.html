AI Agents Edit a Model's Weights Directly, Gaining Big Boosts

Researchers studied Weight-Space Programming, where an AI agent looks at a neural network's internal settings (its parameters) and changes them directly, instead of retraining the model the usual way. They ran 16 editing attempts on seven tests, using two Qwen3.5 models. Small edits sometimes helped a lot. Changing just 1,023 parameters raised accuracy on MATH-500 by 29.45 points. A model edited for math also did better on three other tests, though one coding test did not change. But results were uneven. In six of the 16 attempts, the agent could not confirm a real improvement, so the original weights were kept. The work shows promise for tiny, targeted model fixes, and also shows that finding reliable edits is still hard.
