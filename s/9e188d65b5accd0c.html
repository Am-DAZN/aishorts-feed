Mathematician says he and Claude found proof of Riemann Hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician, says he worked with Anthropic's AI model Claude and believes they have proven the Riemann Hypothesis, one of math's best-known unsolved problems. He says the method also extends to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, and he expects it may reach the Extended version with more time. He adds that he and Claude have formalized the arguments without assumptions, meaning written them in a strict, checkable form. He says Claude far outpaced him near the finish. These are his own claims, shared in a short remark, and the source does not say whether others have checked the proof.
