New method makes AI 3D view synthesis up to 23x faster

Some AI models can create new views of a scene from a few photos without building a 3D model first. The best ones, called decoder-only view synthesis models, give great quality but are slow to render. A faster alternative, called Test-Time Training, saves computing power but lowers image quality. Researchers now propose Test-Time Baking. It teaches a small, light neural network to copy what the slower attention step does, rather than replacing that step. It also separates camera information from image content so the small network handles new views better. The team reports up to 23 times faster rendering, with quality on par with the best current methods across several benchmarks.
