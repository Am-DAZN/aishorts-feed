Frozen AI Model Learns Natural Back-and-Forth Talking With Little Data

Full-duplex voice systems listen while they speak. They can say "uh-huh," give up the floor, or be interrupted. Today they usually need hundreds of thousands to millions of hours of speech to train. This paper shows a cheaper way. The researchers keep a general-purpose multimodal language model frozen and train only small add-on parts, about 97 million parameters. The conversion takes about 30 GPU-hours. The system decides every 80 milliseconds whether to speak, pause, or yield. In tests on Full-Duplex-Bench and InteractionBench, it matched open-source systems trained on far more speech. It was least likely to cut in when a user just paused, and it backchanneled most often. It also led in spoken-task accuracy and coherence over long chats.
