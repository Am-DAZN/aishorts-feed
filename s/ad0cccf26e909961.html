Direct3D-V3 Generates 3D Shapes Without Compressing Them First

Most 3D generators first squeeze a shape into a compact code, then create new shapes from that code. Direct3D-V3 skips this step. It works directly on raw voxel grids, which are 3D grids of tiny cubes that store distance to a surface. Dropping the compression step removes a bottleneck that can lose fine detail. The authors say the meshes show richer local detail. The model adds a rule to avoid surfaces with holes. It also learns to guess the camera angle of the input image, which helps the 3D shape match the picture. The method can also rebuild 3D shapes from views, filling in the parts that are not visible.
