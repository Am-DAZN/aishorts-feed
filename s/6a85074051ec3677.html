Why business AI needs context to make good decisions

MIT Technology Review's Business Lab, made with Celonis, looks at why company AI often falls short. Devavrat Shah, Celonis's chief scientist of enterprise AI, says AI agents need to know how a business really works. Without that, they can miss limits, make costly mistakes, or waste computing power. He suggests adding a layer of business context, set guardrails, and human review of recommendations. Companies should start with one specific decision, find its hardest steps, and test whether AI helps. Gains in speed and value can show if it is worth scaling. Shah says this could free staff for more strategic work, but companies must train employees to understand AI outputs instead of simply handing over decisions.
