AI Is Overwhelming Local Offices With Public Records Requests

Town offices across the US are seeing more public records requests, and AI is part of the reason. In Panton, Vermont, clerk Kyle Rowe went from one request a year to monthly ones from companies. Venture-backed startups have raised over $100 million to collect government data and turn it into AI-powered sales signals. AI tools also make it easier for people to write requests, which tend to be longer and broader. Many places have seen double-digit yearly increases, but staff and budgets often have not grown. Some states are now passing laws that make it easier to deny requests, which could make government less open.
