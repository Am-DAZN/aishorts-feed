GameRealm Turns Video Games Into a Giant Training Dataset for Vision AI

Researchers have released GameRealm, a large dataset built from video game footage to help train AI models that understand images and video. It holds over 120 million frames and 1,000 hours of gameplay. Each frame comes with extra data straight from the game, such as depth (how far away things are), camera settings, sound, player controls, and 3D point paths. It includes single-view, stereo, multi-view, and panoramic recordings. The team collected the footage using both human players and AI agents that explore games on their own. In tests, adding GameRealm improved visual foundation models at generating images and at learning 3D geometry. The authors say existing training data is scattered and labeled unevenly, and this dataset aims to fix that.
