NVIDIA and Korea Deepen Partnership on Homegrown AI Chips and Skills

NVIDIA and Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT are widening their work on Korea's "sovereign AI," meaning AI built and run within the country. The effort covers chips, data centers, training, startups, AI models and security. Korean makers of AI processors could explore joining NVLink Fusion, NVIDIA's system for linking different chips together. NVIDIA will also offer training, certification, hackathons and seminars to help people use GPUs better. Through Korea's AI for All program, it will work with three consortia and four Korean model builders, using Dynamo software to run AI models faster and at larger scale. NVIDIA also announced a project with SK Telecom and Rebellions, backed by the ministry, to build AI infrastructure for Korean models.
