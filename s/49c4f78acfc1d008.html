VidUEU-Agent Turns Raw Videos Into Training Data for Multimodal AI

Training AI that works with images and video needs lots of good instruction data, and making it by hand is costly. Researchers built VidUEU-Agent, an automated system that creates this data from raw videos. It works in four stages: it filters out poor-quality shots, picks key frames and other signals, builds descriptive metadata, and uses that to write training samples. The agent can also adjust its data mix based on how models perform after training. Using it, the team built VidUEU-100K, a dataset of 100,000 samples covering many topics and tasks, including understanding, editing, and combined tasks. They report that fine-tuning baseline models on this dataset gave steady performance gains across several benchmarks. The work points to a cheaper way to get quality data.
