Google lets users link Wallet, Drive and Gemini to personalize AI Mode in Search

Google is adding a way to make Search answers more personal. People can choose to connect Google Wallet, Google Drive, and the Gemini app to a feature called Personal Intelligence in AI Mode, Google's AI-powered way of answering questions in Search. The goal is more helpful responses based on a user's own information. The connection is optional. Users must opt in, and Google says the link is made securely. This matters because it shows Google moving Search toward answers shaped by each person's own data, while leaving the choice to share it with the user.
