Kazakhstan Builds Up AI Computing as Nvidia's Jensen Huang Visits Astana

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang visited Astana on Oct. 23. He met President Tokayev and joined a public talk with Kazakhstan's AI minister, Zhaslan Madiyev. Madiyev said the country wants AI that speaks Kazakh and understands its culture. Huang said Kazakhstan could turn its energy into intelligence and export it. He also said a country's data is its natural resource. Nvidia announced an investment in Higgsfield AI, a startup founded in Kazakhstan. On infrastructure, a company called Firebird signed an investment deal with the government. It has a U.S. export license for 45,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell chips. The first phase is being built with Kazakhtelecom, aiming to launch commercially in the third quarter of 2027.
