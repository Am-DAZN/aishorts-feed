Cisco teams up with OpenAI models to speed up security defenses

Cisco is using OpenAI's AI models together with its own network intelligence, the data it gathers about how traffic moves across company networks. The goal is to make security for businesses stronger and quicker to react. In a post on OpenAI's blog, the two companies describe how Cisco is building defenses that can adapt as threats change. The source gives few details on specific tools or results. It does show how security vendors are turning to AI to keep pace with fast-moving attacks.
