TwinD Detector Spots Fully AI-Generated Faces That Older Deepfake Tools Miss

Most deepfake detectors learn from fakes where a generated face is pasted into a real video frame. Newer tools create the whole face from scratch, leaving no pasting traces. On a test of 19 datasets, the best earlier detector scored 96.2% on pasted fakes but no more than 78.1% on fully generated faces. TwinD is a new detector that trains on extra 'twin' fakes made from real frames. One twin is a real frame rebuilt by an autoencoder, a type of neural network, with some real pixels put back. The other blends an altered copy of a frame with the original. TwinD scored 96.3% on average, 8.9 points above the previous best, and 95.6% on fully generated faces. Models trained on twins alone still averaged 94.3%. The authors have released the code, model and training data.
