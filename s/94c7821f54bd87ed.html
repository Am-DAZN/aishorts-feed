INCLUDE-2.0 Tests AI on Local Knowledge Across 91 Languages

Researchers released INCLUDE-2.0, a new test for how well AI language models handle questions in many languages. It covers 91 languages from 86 countries and has over 220,000 questions taken from native regional sources. Older tests often mix up two things: knowing a language and knowing facts about a region. This one separates them. Each question is labeled by the kind of regional knowledge it needs and comes with an English translation. The team found that average scores hide big differences between languages and regions. Top models did about 14.5 points worse on knowledge found mainly in local sources than on well-documented facts about a place. For open models, bigger size helped accuracy but did not reliably end the gap between English and the original language. The dataset is free to download.
