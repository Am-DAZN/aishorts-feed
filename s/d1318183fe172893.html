Shield uses OpenAI agents to take over legacy portal tasks for IT technicians

Shield, a company working with OpenAI, has built AI agents called Forge to help IT technicians. Many technicians must click through old, hard-to-use web portals to get work done. Forge agents now move through those systems on their behalf. This lets technicians hand off the routine portal steps. They can then spend their time on problems that need human judgment. The source gives few details, such as how the agents work or how well they perform. It shows how AI agents are being used to handle tedious software tasks in everyday IT work.
