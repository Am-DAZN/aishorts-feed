Researchers Explain How ChatGPT Helped Disprove a Famous Network Flow Conjecture

Goemans guessed that any flow split across many paths could be turned into a single-path flow without extra cost, and with each link's load rising by at most the largest demand. Dmitry Rybin recently used ChatGPT to find a seven-point example that proves this wrong. But that find did not explain why it worked. This paper builds a step-by-step method to construct such counterexamples. It starts with a simple tree-like network and adds shortcut links. The method shows exactly which demands, flows and costs make the guess fail. When told to follow it, ChatGPT found valid counterexamples starting from 93 other tree shapes. The authors also explain why the original example was hard to find by blind search.
