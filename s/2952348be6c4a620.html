NVIDIA RTX Spark Laptops Arrive With New Speech Model and Agent Controls

NVIDIA says thin, light Windows laptops with its RTX Spark chip are now available from ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft and MSI. The chip puts a graphics unit and a processor together, with up to 128GB of shared memory. NVIDIA also released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open speech model. One model can write a transcript, label speakers, translate and answer questions about audio in 40 languages. It ranks first on the Hugging Face Open ASR board. A new setting in the NVIDIA App lets users decide whether a game or a background AI agent gets more graphics power. Liquid AI's LFM3 open models also run on the laptops.
