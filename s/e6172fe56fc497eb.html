Researchers Recreate Model Steering Using Only Text Added to the Prompt

Activation steering nudges an AI language model by changing its internal values as it writes. That needs deep access to the model. This paper offers a way around it. The authors built ActGCG, a method that searches for a short piece of text, called a suffix, that makes the model act as if it had been steered. They tested two goals: making the model answer in German, and making it stop refusing requests, which is a jailbreak. For German, the suffix matched the steering almost perfectly and worked across prompts and models. For refusals, results were weaker and only partly held up under a stricter test called HarmBench. The work suggests a steering effect can be reached from the input alone, though how well it works depends on the behaviour being steered.
