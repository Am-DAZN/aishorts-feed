OpenAI says ChatGPT and Codex can speed up engineering work

OpenAI has published a blog post about how its tools, ChatGPT and Codex, are being used by engineers who design physical things. The post points to several kinds of work that can move faster with AI help. These include CAD, which is software for drawing 3D parts, and PCB design, which is the layout of circuit boards. It also covers simulation, where computers test how a design might behave before anything is built. The post also mentions other workflows across the physical world. It shows OpenAI is looking beyond software and writing tasks to hardware and engineering fields.
