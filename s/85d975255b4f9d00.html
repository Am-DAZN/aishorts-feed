Hugging Face Post Announces Open Speech and Decision Models for Apple Platforms

Hugging Face has published a blog post titled "Introducing AnySpeechRecognizer and AnyDecisionModel: Open Models for Speech and Decisions on Apple Platforms." From the title, the post presents two open models. One appears to handle speech recognition, which means turning spoken words into text. The other appears to be for making decisions. Both are aimed at Apple platforms. The source gave no article text, so details such as how the models work, who built them, how well they perform, and how to get them are not known. Open models are ones that people can generally use and build on, so this could matter to developers making apps for Apple devices.
