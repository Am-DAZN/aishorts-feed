Google Maps adds new Ask Maps tools for holiday travel

Google has added new features to Ask Maps, the part of Google Maps where people can ask questions in everyday language. The update is aimed at holiday travel and preparation. Users can plan road trips, get tips about their routes, and find local appointments. They can also search their saved lists of places. The source gives few other details, such as when the features arrive or where they will be available. The update shows Google working to make its map app more helpful for busy trips and errands.
