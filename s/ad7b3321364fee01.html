SleuthVLM Understands Videos Without Hearing Audio, Beating Omni Models

Many AI models answer questions about videos by processing frames and sound together. This paper asks if the model needs to hear the audio at all. The researchers built SleuthVLM, a setup around a vision-language model (an AI that reads images and text). Speech is first turned into text, and the model then investigates the video on its own, with no fixed workflow. On four benchmarks with videos up to 90 minutes, it scored 75.0% on average, 2.6 points above Gemini 3.8 Flash. It also topped the Video-MME-v2 leaderboard at 77.8%. With an open-source 27B model, it beat all open-source omni models. The authors conclude that hearing is not the bottleneck. Better agent skills, meaning the ability to search and reason on its own, matter more than extra audio training data.
