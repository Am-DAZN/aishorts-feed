KernelMosaic dataset teaches small AI model to write fast GPU code

Researchers released KernelMosaic, an open dataset for training AI models to write CUDA kernels. These are small programs that run on Nvidia graphics chips. The dataset pairs many different tasks with checked solutions and the step-by-step work that led to them. A strong AI model, guided by a framework that tests and fixes its code, produced those solutions. The team then fine-tuned a small open-source model on the data. Its correct answers on the KernelBench tests rose from 4%, 0% and 0% to 88%, 82% and 16% across three difficulty levels. It also wrote kernels faster than standard PyTorch for more tasks at every level. Training on the step-by-step work beat training on final answers alone. It also led the model to test and revise its code more.
