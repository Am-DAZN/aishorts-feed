New Training Method Makes Vision Models Easier for AI to Edit

Researchers studied whether large language models (LLMs) can directly change the internal settings, called weights, of image-recognition networks. They tested five LLMs on tasks like fixing wrong labels and correcting errors, using four image datasets: Waterbirds, CIFAR-100, PACS and Entity-30. The team ran more than 2,600 edits and scored each on success, side effects and lost accuracy. They also built Edit-Ready Training (ERT), which prepares a network by practicing the kind of direct edits an LLM makes. ERT sharply improved edit success where standard networks were hard to edit. It also gave the highest average score for GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5. The team plans to release its testing framework, tasks, edit records and scores.
