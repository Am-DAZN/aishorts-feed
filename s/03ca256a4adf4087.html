Gemini app now runs on Android phones with less memory

Google says its Gemini app is now available on lower-memory Android devices. Gemini is Google's AI assistant, which people can use to ask questions and get help with tasks. Until now, phones with less memory may not have been able to run it. The announcement was posted on Google's AI blog. The source gives no further details, such as which phones are supported or when the rollout happens. It matters because many people use budget phones, and this change could let more of them try the assistant.
