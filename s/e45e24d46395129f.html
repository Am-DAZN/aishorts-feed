LiveFoley generates matching sound for live video in real time

Researchers have built LiveFoley, a system that creates sound effects for video as it plays. This is called streaming video-to-audio generation, and it matters for games and interactive worlds. Until now, streaming tools lagged behind offline ones. They matched sound to picture less well, and the audio quality was lower. LiveFoley tackles both problems. A sliding window lets existing video encoders work on a live stream without retraining, and the delay stays below what people can notice. For audio, a version called History LocDiT uses past sound as context to improve quality at no extra delay. The authors say LiveFoley matches offline models on timing and beats other streaming systems on audio quality.
