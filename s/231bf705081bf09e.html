DemoFoundry Uses Coding Agents to Make Realistic Robot Training Videos

Robots learn best from demonstrations that look real and come with exact action labels. Simulations look fake, and AI-generated video often breaks the laws of physics. DemoFoundry combines both. A coding agent reads a scene and a task, rebuilds the key shapes, and writes a control program. A physics simulator runs it and records the robot's moves. A video model then turns that rough footage into photorealistic video of the same scene. Automatic checks keep only successful runs. No human demonstrations of the target task are needed. In tests, robot policies trained on this data beat ones trained on raw simulation and came close to those trained on the same number of real demonstrations.
