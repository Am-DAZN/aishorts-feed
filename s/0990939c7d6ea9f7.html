Holiday Extras uses OpenAI's Codex to improve its apps and bookings

Holiday Extras, a travel services company, is using Codex, an AI tool from OpenAI, to build software. According to OpenAI's blog, the company is turning what it knows about its customers into better apps. It says this has led to higher booking conversion, meaning more visitors go on to complete a purchase. The company also sees new chances to grow. The source gives few details, so it does not say how large the gains are or exactly how the tool is used. Still, the story shows how a business outside the tech industry is using AI coding help to change what it can build.
