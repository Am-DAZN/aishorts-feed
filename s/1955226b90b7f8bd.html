New benchmark tests how well AI turns movies into games

Researchers introduced Movie2Game-Bench, a test for how well AI models adapt films into games. It goes beyond asking whether a game simply works. It checks if the game keeps the film's content and turns it into player actions, rules, and feedback. The test covers 22 films and 658 reference items taken from them. Three AI players also play each game. The team tested 14 models. Each did well in different areas, such as game quality, adaptation, or player experience. All 14 struggled most with playable sequences from the films, scoring 37.9 out of 100 on average. Iconic elements scored 54.8. The authors say future work should keep film moments that suit gameplay and link them to player actions and consequences.
