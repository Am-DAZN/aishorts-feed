JioStar's chief architect shares lessons on AI discovery for 500M users

OpenAI's blog features Vijay Seshadri, chief architect at JioStar, who explains how his team uses AI to help people find content at a scale of 500 million users. He shares lessons on three areas: evals, guardrails, and taking AI into production. Evals are tests that check how well an AI system works. Guardrails are rules that keep it from going off track. The source gives few other details, but the piece offers a look at what it takes to run AI for a very large audience.
