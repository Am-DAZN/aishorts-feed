Kuken Industries Uses OpenAI to Search 70 Years of Drawings

Kuken Industries, a maker of cooling towers, now uses the OpenAI API to search its archive of drawings that goes back 70 years. An API is a tool that lets a company plug an AI model into its own software. Engineers use it to find the drawing they need for a repair. The search now takes about 4.5 minutes. It used to take nearly an hour. This matters because repair crews can get the right old plans much faster, and the company can put decades of stored records to better use.
