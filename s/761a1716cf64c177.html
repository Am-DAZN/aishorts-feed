Energy firms adopt NVIDIA DSX to tackle AI's power bottleneck

Power is now the biggest bottleneck for building AI infrastructure. NVIDIA says over $300 billion in equipment is backlogged, and more than 200 gigawatts of projects wait in U.S. grid connection queues. NVIDIA's DSX platform tries to fix this by treating the power grid and the AI factory as one system. Engineers can simulate both before building, and AI factories can adjust their demand to grid conditions. GE Vernova, Hitachi Energy, Schneider Electric and Siemens Energy are building on it. Phaidra says its tools give about 10% more compute in the same power limit. Emerald AI says its approach could unlock up to 100 gigawatts on the existing U.S. grid.
