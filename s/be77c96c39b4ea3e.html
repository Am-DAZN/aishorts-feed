Google Adds Brain Science Lesson to Its AI Quests Series

Google has released a new lesson in its AI Quests series. It is called "Polar Peaks" and focuses on neuroscience, the study of the brain and nervous system. The series is meant to teach AI literacy, which means understanding the basics of what AI is and how it works. The new lesson ties that learning to the topic of mapping the human brain. The source gives few other details, such as the age group or how long the lesson takes. Still, it shows Google using real science topics to help people learn about AI.
