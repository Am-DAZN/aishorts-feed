Simon Willison Has an AI Turn Yonkers Into a Stomping Mech

Simon Willison wasn't sure where Yonkers was, so he looked it up on Google Maps. To help the city's location and shape stick in his memory, he tried a playful test. He asked Claude Fable 5.2 Medium to build a three.js animation, a 3D graphics tool for web pages. In it, Yonkers stands up as a big mech, a giant walking robot, and stomps around Manhattan, Queens and Brooklyn. He asked it to use OpenStreetMap data for the maps. His prompt had a phone-keyboard typo, "átomos" instead of "stomps." The model correctly worked out what he meant. The post shows how the model handles 3D modeling and animation tasks.
