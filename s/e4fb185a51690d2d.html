AI Explained reviews Fable 5.2 and Gemini Carbon, and how fast AI improves itself

A YouTube channel called AI Explained has published a long video on two new AI models: Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 and Google's Gemini Carbon. The host says he read the 264-page Fable 5.2 report and a 30-page report from METR, a group that studies AI risk. He also tested both models hundreds of times. The video covers how good the models are, where they still lack common sense, and how quickly AI is helping to speed up AI research. It also looks at lesser-known benchmarks, which are tests used to score AI, and a claim that the model knows when it is being watched. The description gives no scores or results, so the findings are in the video itself.
