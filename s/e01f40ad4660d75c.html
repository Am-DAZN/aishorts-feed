torchtsetlin brings fast, readable Tsetlin machines to PyTorch

Tsetlin machines learn simple logic rules, so people can read how they decide. They learn through rewards and penalties instead of the usual gradient method. Until now, their software ran as separate C and CUDA code outside the main deep learning tools. A new open-source package, torchtsetlin, builds the whole Tsetlin machine family as regular PyTorch modules. It covers rule models for classification, regression, sequences, images, volumes and per-pixel segmentation. On ten benchmarks it matched the accuracy of existing libraries. On all but the two smallest, it trained 2.4 to 10.9 times faster than PyTsetlinMachineCUDA and 10.3 to 81.2 times faster than tmu on the same GPU. The package is pure Python, MIT-licensed and on PyPI.
