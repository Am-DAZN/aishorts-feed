UniSpark Mixes Two Attention Types to Both Understand and Create Images

Researchers present UniSpark-9B, a single AI model that can both understand images and generate them. It answers questions about pictures, makes images from text, edits images from instructions, and produces mixed text-and-image outputs. The model blends two ways of reading data. Full attention lets every piece of data look at every other piece. Linear attention keeps a compact memory of earlier context. It was trained on 159.7M image-generation samples, 213.4M understanding samples, and 624.8K mixed sequences, in four stages. It can make images up to 2K resolution. It scores 2470.3 on MME-S and 0.83 on GenEval, two common benchmarks. The authors say this suggests mixing attention types is an effective base for models that do both tasks.
