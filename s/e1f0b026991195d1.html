K2-Horizon: Six Fully Open AI Models, With Data and Code Included

A research team has released K2-Horizon, a set of six open language models. They range from a small 0.9 billion parameter model to a large 375 billion parameter one. The models are built for reasoning, coding, research and office tasks, and they lead or compete with similar-sized open models on coding and agent tests. The team also shares two new techniques. One changes how the model's attention works, and the other speeds up text generation by up to 2.2 times. What sets the release apart is its openness. It includes training data, checkpoints from every stage, logs, and all the code. Around 10 trillion training tokens were synthetic, meaning AI-made. This shows such data can play a big role in training large models.
