Big Forecasting Models Fall Short as Time Series Classifiers

Some AI models are trained to predict future values in time series data, like sensor readings. People now reuse them, without any changes, to sort time series into categories. This study tested two such models, Reverso and Chronos-Bolt, on six large datasets from the MONSTER benchmark. Training helped: the models beat versions with random settings on five of six datasets, by up to 23 percentage points. But they did not reliably beat the strongest classifier that needed no pretraining. They did worse in 15 of 17 settings and tied in two. Putting back size information of the signals closed part of the gap. Fine-tuning and forecast-error features did not. The authors say researchers should always compare against strong task-specific baselines and random-weight controls.
