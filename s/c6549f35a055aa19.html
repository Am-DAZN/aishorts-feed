Anthropic's IPO filing warns its own AI could pose existential risks

Anthropic has filed a 335-page prospectus for its stock market debut. About 100 pages cover risks. The company says its technology could harm society, upset global politics and, if things go wrong, pose catastrophic or existential risks to humanity. It asks buyers to see the stock as a chance to be part of something bigger. The article argues Anthropic is skilled at telling a dramatic story. Cofounder Jack Clark told an IMF meeting that the singularity, the point where tech outpaces its makers, has already begun. Cofounder Chris Olah told the Vatican the company finds unsettling signs in its models. The filing even lists questions about whether its AI has feelings as a business risk. The author says the company may be right, but notes many researchers doubt such claims.
