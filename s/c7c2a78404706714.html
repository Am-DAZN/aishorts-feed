New Method Uses Language Models to Give AI Agents Better Step-by-Step Rewards

Teaching an AI agent to finish long tasks is hard when it only gets a reward at the very end. It is tough to tell which earlier steps helped. Researchers propose a method called Skill-LR to fix this. A vision-language model, which understands images and text, works out which subgoal the agent is pursuing at each moment. A large language model then writes a matching reward for that stage. This gives the agent feedback that follows its plan. In tests on multi-stage MiniGrid and BabyAI tasks, such as DoorKey and Unlock-to-Unlock, Skill-LR learned faster and more reliably than sparse rewards and methods like RUDDER and LaRe. The authors say it offers a clearer way to assign credit in long tasks.
