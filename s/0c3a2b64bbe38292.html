New AI Spots Mistakes in Step-by-Step Tasks as They Happen

Researchers studied how an AI assistant could catch mistakes while someone does a task, such as cooking, so there is still time to fix them. The AI watches first-person video and reads the task instructions. It finds each finished step, marks when it happened, and labels it as correct, modified, a slip, a correction or an addition. Steps that were skipped are found after the video ends. The team compared two designs: one with separate parts for each job, and one single model that does everything at once. Both were equally good at splitting video into steps. The single model was better at spotting mistakes, scoring 84.1% against 80.7%, and did much better on slips. Errors passing from one stage to the next likely hurt the separate design. Both beat two earlier systems, AEM and ESTANet, on test videos with mistakes.
