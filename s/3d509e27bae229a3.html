OpenAI names Jill Hazelbaker as Chief Corporate Affairs Officer

OpenAI has announced that Jill Hazelbaker will join the company as its Chief Corporate Affairs Officer. In this role, she will lead OpenAI's global communications and corporate positioning. In simple terms, she will be in charge of how the company talks to the public and how it presents itself around the world. The announcement came from OpenAI's blog. The post gives no other details, such as a start date.
