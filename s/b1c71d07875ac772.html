OpenAI announces new tools and partnerships to fight online child abuse

OpenAI has shared a new effort to help stop online child sexual exploitation and abuse. The company says it will offer new tools, practical guidance, and partnerships. These are meant to help prevent abuse, detect it, and respond when it happens. The announcement came in a short blog post. It gives few details on how the tools work or who the partners are. The focus on prevention, detection, and response shows OpenAI wants to address the problem at every stage as AI becomes more widely used.
