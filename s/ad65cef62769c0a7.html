New test shows AI models struggle to read charts in non-English languages

Researchers built MInfoVisQA, a test of how well AI models that handle images and text understand charts and infographics in different languages. It has 8,960 question setups drawn from 128 original examples across 24 languages. The team kept the numbers and layout the same and only swapped the translated labels, so results can be compared fairly. They tested 25 models. Two models had the same average accuracy of 80.4%, yet their results varied by 7.8 and 20.3 percentage points across languages. When the questions stayed in English but the chart text changed language, all 25 models scored lower on non-English charts. The authors say average scores can hide weak spots, so we need measures that check how well models hold up across languages.
