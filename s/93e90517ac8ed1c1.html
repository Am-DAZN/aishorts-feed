New Method Stops AI Video Worlds From Letting Objects Pass Through Walls

Interactive video world models are AI systems that generate video in response to actions, like moving a character. Researchers found they often let objects pass through obstacles. They built NoPeBench, a test for this problem, and found no open-source model reliably avoided it. They also found the models already hold clues about these errors in their internal data. Using that, they built NoPeWorld. It trains a small add-on to judge if an action is safe, then swaps unsafe actions for safe ones that stay close to the original intent. Across six models, the rate of physical violations fell from 74% to 16%. It also worked without extra training on open-domain images, third-person views, and moving obstacles. The team released the code, benchmark, and model weights.
