PhyRubric Tests Whether AI Videos Obey Basic Physics

Text-to-video models turn written prompts into video, and some people see them as simulators of the real world. But do their videos follow basic physics? Current tests use a single gut-feeling score from a human rater. That score is subjective and does not say which physical rule was broken. PhyRubric takes a different approach. Before any video is seen, it builds a checklist from the prompt, split into what should appear and what physics should hold. Judges then mark each item, citing timestamps in the video. The team built a benchmark with 34 categories across five physical areas, plus 3,788 training videos. They used these to fine-tune an open-source judge model. Tests on ten video models show physical common sense is far from solved. The judge tracks human ratings better than other open-source judges and matches a top proprietary one.
