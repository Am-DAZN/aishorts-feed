Phantom Blade Zero and World of Warcraft: Forever Head to GeForce NOW

NVIDIA's weekly GFN Thursday post says the martial arts action game Phantom Blade Zero is now on GeForce NOW, its cloud gaming service. Games run on NVIDIA's servers, so players need no big downloads. World of Warcraft: Forever follows on Nov. 4 at 3 p.m. PT, and new players who don't already subscribe can claim a launch-week access reward while supplies last. Mod support from Mod.io is also coming to Cyberpunk 2077 and The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered. Nine more games join the library this week. In India, four-month Performance and Ultimate memberships become available tomorrow. The update gives cloud players more games to choose from.
