Entangle lets robots learn new tasks by watching one human video

Researchers built Entangle, a model that lets a robot copy a task after watching a single human video. It needs no extra training for each new task. The model takes the video and the robot's current view, then predicts what will happen next and which actions to take. A big problem was a lack of matched human and robot footage. The team turned recorded robot videos into human-style videos using video generation, and added some real paired recordings. They also trained the model to tell the correct action path from other tries at the same task, so it can't just guess the task. On an Astribot S1 robot, it scored 83.5 out of 100 across six unseen tasks. On seven unseen RoboTwin tasks, it hit a 69.5% success rate, 22.6 points above Zero-WAM. It also handled rearranged layouts and swapped tools.
