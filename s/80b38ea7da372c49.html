20,000-plus veterans claim free year of ChatGPT Plus

OpenAI says more than 20,000 veterans and service members who are leaving the military have signed up for a free year of ChatGPT Plus, its paid chatbot plan. The offer is aimed at people moving into civilian life. In a blog post, OpenAI shares how some of them use the tool to prepare for civilian careers. The post does not give further numbers here, but the sign-ups show strong interest from this group in using AI tools as they look for new jobs.
