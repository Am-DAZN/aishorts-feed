Legal tech firm Legora builds AI work system for lawyers on OpenAI tools

Legora is a legal technology company that is building what it calls an agentic operating system for legal work. Agentic means the software can carry out tasks on its own, step by step. According to OpenAI's blog, Legora uses OpenAI models, ChatGPT, and Codex, OpenAI's coding tool, to speed up legal workflows. It also uses them to expand contract analysis, which means reviewing agreements for key terms and issues. The goal is to take routine tasks off lawyers' plates so they can spend more time on higher-value work. The post is a customer story showing how a law-focused company applies OpenAI's technology in a professional field.
