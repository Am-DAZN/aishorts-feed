OpenAI to honor robots.txt signal for AI training preferences

OpenAI says it will respect a shared signal placed in a website's robots.txt file. This is a small text file that tells automated programs what they may or may not do on a site. With the new signal, website owners can say whether they want their content used to train AI. The signal is meant to work across participating AI companies, so owners do not have to set rules for each one separately. It matters because many publishers want more say over how their content is used to build AI systems. The source gives no further details on how the signal works or which other providers are taking part.
