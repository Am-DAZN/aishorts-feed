TopicScope tracks topic of every word an AI writes, live

Researchers built TopicScope, a tool that labels the topic of each token (a word or word piece) as an AI model writes its answer. The label can change midway if the subject shifts. It uses sparse autoencoders, which break a model's inner activity into simpler, more readable features. Earlier live monitors mostly spotted harmful content and handled only two or three classes. This one covers seven topics. The team trained small classifiers on the model's reading of a prompt, then used them while it generated text. This worked on Gemma-3 1B and 4B. The classifiers lost at most 5 points of accuracy when off-topic asides were added, topics removed or sections reordered. Without the autoencoder features, similar accuracy needed classifiers several times larger. The authors say cheap topic monitors are possible, and that these tools help beyond safety checks. Code, data and models are public.
