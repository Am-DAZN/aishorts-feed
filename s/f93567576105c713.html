OpenAI unveils plan for responsible AI data center growth

OpenAI has introduced two documents about how it builds the large computing sites that power AI. They are called the National Community Compact and the Compute Campus Policy Blueprint. According to OpenAI, the goals are to protect local resources, create jobs, and guide responsible development of AI infrastructure. The source gives only a short description, so details of the commitments are not included here. The announcement matters because AI needs huge amounts of computing power, and the sites that provide it can affect the communities nearby.
