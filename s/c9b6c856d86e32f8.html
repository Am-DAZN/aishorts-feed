New Method Looks Inside AI Models to See How They Solve Tests

Most AI tests only give a score, which shows how well a model did but not how it got there. Researchers have proposed a new framework that looks at what happens inside a language model while it works on a benchmark, which is a standard test. It combines signals from the model's attention and activations with measures of its internal representations. This lets tests be placed on a scale from memory-based to reasoning-based, and it can expose failures that a single score hides. The team tested 16 models on 24 benchmarks. They found clear differences in how models process knowledge-focused tasks versus reasoning-focused ones. The authors say this could help people pick better benchmarks and choose models more wisely. The code is open source.
