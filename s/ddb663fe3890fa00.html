ReWeave Helps Robots Learn Long Tasks Without Retraining Their Core Models

Researchers introduced ReWeave, a method for improving robots on long, multi-step tasks without changing the robot's trained policy weights. Instead, it tunes the setup around the robot, such as context, workflows and control loops. It also builds a map of which physical conditions must hold before each step. As the robot tries tasks, the map is updated and old attempts are re-read against it. This shows which progress was kept and where things went wrong. On the LIBERO-PRO benchmark, held-out success rose from 89.00% to 98.75%. On RoboDojo, it rose from 25.50% to 58.25%. On eight unseen LIBERO-PRO tasks, the shared setup reached 51.3% success versus 41.3% for direct distillation, and 75.0% after continual learning. This matters because it offers a way to reuse lessons across tasks.
