Lyris turns one photo into a three-minute 3D walkthrough video

Researchers built Lyris, a system that makes long, camera-controlled walkthrough videos from as little as a single photo. Past methods remember what they have already shown, but drift off course once they go beyond it. Others use hand-made layouts to place objects, which takes human effort. Lyris combines two anchors. A point-cloud memory, a 3D map of dots, holds areas already seen or generated. For new areas, a vision-language model, an AI that reads images and text, drafts a simple layout with one labeled 3D box per object. Users can edit these boxes. The team shows walkthroughs over three minutes and 50 meters long, including going through doorways into unseen rooms. On minute-long round trips across four datasets, it cut camera error by 3.8 times and improved consistency on return by 22% on average.
