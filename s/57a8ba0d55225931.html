NVIDIA Backs Korean AI Startups, Plans More Investments

NVIDIA and Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT held a forum in Seoul with over 20 Korean AI startups. NVIDIA announced an investment in Point2, which makes radio-based links to move data between chips in AI data centers. It also plans to invest in Motif Technologies, RLWRLD and Upstage. Motif builds AI models, RLWRLD builds software that helps robots handle delicate tasks with fingers, and Upstage makes language models and tools that read business documents. NVIDIA says the group covers the full AI stack, from hardware to models to robots. The plans build on its Inception program, which has more than 1,000 Korea-based members.
