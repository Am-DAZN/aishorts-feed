New Japanese Finance Test Aims to Stay Ahead of AI Cheating

Researchers built JFinBench, a test of how well AI language models handle Japanese finance. It has 321 questions from the 2026 editions of six real certification exams, covering accounting, tax, financial planning and banking. A coding agent helps convert each exam, and a human checks every question. This makes it easier to refresh the test each year, so models cannot simply memorize old questions. Of 12 models tested, Claude Fable 5 and GPT-6 Astra scored above 93%. The best open-weight model, Kimi K3.1 Pro, trailed Claude Fable 5 by 12 points. Giving models a terminal and web access helped all of them, but about 4 points of gap remained. The team has released the data, evaluation code and building pipeline.
