Phonon TTS launches as on-device speech model aiming for top benchmark scores

Hugging Face's blog has published a post titled "A Closer Look at Phonon TTS." The title says Phonon is a text-to-speech system, meaning software that reads written text aloud in a human-like voice. It is described as high quality and able to run on a device, such as a phone or laptop, instead of in the cloud. The title also says it tops the benchmarks, which are standard tests used to compare speech systems. No further details were provided, so the creators, the exact scores, and the technical approach are not known from the source.
