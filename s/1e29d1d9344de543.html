Researchers Propose Roadmap for 'Agentic Robotics' Built on Foundation Models

A new paper says robotics is entering a new phase called agentic robotics. Instead of training a separate control program for each type of task, one large AI model handles open-ended jobs. It watches its surroundings, works out what the user wants, plans, uses tools and skills, checks the results, and changes course. The authors say frontier models with this setup quickly matched or beat task-specific programs on many robot benchmarks. But the field still lacks a shared definition, a reliable way to test these systems, and a plan for progress. The paper proposes an evaluation method that measures both the model and the full robot system. It also covers what carries over from digital agents and what challenges remain, such as slow reaction time, mechanical limits, and mistakes that cannot be undone. The goal is robots that work safely and reliably with growing responsibility.
