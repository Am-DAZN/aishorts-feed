Faster AI Makes Full-Waveform LiDAR Work in Real Time

LiDAR is a laser sensor used in self-driving cars and robots, but fog, rain, and clear or dark surfaces can confuse it. Full-waveform LiDAR keeps the whole returning signal, so it catches faint and bounced echoes. AI models called Transformers work well on this data, but they are slow and need lots of labeled examples. Researchers propose two fixes. FWL-ToPM drops or merges unneeded data pieces in dim background areas. FWLAug creates varied training data while keeping the signal's physical structure. On the Ghost-FWL benchmark, FWL-ToPM ran over 8 times faster at real-time speed and gained nearly 9 points in F1 score, a measure of accuracy, over the prior best. FWLAug also helped on unseen scenes. The code and test dataset are public.
