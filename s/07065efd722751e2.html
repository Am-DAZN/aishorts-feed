LLM 0.37.1 bugfix release arrives with community contributions

Simon Willison has released version 0.37.1 of LLM, his tool for working with large language models. The update is a bugfix release, which means it fixes problems rather than adding new features. The source does not say which bugs were fixed. Four contributors helped with the release: Steven Huang, Tao An, Arjan Mossel and XQ. Small updates like this keep open tools stable for the people who rely on them.
