OpenAI brings outside research groups in to test its AI models

OpenAI has started a program it calls embedded assessments. In it, outside research groups work with the company to independently check its AI models. The first partners are Apollo Research, METR, Transluce, and Goodfire. They will look at three things: whether a model's behavior matches human goals (known as alignment), whether its safeguards work, and how well the systems that monitor it perform. Having independent groups do this work can make safety claims easier to trust, since the checks do not come only from the company that built the models. The source gives few other details about how the program will run.
