Glance lets one AI model both rank passages and write the answer

Many AI systems that look up documents use two models: one to rank the passages and another to write the answer. That means more memory and repeated work. Glance combines them. It adds a small add-on to the answer-writing model so it can score passages itself, reusing work it already did when reading them. One 9B model handles writing, decisions and ranking, with about 0.1 GiB of extra memory per skill. After 39 minutes of training, it beat a standard ranker, bge-reranker-v2-m3, on SciFact and NFCorpus. On 300 HotpotQA questions, its chosen passages gave slightly better answers. It kept improving with up to 200 candidate passages. The authors note these are early results, tested on limited datasets and one model family.
