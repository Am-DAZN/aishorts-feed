AB-Attention Speeds Up AI Video Generation by Skipping Most Attention Work

Video AI models called diffusion transformers make good videos, but they are slow. The main reason is attention, the step where every part of the video is compared with every other part. Existing shortcuts skip some of these comparisons, but quality drops when too much is skipped. Researchers propose AB-Attention, a method that needs no retraining. It picks important blocks of data more carefully, then samples blocks and adjusts their weights so the result stays close to full attention. It also packs small pieces of data together to use the GPU efficiently. On Wan2.1 and HunyuanVideo, it skips 89 to 92 percent of attention. Other methods compute 1.8 to 3.6 times more. It improves image quality scores by up to 4.4 dB and gives up to 2.23 times faster end-to-end generation. The code is public.
