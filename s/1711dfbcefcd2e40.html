Gamer: One AI Model Learns to Play Many Games in Real Time

Researchers introduced Gamer, an AI agent built to handle long tasks in complex games. It sees the screen, remembers what happened, and plans several steps ahead. The team first trained it on tens of thousands of hours of keyboard and mouse play from games and simulated worlds. They then taught it to think only when needed, which cuts wasted computing. Reinforcement learning, where the AI learns from rewards, helped it handle long play sessions. A cloud-and-edge system keeps response time low while running many game environments at once. In several 3D open-world games, Gamer showed solid generalization and efficiency comparable to skilled players. It also scored competitively on 2D game tests. The authors say this points toward AI agents that can act in both digital and physical worlds.
