Smaller AI Agent Learns to Navigate Live iPhone Apps Through Trial and Error

Big AI models can use phone apps well, but they cost a lot to run. Smaller models trained on fixed examples often just memorize them, so they break easily and cannot fix their mistakes. Researchers propose training small agents directly on live iOS apps using reinforcement learning, where the agent learns from feedback. The agent is split into a planner and a model that finds the right spot on the screen. An AI judge scores each step and the final result. The team also built a method called GiGPPO to keep training stable, and a system that runs hundreds of virtual iOS devices at once. On benchmarks, their 8B agents beat the standard fine-tuned baseline. They improved nearly sevenfold on Android tasks they were not trained on. On real iOS apps, they beat Gemini-3.5-Flash and tied with Gemini-3.1-Pro.
