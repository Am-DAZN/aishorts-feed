NVIDIA Releases Ready-Made Deployment Recipes for Four Korean Open AI Models

NVIDIA has released deployment recipes, which are step-by-step setups, for four leading Korean open models. They are A.X K2 from SK Telecom, K-EXAONE 2.0 from LG AI Research, Solar Open 2 from Upstage and Motif-3 from Motif Technologies. Big models often do not fit on one GPU, so they must be split across many GPUs. That needs careful coordination of requests and communication. The recipes use NVIDIA Dynamo, an open source tool for running models across many machines in a data center. They are tuned for NVIDIA B200 GPUs and offer up to 2x performance out of the box. NVIDIA says this helps developers move these models into real services faster.
