New Benchmark Shows AI Struggles to Read Documents in Several Languages

Researchers built MDU, a test for how well AI models that read images and text understand documents. It has 23,146 examples across 13 tasks in five languages: English, French, German, Italian and Spanish. Tasks include pulling out text, summarizing, sorting documents and finding names. The team tested 45 open models plus one commercial system. The best average score was 69.0, and only eight systems reached 65. The two top results came from 27B-size models, not the biggest ones. The gap between the best and worst language did not shrink as models grew. Skills also did not rise together: models good at copying text were often weaker at pulling out meaning. The results show that current document AI still has clear gaps.
