NVIDIA Expands Cosmos With New Tools for Building World Models

NVIDIA has added new training tools, simulation checkpoints and data blueprints to its Cosmos platform. Cosmos helps developers build world models, which are AI systems that understand and simulate the real world for robots and self-driving cars. The Cosmos Data Factory now offers five cloud blueprints for tasks like making synthetic driving scenes, defect images and weather changes in video. Porsche expects to cut repeated test drives by up to 90% using it. ANYbotics made over 1,500 synthetic clips to train inspection robots. At GTC Berlin, NVIDIA also extended Cosmos Framework tools to more open models, including GR00T, Alpamayo, QWEN and Pi0.5. Developers can fine-tune, shrink and deploy them faster.
