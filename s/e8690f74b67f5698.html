NVIDIA Announces Cosmos 3 Open Models for World Simulation

NVIDIA has announced Cosmos 3, a new set of open models for interactive world simulation, according to a post on the Hugging Face blog. World simulation models try to predict how a scene or environment changes over time, which can help in areas like robotics and self-driving systems. The post title says the models are open, meaning developers can likely access them. The source gave no further details, so specifics such as model sizes, abilities and licensing are not known yet.
