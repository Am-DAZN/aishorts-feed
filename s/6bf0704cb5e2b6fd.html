Census Bureau's AI Survey Now Asks About 'Super Intelligence' After Executive Order

Simon Willison reran an old chart of U.S. business AI use, built from the Census Bureau's twice-monthly survey of 200,000 companies. Large firms said yes about 12% of the time in September 2025. After the question was reworded to cover "any business function," about 30% said yes by February 2026 and around 40% by October 2026. Because the wording changed, the old and new numbers can't be compared directly. Then he found a second change. Starting October 19, 2026, Executive Order 14434 made the Bureau rename AI as "Super Intelligence." Willison asks whether the new name will shift how businesses answer.
