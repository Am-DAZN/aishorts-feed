InstructReplay Helps AI Models Learn New Facts Without Forgetting Skills

When you teach a chatbot new facts through fine-tuning (extra training on new data), it often gets worse at other things. Researchers found standard fine-tuning cut scores on instruction-following, reasoning and coding tests by 35 points on average. The models also began giving much shorter answers, at least half as long as before. The team proposes InstructReplay. The original model first answers a set of general instructions. During fine-tuning, the model is then kept close to those saved answers. Tested on three model families and two domains, it cut the average drop on five general benchmarks from 28.1 to 2.4 points. It also kept answer length close to the original, and learned new facts as well as standard fine-tuning. It beat seven other methods in all six settings. The authors say the gain comes from replaying the model's own answers.
