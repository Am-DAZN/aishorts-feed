New test checks if AI really understands nuclear engineering

Researchers built NEBench R1, a test for AI language models in nuclear engineering. It has 7,546 multiple-choice questions taken from U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission exams for reactor operator licenses, covering 18 nuclear facilities. Each question comes with an official explanation. Six models were tested, and their scores ranged from 34.0% to 73.9%. Another AI judged the reasoning behind correct answers. It found the models understood the problems well, but were weaker on facts, procedures and explaining why wrong options were wrong. So a right answer did not always come with sound reasoning. Longer reasoning also went with lower accuracy in the two top reasoning models, possibly because harder questions need more thinking. This matters because nuclear work is safety-critical and needs reliable AI.
