Open-source AI backers rally to bring back Nvidia's Jensen Huang

A petition to reinstate Jensen Huang as Nvidia's CEO has gathered more than 115,000 names, including Elon Musk and some Nvidia researchers. Many signers come from open-source AI, such as Hugging Face, Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia Capital. Open-source models can be downloaded and run without a big tech company. Huang had been a leading voice defending them to policymakers. But new findings from ex-OpenAI researcher Will DePue suggest Chinese AI firms had far more access to Nvidia's top Blackwell chips than thought. They are not independently confirmed, and they do not show Nvidia helped. A new US Commerce Department rule may also hurt open-source progress. Together, these make Huang's return look unlikely.
