OpenAI and Oracle expand partnership to bring Oracle apps into ChatGPT

OpenAI and Oracle are growing their partnership. The goal is to bring Oracle's business applications and AI agents into ChatGPT. An AI agent is a program that can carry out tasks for a user instead of only answering questions. The deal will also help power new Oracle products. For companies that use Oracle software, this could mean working with those tools directly inside ChatGPT. The announcement gives few other details.
