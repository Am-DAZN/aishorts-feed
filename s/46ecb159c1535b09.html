OpenAI Offers Free ChatGPT Workshops for Caregivers in Five U.S. Cities

OpenAI Academy, the company's learning program, is running free, hands-on workshops about ChatGPT for caregivers. The sessions will be held in five U.S. cities. The goal is to help caregivers build practical AI skills they can use in everyday life. Hands-on means attendees will try the tool themselves rather than just listen to a talk. The announcement gives few other details, such as which cities are included or what the workshops will cover. It shows OpenAI trying to reach people who may not work in tech.
