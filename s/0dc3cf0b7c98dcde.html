OpenAI Explores How AI Could Fit Into the Company Org Chart

OpenAI has published a blog post called "The future of the firm." It looks at how AI might become part of the way companies are organized. The short description says AI could show up in three places on the org chart: beneath people, beside them, and between them. The text provided gives only this brief teaser, so it does not explain what each role would look like. Even so, the idea points to a bigger question for workplaces. As AI tools improve, companies may need to rethink who does what and how teams work together.
