SpecWM Speeds Up Video World Models by Fixing Drafts Instead of Redoing Them

Video world models generate interactive scenes frame by frame. Big models look better, while small models run faster. Earlier methods let a small model draft frames, then had a big model redo any draft that a scorer rejected. That scoring adds cost and gives no way to fix a bad draft. SpecWM is a new method that needs no extra training. It checks each draft using the big model's own internal signal, which also gives a correction. Based on two thresholds, a draft is accepted, refined, or rejected. On the WBench Navigation test, SpecWM runs 1.63 times faster than the big model and lifts the NavScore from 75.99 to 85.63. The authors also built LongSpecBench, which shows quality holds up over long, multi-turn runs. Tests on Hy-Nomas show it works across different models.
